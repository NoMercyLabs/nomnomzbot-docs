## Flows

### Change the title, category and tags
1. Open Stream in the sidebar. The Overview tab shows.
2. In "What viewers see", type in Title (the counter shows "x / 140"). Or click one of the lines under "Recent titles".
3. Under Category, type a name in "Search for a category" and click a result. Or click a button under "Recent categories".
4. Under Tags, type a tag in "Add a tag and press Enter" and press Enter. Click the x on a tag to remove it; click Undo in the line "Removed {tag}." to bring it back.
5. Switch on the Content labels that fit your stream, and Paid promotion if a sponsor paid for it.
6. Click "Save changes". The two buttons at the bottom go grey again. Click Discard to drop all edits instead.

### Check you are ready to go live
1. Open Stream. Read the panel "Before you go live".
2. Fix any red line: set a title and a category, get the bot into chat, turn off shield mode if you do not want it on.
3. A grey line is information: no tags, some rewards off, or a check that could not run.

### Connect Kick or YouTube from the stream page
1. In the panel "Where it goes", find the platform with the badge "Not connected".
2. Click Connect. The line asks "Open {platform} to connect?".
3. Click Connect again to confirm. A sign-in window opens (opened by the connect-window helper).

### Plan a stream
1. Open Stream, tab Schedule.
2. Click "Add segment".
3. Fill Start time, "Length in minutes", Title, Category. Switch on "Repeat every week" if it repeats.
4. Click "Save segment". The row appears in the list.
5. Use Cancel on a row to skip one stream. The row gets the badge Canceled and the button becomes Restore.
6. Use Delete, then "Delete segment" in the confirm, to remove it.

### Set a vacation
1. Open Schedule. Switch on "Vacation mode".
2. Pick "Vacation starts" and "Vacation ends" (the end must be after the start).
3. Click "Save vacation". The button hides again when Twitch holds the same window.

### Raid another channel now
1. Open Stream, tab "Raid rules".
2. In "Raid a channel", type the channel name in "Channel name". Click Raid.
3. Check the name in the dialog "Raid {name}?" and click "Start raid".
4. A toast says "The raid to {name} is counting down." Click "Cancel raid" before the countdown ends to stop it.

### Set your raid rules
1. Stay on the tab "Raid rules". Scroll to the panel "Raid rules".
2. Add rules in the groups: Preferred categories, Keywords, Viewer bands, Time since your last raid, Who owes whom a raid. Give each rule points (-1000 to 1000) and a short reason.
3. Click "Save rules". If the server refuses, red lines next to the rule say why. Nothing is stored in that case and the old set stays.

### Run an ad
1. Open Stream, tab Ads.
2. Click one length button (for example "60 seconds").
3. Click "Run ad". The ad starts at once. A line says "The ad started."

### Make a timer
1. Open "Triggers and timers" in the sidebar. The Timers tab shows.
2. Click "New timer" (or "Make the first timer" on an empty list).
3. Type a Name. Type one message per line. Set "Minutes between posts".
4. Click "Create timer". The timer is on at once.
5. Select it in the list to change more: "Chat lines needed", "Post once", "Read it aloud", or switch the Messages field to run a command, pipeline or action.
6. Click "Save changes". Use the Enabled switch to turn it off (this saves at once). Use Duplicate to make a copy that starts off.

### Make a chat trigger
1. Open "Triggers and timers", tab "Chat triggers".
2. Click "New trigger". Type "Words to watch for" and the Reply. Click "Create trigger".
3. Select the trigger. Change "How chat must match", "Match capital letters", "Seconds between replies" and "Who can set it off". Click "Save changes".

### Set up a supporter service
1. Open Settings, then "Supporter sources".
2. Click "Set up" on the service.
3. For a service with a secret: paste the secret into "Secret or token". For Patreon, Shopify or Treatstream: pick a "Connected account" (connect it under Integrations first).
4. Click Save. The toast says "Connection saved."
5. For a webhook service, open Manage again and copy the "Address to give the service" into the service's webhook settings (see Traps for why it is not there on the first open).
