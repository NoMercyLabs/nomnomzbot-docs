## Flows

1. Move between pages (wide window)
   1. Look at the sidebar. The channel name is on top; the account name is at the bottom.
   2. Click a group heading such as "Build". The group unfolds. Click it again to fold it (not possible while the open page is inside it).
   3. Click a page name. The page opens and its group stays open in this browser.
   4. If a page you may not read is missing, you lack its read key. Until the bot answers, all pages show; some then disappear.

2. Move between pages (tablet, 600 to 839 px)
   1. The sidebar is replaced by a narrow icon column.
   2. Hover an icon to read its name in a tooltip. Click it to open the page. The open page is a tinted tile.

3. Move between pages (phone, under 600 px)
   1. Use the bottom bar. It has four pages and "More" when you hold more than five pages; the order is Home, Chat, Stream, Commands first in the streamer sidebar, because they carry the bottom flag.
   2. Tap "More". A drawer opens from the bottom with the remaining pages in their groups.
   3. Tap a page. The drawer closes and the page opens. Tap the "Close" button (cross) to close without choosing.

4. Switch to another channel
   1. Click the channel switcher at the top of the sidebar (or in the top header on narrower windows).
   2. The list shows "Your channel" and "Channels you moderate ({live} live of {total})", live channels first.
   3. Type in "Find a channel" to filter, and to search every channel the bot serves ("Visit a channel") and Twitch channels the bot does not serve ("More on Twitch"). The line says "Searching every channel..." until the answer arrives.
   4. Choose a row. The same page opens for the other channel. If the page belonged to one item (for example one pipeline), the channel's Home opens instead.
   5. A channel you moderate on Twitch where the bot is not set up yet is set up in moderator mode when you choose it (useCurrentChannel.ts:134-139, api/channels.ts:111-127).

5. Turn Shield mode on or off
   1. Look for the "Shield on" or "Shield off" button in the page header (next to the inbox button).
   2. On a wide screen, click it. The change is sent at once.
   3. On a narrow screen (under 768 px), click it. A dialog asks "Turn Shield mode on for {channel}?" with the effect line. Click "Turn on" or "Turn off", or "Cancel".
   4. The button follows the state Twitch reports.

6. Read and clear the "Action required" inbox
   1. Click the inbox button. A badge shows how many items wait.
   2. Read each item. Failed saves say "Not saved: ..." and "On <page name>".
   3. Click "Open" to go to the page that needs you. Click "Dismiss" to clear the item.

7. Preview the dashboard as a smaller role (owner only)
   1. Open the account menu. Choose "View as moderator" or "View as viewer".
   2. A bar says "Viewing as a moderator" (or "a viewer"). The sidebar shows only pages that role can open. The channel switcher shows only the channel name.
   3. Click "Exit" in the bar to return. For a viewer preview that started from a page, you return to that page.

8. Change language, readability, fullscreen, sign out
   1. Open the account menu.
   2. "Readability" opens the Readability settings page. "Language" opens a list: choose a language. "Fullscreen" appears only where the browser allows it. "Sign out" ends the session and opens the login page.

9. Show the keyboard shortcuts
   1. Press `?` while not typing in a field. A dialog "Keyboard shortcuts" lists the keys of the open page.
   2. Press `?` again, or click "Close", to close it.

10. A suspended channel or a channel you cannot manage
   1. A suspended channel replaces the page with "{name} is suspended", the reason, the date and either "Contact us" or the note to ask the people who run the bot. "Sign out" is on the page. If you have another channel, a switcher shows.
   2. An address for a channel you cannot manage shows "No access to {name}". Use the switcher or ask the streamer to make you a moderator.

11. Viewers: find any channel
   1. In the viewer header, click the channel button (it shows the channel in the address, else "Find a channel").
   2. Choose a recent channel, or search and choose one under "Visit a channel" or "More on Twitch".
