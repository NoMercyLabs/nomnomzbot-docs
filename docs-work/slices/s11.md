## Flows

1. See what happened in the channel. Sign in as the Broadcaster. Open Settings > Event journal. The tab "What happened" is open. Read the list newest first (order is set by the bot; not checked). Pick "Event" and/or "Source", set "From date" and "To date". The list returns to page 1. Use "Previous" and "Next". Turn on "Show bot activity" to see the rest. Use "Clear filters" to reset the four filters. Your choices stay on this device.
2. Save a backup of the journal. Event journal > tab "Backup and rebuild" > "Export journal". A line says "Preparing the file. A large journal can take a few minutes; the download starts by itself." with a running clock. A `.jsonl` file downloads. A line says "Saved {count} events to the file."
3. Load a backup. Same tab > "Import journal" > choose a `.jsonl` file. A line says how many lines were read, how many were new, already there, or upgraded. Then run "Replay imported events" and/or "Rebuild pages" so profiles and analytics show them. "Rebuild pages" asks first and warns that numbers are partial until it ends.
4. Copy the old bot's history. Same tab > "Import old history". A line shows the counts and a line for each type that was not recognized.
5. Turn a feature on. Settings > Features. Find the feature in its area (Chat, Automation, More features). Flip its switch. It takes effect at once. A row with a badge ("Higher plan" or a tier name) is locked and its switch is off. Required Twitch permissions show under the name.
6. Share with other bots. Settings > Federation. In "Sharing with other bots", for each of the four kinds, flip "Accept" (take data in) and/or "Share" (send data out). Everything starts off. Below, decide "Moderator mass bans" ("Accept") and "Network blocks" ("Ban here"). Only the Broadcaster can change the last two.
7. Connect a service. Settings > Integrations. Click "Connect" on the row. A sign-in window opens (for Shopify, type the "Shop name" first). After sign-in the row shows "Connected" and "as {account}". "Details" shows permission sets and last sync. If it shows "Sign in again", click "Reconnect".
8. Disconnect a service. Click "Disconnect". A dialog "Disconnect {name}?" lists what stops working with counts. Confirm with "Disconnect". A toast says "{name} is disconnected." Twitch has no Disconnect button.
9. Use your own Spotify app. In the Spotify row click "Add your Spotify app" (or "Use your own Spotify app" / "Edit"). Fill "Client ID" and "Client secret" and click "Save". The secret is stored and never shown again. "Remove my app" (or "Use the shared app" when a shared one exists) clears it.
10. Add a moderator to the team. Settings > Permissions > "Team". In "Person to add" type a name and pick the person. Pick "Role for the new person". Click "Add to team". Change a role later in the row picker. Remove with the trash button. The owner row is locked.
11. Change who may use an action. Permissions > "Actions". Pick an area (or search "Search actions"). Under "Chat and community" or "Channel management" pick the lowest role in the picker on the row. A "Protected" badge means roles under the floor are greyed out. The reset button returns the row to the default. The area list shows "{n} changed".
12. Give one person a dangerous capability. Permissions > "Per person". Pick the person, pick the "Capability", click "Give capability". For "OBS control" and "Bot manager" a dialog lists what they can and cannot do; confirm with "Give it". Remove with the trash button "Take the capability from {name}".
13. Work through viewer reports. Settings > Viewer reports. The list shows open reports. For each, click "Dismiss" or "Escalate" (Lead moderator or above). Switch "Show reports that are" to Escalated or Dismissed to review them.
14. Check limits. Settings > Resource limits. Read the Plan card, the Limits sections and the usage. Bars turn dark at 80 percent and red when full. "Change plan" appears only if the Billing page exists for you.
15. Set the shoutout message. Settings > Shoutouts. Switch "Shout out a known streamer automatically" on if wanted. Type a "Message" (max 500 characters, variables allowed), click "Save". "Use the default" clears it so the built-in message is used.
16. Tune spam defense. Settings > Spam defense. Read each setting's text, change values, click "Save". "Unsaved changes" shows until saved. Out-of-range values turn Save off with a line "Enter a value from {min} to {max}."
17. Tune trust. Settings > Trust policy. Change numbers in the six panels (watch "Total {sum} of 1" on the weights). "Reset to defaults" fills the form with shipped values; click "Save" to keep them. A refused value shows its reason on the row.
