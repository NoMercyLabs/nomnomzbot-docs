## Flows

### Read how the channel did (needs `analytics:read`)
1. Open "Analytics" in the sidebar group "Live". The Overview tab opens with the 30 days choice.
2. Pick "30 days", "90 days" or "12 months" in the "Range" switch. The choice is kept in this browser.
3. Read the six tiles on top ("Streams", "Chat lines", "New follows", "Twitch subs", "Bits cheered", "Peak viewers"). A tile with no data says "No data".
4. Read "Follows" and "Chatters", then the "Highlights" list for average stream length, top category, commands, rewards and song requests.
5. Open the "Streams" tab to see every stream in the range with start, length, category, chat lines, chatters, new follows and peak viewers. The sentence above the table gives the average length and the busiest chat day.
6. Open the "Chat" tab. The grid "When chat is busy" shows the weekday by hour. The panel "Busy days and hours" lists lines per weekday and the 7 busiest hours. The grid notes the time zone ("Times are in {zone}.").
7. If a panel cannot load it says "This could not load." with a "Try again" button. With no data it says "Nobody chatted in this range yet." or "No streams in this range."

### Save a stream recap as a picture
1. Open Analytics, then the "Recaps" tab. The list shows "{count} recaps kept, newest first."
2. Click a column header to sort (Stream, Title, Length, Peak, Chat). Use "Previous" and "Next" past 12 rows.
3. Click "Open" on a row. A dialog shows the recap drawn by the Raid Recap widget, with the pixel size under the title.
4. Click "Save as PNG". The browser downloads `recap-<date>.png` at the full size. If the file cannot be made the dialog says "The PNG could not be made. Try again."
5. Close the dialog with Escape or by clicking outside (the dialog has no close button).

### Turn a reward on or off (needs `reward:manage`)
1. Open "Channel points" in the sidebar group "Loyalty".
2. In the "Rewards" panel flip the switch on the row. The row changes at once. A reward the bot did not create has its switch disabled.

### Edit a reward (needs `reward:manage`)
1. In "Rewards" click "Edit" on the row.
2. Change "Name", "Cost in points", "Description", "Color", the switch "Viewer must type a message", and the "Enabled" and "Paused" switches in the header.
3. Under "Reaction" pick "Simple reply" and type the "Chat reply", then choose where it goes under "Send the reply to"; or pick "Pipeline" and choose what it "Runs".
4. Optionally open "Limits" and set "Limit per stream", "Limit per viewer per stream", "Cooldown in seconds", "Timer in seconds".
5. Click "Save". The dialog closes. On a refusal from Twitch or the bot the dialog stays open and shows the message and field problems.

### Delete a reward
1. Open the reward with "Edit", then click "Delete".
2. A dialog "Delete {title}?" lists what goes with it (counted by the bot) or "No viewer has redeemed this reward."
3. Click "Delete reward" to confirm. The reward is removed here and on Twitch.

### Duplicate a reward
1. Open the reward with "Edit", click "Duplicate".
2. A copy named "<title> copy" is made, with the same settings, and switched off. The dialog closes. If a reward with that name already exists the dialog stays open with the bot's error.

### Work the pending queue
1. In "Pending rewards" choose "All requests" or one reward on the left.
2. Click the check icon ("Complete") on a row to mark it done, or the cross ("Reject") to cancel it.
3. "Reject" asks first ("Reject {count} requests?") and tells you the viewer gets the points back. Click "Reject {count}" to confirm.
4. For many rows, tick boxes (or "Select all requests") and use "Complete {count}" or "Reject {count}" at the bottom. With no ticks they act on every row in the open filter.
5. A failed decision puts the rows back and shows in the error inbox.
