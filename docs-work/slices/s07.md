## Flows

### 1. Make and edit a pick list
1. Open "Pick lists" in the sidebar (needs `picklists:read`).
2. Press "New pick list" (needs `picklists:write`). A dialog "New pick list" opens: "A list a reply can draw one random entry from."
3. Type a Name, an optional Description, and the Entries (one per line).
4. Press "Add pick list". With a problem the dialog stays open and shows one of: "Type a name.", "The name may have at most 100 characters.", "The description may have at most 500 characters.", "A list may hold at most 500 entries.", "An entry may have at most 500 characters." (pickListForm.ts:45-63; en.json:31-37). On success the dialog closes and the list shows a row "N entries · {list.pick.<name>}".
5. Type `{list.pick.<name>}` in a reply to draw one random entry (PickListRow.vue:36 shows the exact text; the template engine itself is outside this slice).
6. To change a list press the pencil ("Edit {name}"), change the fields, press "Save pick list". Renaming breaks replies that use the old name.
7. To delete press the trash. The page asks the server for a counted list of what uses the list, then shows "Delete {name}?" with that list or "No pipeline step uses this list." Press "Delete pick list".

### 2. Make a pipeline and add steps
1. Open "Pipelines". Press "New pipeline" (or "Make the first pipeline" when the list is empty).
2. Type a Name (placeholder "Roast someone"), press "Make pipeline". The workbench opens with "This pipeline has no steps yet." and "Nothing starts this pipeline yet."
3. Press "Add a step at the end" (or press A). The left panel "Steps to add" opens with a search "Find a step". Pick a group item under "Checks and groups" (If, otherwise; Switch on a value; Pick one at random; Repeat; Catch a failure; Run on its own) or an action under its category.
4. The new step appears as a numbered card and opens in "Step details" at once. Fill the fields on the "Settings" tab. A required field left empty shows "Not set up yet: {fields} is empty." on the card.
5. For a check, press "Add a step to {lane}" inside a lane to put steps in it. The palette then says "Adds to {lane} of step {number}"; the cross button "Add at the end instead" cancels that aim.
6. Press "Save" (or Ctrl/Cmd+S). A toast "Pipeline saved." appears. If saving fails the toast says "The bot did not save the pipeline." or the bot's reason.
7. A pipeline does nothing until something starts it. The banner "Starts when ..." lists what starts it (commands, chat triggers, timers or event responses connected to the pipeline). The connection is made on those other pages (not in this slice).
8. Flip the "Enabled" switch in the card header to turn the pipeline on or off. It saves at once.

### 3. Try a pipeline without running it for real
1. With at least one step, press "Test run". The page dry-runs the draft shown right now (not only the saved version).
2. Each card gets a badge: Done, Failed, or Did not run, with output or error text; checks say "This run: {lane}"; repeats say how many rounds ran. The strip shows "Test run 1: 3 steps ran."
3. Press "Input (N)" and add Name and Value rows to give the steps values, then "Run again". Help text: "Nothing is sent, said or changed."
4. Any edit to the steps clears the marks. "Clear test run" clears them by hand.

### 4. Read what a pipeline really did
1. On the workbench press "Runs" in the bar. A side panel opens and the address gets `?view=runs`.
2. Each row shows a status badge (Done / Failed / Took too long / Not allowed), a "Test run" badge for dry runs, and "{ago}, took {length}".
3. Open a row to see "What each step did": "Step 1: <name>", the duration and Done or Failed, and an error line when a step failed. "Show older runs" loads more.

### 5. Leave without losing edits
1. Edit steps, then click any other page. A dialog "Leave without saving?" opens. "Leave without saving" discards the edits; closing the dialog keeps you on the workbench. (A deleted pipeline skips this dialog.)

### 6. Start and finish a poll
1. Open "Polls" in the sidebar.
2. With nothing running, the card "Now" says "Nothing is running. Start something for your viewers." Press "Start a poll".
3. In the dialog fill Question, at least two Choices, Minutes (1 to 30), optionally turn on "Let viewers vote with channel points" and set "Channel points per vote". Press "Start poll". The "Start poll" button stays disabled until the form is valid.
4. The card "Now" shows the poll with a countdown and a bar per choice. Press "End poll" to end early. A finished poll moves to "History".
5. History: use "Show" to filter All / Twitch polls / Predictions / Chat polls. Press "{count} with no votes" to open the folded polls.

### 7. Run a prediction
1. Press "Prediction" in the card "Now". Fill Question (45 characters), Outcomes (2 to 10), "Minutes to predict". Press "Start prediction".
2. While open the card "Now" shows points per outcome and a countdown. Press "Lock predictions" (or open "Details").
3. After it is locked the card shows "Pick the winner". In the dialog choose "{name} wins" and press "Resolve prediction". "Cancel prediction" asks first and says "Every viewer gets their points back."

### 8. Keep quotes
1. Open "Quotes". Press "New quote".
2. Fill Quote (up to 500 characters); optionally "Said by", "Game", "Said at". Press "Add quote". The server assigns the number shown as "#N".
3. Search with "Search the quotes"; press "Random quote" to see one at random; use the pencil and trash on a row to edit or delete (delete needs Mod; add and edit need Vip).

### 9. Add and tune a sound clip
1. Open "Sounds". Press "New sound clip".
2. Choose an Audio file (at most 10 MB), type a Short name (cannot change later), a Display name, and Volume (0 to 100, default 80). Press "Add sound clip".
3. Press the pencil on the row to set Display name, Volume, "Cooldown in seconds", "Who may play it" and "Chat word", then "Save sound clip".
4. Turn the row switch off to stop the clip from playing; delete with the trash after reading the counted consequences.
