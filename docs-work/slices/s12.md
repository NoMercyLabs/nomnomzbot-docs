## Flows
1. Make an outbound webhook.
   1. Open Settings, then "Webhooks" (group Developers). The card "Approved hosts" is at the bottom.
   2. In "Approved hosts", type the host (for example `hooks.example.com`) in the box and press "Add host". The host appears in the list with a switch. If the host is not a plain domain name the page shows a toast "The host could not be added: {reason}".
   3. In "Outbound webhooks", press "New webhook".
   4. Fill "Name", "Host" (host only, no `https://`; must be on the approved list) and optionally "Path".
   5. Tick the events under "Events to send".
   6. Optionally fill "Message body" (empty means the default event body), set "Body is JSON", and add "Custom headers", one `Name: value` per line.
   7. Press "Create webhook". The dialog "Your signing secret" opens. Press "Copy secret", then "I saved it". The secret is never shown again.
   8. The row shows "{count} events" and "Nothing delivered yet". Press the paper-plane icon "Send a test to {name}"; the line under the cards says "Test to {name} delivered. Answer {code} in {ms} ms." or "... failed ...".
2. Read the delivery log and send one again: press the clock icon "Deliveries of {name}". A side panel "Deliveries" lists attempts, newest first, with a status word and the error text. A person who may write sees the arrow icon "Send again" on each row.
3. Replace a leaked signing secret: press the key icon "Make a new signing secret for {name}", confirm "Make a new one". The signing secret dialog opens with the new secret.
4. Turn a webhook the bot switched off back on: the row shows a "Switched off" badge and the reason. Press "Turn back on".
5. Remove an outbound webhook: press the bin icon. The page counts what depends on it first (pipeline steps, deliveries in history, deliveries waiting, custom data sources, supporter connections). The confirm lists the counts and starts with "Deliveries to this address stop at once." If the count cannot be read, no confirm opens. Press "Delete webhook".
6. Make an inbound webhook.
   1. Press "New inbound webhook".
   2. Fill "Name", pick "Provider" and fill "Secret". For "Generic" also fill "Event kind path" and "Event id path". The line "Still needed to create: {fields}." names what is missing.
   3. Open "Advanced" to choose "Pipeline to start", "Event to raise", and for Generic the signature fields.
   4. Press "Create webhook". The row shows "Nothing received yet". Press "Copy link" and give that link to the provider.
7. Replace an inbound link: press the rotate icon "Make a new link for {name}", confirm. The old link is retired at once.
8. Connect the channel's own bot (white-label).
   1. Open Settings, then "White-label bot". The card shows "Shared bot" and the shared bot's name.
   2. Press "Connect your own bot". The panel shows a code and "Open {site}".
   3. Press "Open {site}" (a new tab). Sign in on Twitch as the bot account (use a private window if you are signed in as yourself) and type the code.
   4. The page checks every 5 seconds. When Twitch approves, the badge turns to "Connected" with the bot's name and a toast "Your bot account is connected."
   5. If the code does not work, press "Sign in with the browser" for the Twitch page flow, or "Try again" after an expired or denied code.
9. Go back to the shared bot: press "Disconnect" in the row "Go back to the shared bot", read the two consequences, press "Disconnect". Toast: "Your bot account is disconnected. The shared bot speaks again."
10. Download or erase your data.
    1. Open Settings, then "Your data".
    2. Press "Download my data" to save `nomnomzbot-my-data-<date>.json`. A line shows "Saved {rows} records, {size} bytes."
    3. Under "Your consents" turn a switch off to withdraw that consent. The row stays and can be turned on again.
    4. For "Opt out of extra processing" press "Opt out", then confirm. Nothing is deleted.
    5. For erasure press "Erase my data". The bot counts rows first. The dialog lists the counts. Press "Erase my data" to confirm. The notice says the erasure is requested and shows under "Your requests".
11. Sign in (open build).
    1. Open `/login`. The top row shows which bot you sign in to; "Change" opens the bot chooser.
    2. Press "Continue as broadcaster", "Continue as moderator" or "Continue as viewer". The browser goes to the platform and returns to the page you came from (`return_to`).
    3. To use a code: press "Sign in with a code instead" (and pick the account if more than one platform offers a code), open the shown site, type the code, wait. The page moves on by itself.
    4. To use another bot: press "Change", pick a saved bot or type its address in "Bot address", press "Check", then "Use this bot". For another bot only the code method is offered.
12. Run the setup wizard on a fresh bot.
    1. Open `/setup`. The bar shows the steps and "Finish setup".
    2. Step "Twitch app": follow the numbered instructions, fill "Client ID" (and optionally "Client Secret (optional)"), press "Save"; or press "Use the shared NomNomzBot app". "Next" turns on only after the step is done.
    3. Step "Bot account" (optional): press "Authorize the bot account", approve on Twitch, wait for "Connected: {name}", or press "Skip".
    4. Steps "Spotify", "Discord", "YouTube" (optional): fill the two boxes and press "Save", or "Skip".
    5. "Finish setup" page: read the list ("Done" or "Not set") and the "Event feed" check, press "Finish setup". You land on `/app`, then sign in.
13. Use the viewer pages. Open `/me` to see "My channels"; press "Open" on a channel to see "Your stats in {channel}"; press "Open chat" for the chat page, or the "Me" entry to set pronouns, unlink a sign-in, pick a voice or open "Your data".
