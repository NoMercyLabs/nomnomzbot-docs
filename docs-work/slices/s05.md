## Flows
### Home
1. Open "Home" in the sidebar. The header shows "Offline" or "Live on {platforms}". Live shows uptime, viewers, followers and category. Offline shows "Last stream ended {date} · {length}" when a past stream has an end time.
2. Use the one filled button in the header. Live: "Open chat". Offline: "Edit stream info".
3. Clear the "Review queue": press "Allow" to let a held message through (it leaves the list at once). Press "Deny": the button turns into an undo control and the deny reaches Twitch after the undo time. The row text then reads "Denied".
4. In "Needs attention": press "Open" to go to the page that fixes it, or "Dismiss" to hide the item.
5. In "Connections": a platform that is not connected has the button "Connect" (only with the write key). Press it, answer "Connect {platform} to this channel?" with "Connect", and a sign-in window opens.
6. Snooze an ad: in "Quick actions", row "Next ad break", press "Snooze 5 min". The button is disabled when "No snoozes left".
7. Pause a timer: row "Timers", press "Open timers", then "Pause" on the timer. Press "Resume" to start it. Press "Done" to close.
8. Start a poll: row "Poll or prediction", press "Start a poll". Fill "Question", at least 2 "Choice" boxes, "Minutes". Optionally switch on "Let viewers vote with channel points" and set "Channel points per vote". Press "Start poll". The dialog then shows each choice with votes and percent. Press "End poll" to stop, then "New poll" for another.
9. Start a prediction: press "Start a prediction". Fill "Question", at least 2 "Outcome" boxes, "Minutes to predict". Press "Start prediction". Later press "Lock prediction". After locking, pick the winner in the list and press "Resolve prediction". Press "Cancel prediction" and confirm to refund every viewer.
10. Shout out a chatter: under "Suggested shoutouts · {count}" press "Shoutout" (the row then says "Shoutout sent.") or "Not now" to drop the chatter for this stream.
11. Replay an event: in "Happening now" press the repeat icon on a row. A toast tells how many chat messages, voice lines, overlays and widgets were replayed. Message "Found the event, but there was nothing to show again..." appears when nothing could be replayed.

### Media share
1. Open "Media share". If nothing is set up yet, press "Settings", switch on "Accept media from viewers", and press "Save settings". (Default is off.)
2. Decide on "Waiting for approval": press "Approve" or "Reject" on each item. If "Approve each item before it plays" is off, items skip this group (the code sends an item to "Up next" or "Waiting" by its status; the rule itself is applied by the server and was not read).
3. Order "Up next": use "Play now", the "More actions" menu ("Move up", "Move down"), "Skip", or "Remove" (then confirm).
4. Control the stream: in the card "On stream" press "Play" / "Pause", the skip icon, the stop icon, drag the position slider or the volume slider.
5. When the card says "The Media share browser source is not loaded in OBS...", press "Set up in OBS".

### Music
1. Open "Music". The badge in the header tells about Spotify.
2. In "Now playing" use the big Play/Pause, the previous and next buttons, the seek slider, shuffle, repeat and volume.
3. Open the request queue: press "Close queue" to stop requests or "Open queue" to start them again. The badge shows "Open" or "Closed".
4. Move a song: press "Play next" on a row, or drag the handle to a new place.
5. Remove a song: press the trash icon and confirm "Remove". Ban a song: press the ban icon and confirm "Ban song".
6. Remove all songs of one person: "Pick a requester" then "Remove all", confirm.
7. Add a player button: press "Add button", pick a key, set the icon, label and settings, press "Add button" in the dialog. Edit with a right click, move by drag, "Remove button" in the edit dialog.
8. Change the rules: press "Request rules". Edit values in "Requests" or "Sources". Press "Save" (or "Discard"). The "Requests are open" switch, the "who" selects and the song "Allow" button save at once.
9. Reset: press "Reset to default" and confirm. The dialog lists which rules go back. The requests switch and the banger playlist stay.
