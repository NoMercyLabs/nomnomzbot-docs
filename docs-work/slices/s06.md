## Flows
1. Link OBS to the bot (broadcaster).
   1. Open "OBS" in the sidebar. The header says "OBS is not linked". The connection bar shows a gray dot, "The link to OBS is switched off".
   2. In OBS open Tools, WebSocket Server Settings and note the port and password (dialog text).
   3. Press the filled button "Link OBS". The dialog "OBS connection" opens.
   4. Turn on the switch "Link to OBS". Check "Host" (starts at 127.0.0.1) and "Port" (starts at 4455). Type the "OBS password" if OBS has one. Leave "OBS events to listen to" as it is unless a widget needs the high-volume groups.
   5. Press "Save connection". The dialog closes. The bar shows "Connected" with a green dot and `host:port`. If OBS did not answer it shows "Linked, but OBS did not answer" (orange); a stored failure shows "The link to OBS failed" (red) with the error text.
   6. To change it later press "Edit connection". To keep the stored password leave the password box empty. To delete it press "Remove password" and save.
2. Put the required overlays in OBS (once).
   1. Open the tab "Overlays in OBS". The panel "Required overlays" lists Bot Audio, Alerts, TTS caption, Raid recap, Music Player, Media share and OBS control.
   2. Press the filled "Connect" button on the first row that is on but not open in OBS. A dialog "Set up {name} in OBS" opens with the size and the two boxes to set.
   3. Press "Copy address". The button changes to "Copied".
   4. In OBS add a source of type Browser, paste the address, enter the width and height, and set "Control audio via OBS" and "Shutdown source when not visible" as the dialog says. Press "Close".
   5. When the overlay opens in OBS the dot turns green and the button reads "Configure". The header line moves toward "Everything is loaded".
3. Go live and stop from the dashboard (broadcaster).
   1. On the Live tab find the panel "Outputs". The Stream row says "Off air".
   2. Press "Go live". The row says "Live" and a timer runs.
   3. To end it press the red "Stop stream". The dialog "Stop the stream?" warns that OBS ends the broadcast on every platform and that a recording keeps running. Press "Stop stream" to confirm.
4. Switch scenes and control sources during a stream (editor, or a moderator the broadcaster has allowed).
   1. Live tab, panel "Scenes". Click a scene. It is marked "On air".
   2. Panel "Sources in the scene": press the eye button to show or hide a source, the lock button to lock it, the chevron to open its filters and switch a filter on or off.
   3. Panel "Audio mixer": press the speaker button to mute or unmute an input and drag the slider to change the level (-100 to +26 dB). The write goes out when the drag pauses; a refusal puts the old level back and shows a toast.
   4. A user without the control key sees the same panels locked, with the note "Controlling OBS needs the Editor role or the OBS control permission."
5. Use studio mode and hotkeys.
   1. Open "Studio and tools". The panel "Studio mode" shows only while OBS is linked.
   2. Pick a "Transition". Turn on "Preview before air". Pick a "Preview scene". Optionally type "Duration (seconds)". Press "Transition" to cut the preview to air.
   3. In the panel "Hotkeys" type in "Search the hotkeys" and press a hotkey button. Hotkeys that act on a source OBS does not name are under "Other ({count})".
   4. In "Source screenshot" pick a source, pick png or jpg and press "Capture". The still appears under the controls.
6. Create and set up an overlay.
   1. Open "Overlays". Press "New overlay".
   2. Type a "Name", pick the "Code language" (Vue or Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript), press "Create overlay". The overlay opens beside the list.
   3. Press "Edit code" to write it, or change its fields in the "Settings" panel and press "Save settings". "Open sources reload on save."
   4. Turn the switch "Overlay on". Press "Connect" in the list row (or "Copy browser source address" in the header) and add it to OBS as in flow 2.
   5. In "Reacts to {count} events" press "Test" on an event to send one sample to this overlay. The line under it tells whether it was delivered and whether a browser source had it open.
   6. To remove it press "Delete". The dialog lists what depends on it, then "Delete overlay" confirms. To copy it press "Duplicate".
7. Find a person.
   1. Open "People". Pick a tab (for example "Moderators"). Choose "Last seen" or "A to Z" (remembered).
   2. Click a row. The profile opens beside the list (a full screen with a "Back to the list" link on a phone). Press Esc or the close button to leave it.
   3. To find someone not in the tab type in "Search people". Press Enter to open the first hit, or click a hit. For someone the bot has never seen press "Look up on Twitch".
8. Set the music against bot audio levels.
   1. Open "Overlays", press "Audio levels". The drawer "Music vs Bot audio levels" opens.
   2. Move a slider or type a number for "Master volume", "Voice volume" or "Music duck percent". It saves on release, when you leave the box, or on Enter. A refused value shows a red line under the row and the old number returns.
