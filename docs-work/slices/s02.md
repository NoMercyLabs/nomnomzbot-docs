## Flows

### 1. Read and send in the chat
1. Open "Chat" in the sidebar (needs `chat:read`; a person with only `chat:viewer:read` sees "Viewer chat" instead).
2. The "Chat feed" card shows lines with a "Live" or "Offline" badge on the page header. A count shows as "{n} messages".
3. Click the box with the placeholder "Send a message as you" (or press the compose key `c`) and type.
4. Type `:` plus two letters for emotes and emoji, or `@` plus a letter for chatters. Pick with Up/Down plus Tab or Enter, or click.
5. Press Enter or click "Send". The box clears. If it fails, the text stays and "The message was not sent." shows.
6. Past 400 characters a counter "{n}/500" shows; over 500 it turns red and Send is disabled.

### 2. Reply to a line
1. Hover a line. The tray appears at its top-right corner.
2. Click the Reply button (tooltip "Reply"). A banner "Replying to {name}" shows above the box with the line's text.
3. Type and send. Cancel with the X ("Cancel reply") or Escape.

### 3. Moderate one line
1. Hover a line and use the tray. Timeout opens a menu "Timeout length": 1 min, 10 min, 1 hour, 1 day. A toast "{name} is timed out" shows.
2. "Delete message" removes the line at once with a toast "Message deleted".
3. "Ban" opens "Ban {name}?" with "The ban applies to this channel only." Click "Ban" to confirm.
4. The line is struck through and shows "Timed out · 10 min" or "Banned".
5. No Timeout or Ban button shows for yourself, the broadcaster, or a moderator (a moderator can be sanctioned only by the channel owner).

### 4. Look at one chatter
1. Click a line (not a name or button). Their lines light up and the rest dim. The "Viewer" panel opens.
2. Read the profile header, the currency tile, and "Latest messages here".
3. Click "Timeout", pick a length, or click "Ban" then "Ban" again. The foot shows the result and an "Undo" or "Unban" button.
4. End focus with the "Clear focus on {name}" button, Escape, or by closing the panel.

### 5. Search the history
1. Type at least two characters into "Search chat".
2. The feed becomes "Search results" with "{n} messages match ..."; the send box is replaced by "Search further back" while older results exist.
3. Click "Search further back" for older matches. At the end it says "Searched back to the first message."
4. Clear the field to return to the live feed and the send box.

### 6. Change how the feed looks
1. Click "Display".
2. Use switches and segmented choices in the four sections. Changes apply at once and are kept in this browser.
3. Click "Reset" to go back to the defaults. "Change" under "Font and readability" opens Settings.

### 7. Use chat modes
1. Click "Chat settings" (a person without `moderation:chat:settings:read` does not see the section; without the write key the switches are disabled).
2. Flip a switch under "Chat modes". For Slow mode, Followers-only and Non-moderator delay a number box sits beside the switch; change it and leave the field to save.
3. A bad number shows its range text and nothing is saved. A refused save shows a toast and the old value returns.

### 8. Mass ban raid accounts / clear chat
1. In "Chat settings" read "{n} hostile raid accounts assessed and not yet banned."
2. Click "Mass ban..." and confirm "Mass ban". The count refreshes.
3. Under "Clean up" click "Clear chat" and confirm "Clear chat".

### 9. Change your chat color
1. Click "Chat identity".
2. Click a swatch under "Name color", or type a hex under "Custom color in hex" and click "Apply color".
3. If the Twitch permission is missing, click "Grant permission again", enter the shown code on Twitch, and reload the identity.
4. Badges are only listed here. "Choose on Twitch" opens Twitch chat in a new tab, where the badge choice is made.

### 10. Pick emotes and emoji
1. Click the smile button beside the box.
2. Click an item to put it in the box; the picker stays open. Use the rail to jump; click a heading to fold a section.
3. Right-click an emote to star it (Favorites). Right-click an emoji with tones to pick its own tone. Use the tone button beside the search to set one tone for all.

### 11. Pop out or dock the chat
1. Click "Pop out" to open a separate window, or "Copy dock link" and paste the link into an OBS Custom Browser Dock.
2. The popout and dock use the slim header; search and the three panels are reached from it.

### 12. Chat in another Twitch channel
1. Open the address `<channel>/twitch?id=<twitch id>` (the link is made elsewhere; not found in this slice).
2. Read the live lines. If the page shows "Reading this chat needs the Twitch permission user:read:chat.", click "Allow chat reading".
3. Type into "Message {name}" and click "Send". If sending needs permission, use "Grant permission again".
