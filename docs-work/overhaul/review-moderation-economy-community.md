## Learning and lookup coherence

Read as a new streamer, the moderation sequence moves from a real message decision to policy choices, then distinguishes trust/heat from sanctions. The economy sequence explains an earn/spend loop before store and rankings, with a simple free giveaway before weighted or paid entries. Community pages then teach a person's context, the viewer's own choices, live conversation, stream preparation and recorded outcomes.

Read as an experienced moderator, defaults, role splits, precedence, save behavior, bypasses and exceptional paths remain in named local sections and tables. Examples have meaningful expected outcomes: flagging links preserves the message, signed adjustments add/subtract instead of replacing a balance, and repeated giveaway entries charge repeatedly. The final chat additions explain the current thread popup and native feed without disrupting that task-focused path.

Read as a technical or self-hosting user, advanced details remain accessible without turning ordinary configuration into code. Missing viewer purchase/invitation/age-consent surfaces are described candidly rather than filled in with invented controls. Concept links let a reader jump to permissions, pipelines, currency or profiles without a rigid course.
