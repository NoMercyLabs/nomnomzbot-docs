title: Currency and balances
description: Set up the channel currency, add earning rules, change or freeze a viewer's balance and make savings jars.

You set up the channel's own points on the **Economy** page in the sidebar.
Viewers collect this currency in your channel.
New to the dashboard? Start with the [dashboard tour](/reference/getting-started/dashboard-tour).

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Currency** | The name of the currency, the starting balance and the highest balance. |
| **Balances** | What each viewer holds. |
| **Earning rules** | How viewers earn the currency. |
| **Savings jars** | Shared pots viewers fill toward a goal. |
| **Store** | Things viewers buy, on [Store](/reference/economy/store). |
| **Leaderboards** | Rankings, on [Leaderboards and games](/reference/economy/leaderboards-and-games). |

## Who can use this page

A tab shows only to someone who may read it.
A **Moderator**, **Lead moderator**, **Editor** or **Broadcaster** may see and change the **Currency**, **Balances** and **Earning rules** tabs.
Anyone who may open the page may see the **Savings jars** tab, make a jar and invite a channel.
Closing and deleting a jar needs a **Moderator** or higher.

## Set up the currency

1. Open **Economy** in the sidebar and choose the **Currency** tab.
   The **Channel currency** card opens.
2. **Currency name** starts as `points`.
   Keep it, or type your own name, for example `coins`.
3. Turn on **Currency on**.
4. Choose **Save currency**.
   The currency is saved, and the bot can now pay it out.

A new channel has no saved currency.
The card then shows the defaults below, and **Save currency** sets the currency up.

| Label | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Currency on** | When it is off, no viewer earns or spends the currency, and you cannot adjust a balance. | Off |
| **Currency name** | The name of the currency. It is required. | points |
| **Plural name** | The name the bot uses for any amount that is not 1. | points |
| **Starting balance** | The balance a viewer has when their wallet is made. It cannot be negative. | 0 |
| **Highest balance** | The most one viewer can hold. Leave it empty for no limit. It must be at least the starting balance. | No limit |
| **Decimal places** | A number from 0 to 8. Viewers see no change from it. | 0 |
| **Currency icon URL** | A link to an image. Viewers see no change from it. | Empty |

With an empty name, the card shows "Give the currency a name."

## Switch on an earning rule

An earning rule says what viewers do to earn, and how much they get.
The bot keeps one rule per source.

A new channel already has one rule for each source.
Every rule starts off, so viewers earn nothing until you switch a rule on.

1. Choose the **Earning rules** tab.
   The list shows one row per source, each with an **Off** badge.
2. To change the amount, choose **Edit rule** on the row.
   The **Edit earning rule** dialog opens.
3. Type the **Amount earned** and choose **Save rule**.
4. Turn on the switch at the end of the row.
   The **Off** badge goes away, and viewers earn from this source.

| **Source** | When it earns | The amount | Start amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Chat messages** | A viewer sends a chat message. | **Amount earned** for each message. | 1 |
| **Follows** | A viewer follows the channel. | **Amount earned** once. | 100 |
| **Twitch subscriptions** | A viewer subscribes on Twitch. | **Amount earned** once. | 200 |
| **Cheers** | A viewer cheers bits on Twitch. | **Amount earned** for each bit. | 1 |
| **Raids** | The streamer who raids you earns. | **Amount earned** for each raider. | 500 |
| **Supporter events** | A viewer's supporter event reaches the bot. | **Amount earned** once. | 250 |
| **Watch time** | Nothing pays out for this source. | None | 10 |
| **Twitch gift subscriptions** | Nothing pays out for this source. | None | 200 |

A muted or shadowbanned chatter earns nothing for chat messages.
The bot pays each event once, even when Twitch sends it twice.

The dialog also has **Seconds per payout**.
It does not change what a viewer earns.

### Add a rule again after you delete it

**New rule** at the top of the page opens the **New earning rule** dialog.
Its **Source** list shows only the sources that have no rule.
While every source has a rule, the list shows "Every source has a rule".

1. Choose **New rule**.
2. Choose a **Source**, and type the **Amount earned**.
3. Choose **Save rule**.
   The rule shows in the list, switched on.

### Switch off or delete a rule

- To stop a rule for a while, turn off its switch.
  The row shows **Off**.
- To delete a rule, choose **Delete rule** on the row, then **Delete rule** in "Delete this rule?".
  Viewers stop earning from this source, and the balances they already have stay.

When the list is empty, the card shows "No earning rules yet. Viewers earn nothing until you add one."

## Change a viewer's balance

The **Balances** tab lists each viewer's wallet.
A viewer gets a wallet when they first earn currency.
Each row shows the balance and how much the viewer earned and spent in total.

1. Type a name in **Search viewers** to find the viewer.
2. Choose **Adjust the balance** on the viewer's row.
   The dialog shows the balance now.
3. Type an **Amount**.
   A positive amount adds, and a negative amount takes away.
4. Type a **Reason**.
5. Choose **Adjust balance**.
   The dialog closes and the row shows the new balance.

The amount is a whole number that is not zero, and the reason is required.
Otherwise the dialog shows "Enter a whole amount that is not zero, and a reason."

| The change would | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Take the balance below 0 | The bot refuses it. |
| Take the balance above **Highest balance** | The bot refuses it. |
| Change a frozen wallet | The bot refuses it. |

### Freeze a wallet

Turn on the switch at the end of a viewer's row.
A screen reader names it "Freeze the wallet of" followed by the viewer's name.
A frozen wallet cannot earn or spend.
Turn the switch off to unfreeze it.

## Make a savings jar

A savings jar is a shared pot that viewers fill toward a goal.

1. Choose the **Savings jars** tab, then **New jar** at the top of the page.
   The **New savings jar** dialog opens.
2. Type a **Jar name**.
3. Fill in the other fields, or leave them empty.
4. Choose **Create jar**.
   The jar shows in the list.
   It starts open.
   An open jar takes currency, and a closed jar does not.

| Label | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Jar name** | The name of the jar. It is required. |
| **About this jar** | A short text about the jar. |
| **Goal** | The amount to save. The row then shows how much of the goal is saved. |
| **Jar icon URL** | A link to an image. |
| **Take-out cap per channel** | The most one channel can take out at one time. Leave it empty for no limit. |

With an empty name, the dialog shows "Give the jar a name."

### Open, close or delete a jar

- Turn off the switch on the jar's row to close it.
  The row shows **Closed**.
- Turn the switch on again to open it.
- To delete a jar, choose **Delete jar** on the row.
  A dialog asks "Delete" followed by the jar's name.
  Choose **Delete jar** in it, and the jar and its balance are removed.

Only the channel that made a jar may change, close or delete it.

Under the jar list, the **Deposits, withdrawals and invites** card shows "Not available here yet."
You cannot put currency in, take it out or accept an invite on this page.

### Invite a channel to a jar

You can share a jar with another channel that uses the bot.
The other channel has to accept before it can use the jar.

1. Ask the other streamer for their **Channel ID**.
   It is the channel's ID in NomNomzBot, a long code of letters, digits and dashes.
   It is not the Twitch name.
2. Choose **Invite a channel** on the jar's row.
3. Type the code in **Channel ID**.
4. Choose a **Role**, and fill in the caps, or leave them empty (see the table below).
5. Choose **Send invite**.
   The dialog closes.

| Label | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Role** | **Partner** or **Viewer**. Both roles may use the jar the same way. | **Partner** |
| **Put-in cap per stream** | The most the other channel can put in during one stream. | No limit |
| **Take-out cap** | The most the other channel can take out at one time. | No limit |

The bot refuses a second invite to a channel that is already invited.
