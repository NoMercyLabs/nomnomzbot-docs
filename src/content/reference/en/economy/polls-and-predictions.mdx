title: Polls and predictions

You run Twitch polls and predictions on the **Polls** page in the sidebar.
The page title is **Polls and predictions**.
The top panel, **Now**, shows what runs at this moment, and the **History** panel below it shows what ran before.

## Who can use this

A moderator, a lead moderator, an editor or the broadcaster may open the page, start and end a poll, and start, lock, resolve or cancel a prediction.
Viewers vote and predict on Twitch, not on the dashboard.

## Start a poll

1. Open **Polls** in the sidebar.
   With nothing running, the **Now** panel shows "Nothing is running. Start something for your viewers."
2. Choose **Start a poll**.
   The **Start a poll** dialog opens with the text "Viewers vote on Twitch. The result shows here."
3. Type the **Question** and fill in the **Choices**.
4. Set **Minutes**, and turn on **Let viewers vote with channel points** if you want that.
5. Choose **Start poll**.
   The poll starts on Twitch and shows in the **Now** panel with a countdown and one bar for each choice.

| Label | Choices | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Question** | Any text. It cannot be empty. | Empty |
| **Choices** | 2 to 5 choices. Use **Add choice** for more, and the remove button to take one away. | 2 empty choices |
| **Minutes** | A whole number from 1 to 30 | 2 |
| **Let viewers vote with channel points** | On or off | Off |
| **Channel points per vote** | A whole number of 1 or more. It counts only when the switch above is on. | 10 |

**Start poll** stays off until the question has text, at least 2 choices have text and the minutes are valid.
An empty choice is left out of the poll.
When fewer than 2 choices have text, the dialog shows "Give at least 2 choices."

## End a poll early

1. In the **Now** panel, choose **End poll**.
   The poll ends on Twitch and moves to the **History** panel with the status **Ended early**.

A poll that runs until its minutes are over ends by itself.

## Run a prediction

A prediction has three steps: viewers put channel points on an outcome, you lock it, then you pick the winning outcome.

1. Open **Polls** in the sidebar and choose **Prediction**.
   The **Start a prediction** dialog opens with the text "Viewers put channel points on an outcome on Twitch."
2. Type the **Question** and fill in the **Outcomes**.
3. Set **Minutes to predict**.
4. Choose **Start prediction**.
   The prediction starts on Twitch and shows in the **Now** panel with a countdown.
   Each bar shows the points, the viewers and the share of all points on that outcome.
5. Choose **Lock predictions** when viewers may no longer put points in.
   The **Now** panel shows **Locked** instead of the countdown.
6. Choose **Pick the winner**.
   The dialog shows "Voting is closed. Pick the winning outcome to pay out."
7. Pick the winning outcome and choose **Resolve prediction**.
   Twitch pays out the points, and the prediction moves to the **History** panel.

| Label | Choices | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Question** | Any text. It cannot be empty. | Empty |
| **Outcomes** | 2 to 10 outcomes. Use **Add outcome** for more. | 2 empty outcomes |
| **Minutes to predict** | A whole number from 1 to 30 | 2 |

When fewer than 2 outcomes have text, the dialog shows "Give at least 2 outcomes."
While the prediction is still open, **Details** opens the same dialog, where **Lock prediction** does the same as **Lock predictions**.

## Cancel a prediction

1. Open the running prediction with **Details** or **Pick the winner**.
2. Choose **Cancel prediction**.
   A confirm dialog asks "Cancel this prediction?" and says "Every viewer gets their points back."
3. Choose **Cancel prediction** in the confirm dialog.
   The prediction ends on Twitch with the status **Canceled**.

## Read the history

The **History** panel holds every Twitch poll and prediction the channel ran, and the bot's own chat polls, newest first.
Each card shows the question, the type, the date and a bar with the share of each option.
The winning option carries a trophy.

Use **Show** to filter the cards.

| Choice | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every card |
| **Twitch polls** | Polls that ran on Twitch |
| **Predictions** | Twitch predictions |
| **Chat polls** | The bot's own polls in chat |

The page remembers your **Show** choice.

A card shows a status only when it changes how you read the result.

| Status | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Canceled** | The prediction was canceled and the points went back. |
| **Ended early** | The poll was ended before its minutes were over. |
| **No result recorded** | The poll or prediction started, but the bot never saw it end. |

Polls that nobody voted on fold into one line, such as "3 with no votes".
Choose that line to show their cards.

A chat poll gets a winner only when it is closed and one option has more votes than every other option.

## Start from the home page

The home page has the same **Start a poll** and **Start a prediction** actions.
They open the same dialogs, so a poll or prediction started there shows on this page too.
See the [dashboard tour](/reference/getting-started/dashboard-tour) for the home page.

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**The Now panel does not show Start a poll or Prediction.**
Only a moderator, a lead moderator, an editor or the broadcaster sees these actions.
A person without them sees only what runs and the history.

::
