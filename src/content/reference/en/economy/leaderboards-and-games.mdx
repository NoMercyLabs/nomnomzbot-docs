You add the channel's leaderboards on the **Leaderboards** tab of the **Economy** page in the sidebar.
A leaderboard ranks viewers by the [channel currency](/reference/economy/currency-and-balances).

## Who can use this

A **Moderator**, **Lead moderator**, **Editor** or **Broadcaster** may see, add, change and delete leaderboards.
Someone who may not see leaderboards does not see the **Leaderboards** tab.

## Add a leaderboard

1. Open **Economy** in the sidebar and choose the **Leaderboards** tab.
   The list shows "No leaderboards yet." when the channel has none.
2. Choose **New leaderboard**.
   The **New leaderboard** dialog opens.
3. Choose the fields (see the table below).
4. Choose **Save leaderboard**.
   The dialog closes and the leaderboard shows in the list.

| Label | Choices | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ranks by** | **Balance**, **Earned**, **Spent** | **Balance** |
| **Period** | **All time**, **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** | **All time** |
| **Over** | **Whole channel**, **One jar** | **Whole channel** |
| **Places shown** | A number from 1 to 100 | 10 |
| **Visible to viewers** | On or off | On |

When **Over** is **One jar**, a **Jar** field shows.
Pick a savings jar there.
Without a jar, the dialog shows "Pick a jar."

Each row in the list shows what it ranks, then the set, the period and the places, for example "Whole channel · All time · top 10".
A leaderboard with **Visible to viewers** off carries the badge **Private**.

## How the bot ranks viewers

| **Ranks by** | What counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Balance** | The viewer's balance now. The **Period** does not change it. |
| **Earned** | The currency the viewer gained in the period. |
| **Spent** | The currency the viewer spent in the period. |

**Daily** starts at midnight UTC today.
**Weekly** starts on the last Sunday at midnight UTC.
**Monthly** starts on the first day of the month at midnight UTC.
**All time** counts everything.

A viewer who opted out of leaderboards is not ranked.

::Callout

The bot ranks only **Whole channel** leaderboards.
A **One jar** leaderboard is saved, but it ranks no viewers.

::

## Show a leaderboard in chat

A viewer types `!leaderboard` in chat.
The bot answers in the channel's one bot voice with the top 5 viewers of one leaderboard.
It picks the first leaderboard with **Visible to viewers** on.
When none is on, it picks the first leaderboard of the channel.

| Result | What the bot answers by default |
| --- | --- |
| The channel has no leaderboard | "No leaderboard is configured for this channel yet." |
| The leaderboard ranks nobody | "The leaderboard doesn't have any ranked entries yet." |

## Change or delete a leaderboard

1. To change a leaderboard, choose **Edit leaderboard** on its row.
   The **Edit leaderboard** dialog opens with the saved values.
   Choose **Save leaderboard**.
2. To delete a leaderboard, choose **Delete leaderboard** on its row.
   The dialog **Delete this leaderboard?** shows "The ranking stops. Balances stay."
   Choose **Delete leaderboard** to confirm.
