---
title: Leaderboards and games
description: Add, change and delete leaderboards that rank viewers by the channel currency, and show the top viewers in chat.
---

A balance leaderboard shows who holds the most [channel currency](/reference/economy/currency-and-balances) now, while an earned or spent leaderboard ranks activity during a period.
Configure them on the **Leaderboards** tab of **Economy**, then use the chat command to show a ranking to viewers.

## Who can use this

By default, a **Moderator**, **Lead moderator**, **Editor** or **Broadcaster** may see, add, change and delete leaderboards.
Someone who may not see leaderboards does not see the **Leaderboards** tab.

## Before you add one

Chat shows only one leaderboard, so the order of your list matters.
The list is sorted by **Ranks by** (**Balance**, **Earned**, **Spent**), not by the order you created the boards.
`!leaderboard` picks the first leaderboard in that list with **Visible to viewers** on.
A visible **Balance** board therefore wins over an **Earned** board.
When no leaderboard has **Visible to viewers** on, the command picks the first leaderboard in the list, so turning it off does not by itself keep every ranking out of chat.

With **Over** set to **One jar**, your channel must own the jar or have an accepted membership in it.
Otherwise the ranking is empty.

## Add a leaderboard

1. Open **Economy** in the sidebar and choose the **Leaderboards** tab.
   The list shows "No leaderboards yet." when the channel has none.
2. Choose **New leaderboard**.
   The **New leaderboard** dialog opens.
3. Choose the fields (see the table below).
4. Choose **Save leaderboard**.
   The dialog closes and the leaderboard shows in the list.

| Label | Choices | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ranks by** | **Balance**, **Earned**, **Spent** | **Balance** |
| **Period** | **All time**, **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** | **All time** |
| **Over** | **Whole channel**, **One jar** | **Whole channel** |
| **Places shown** | A whole number of 1 or more (the field suggests up to 100) | 10 |
| **Visible to viewers** | On or off | On |

When **Over** is **One jar**, a **Jar** field shows.
Pick a savings jar there.
Without a jar, the dialog shows "Pick a jar."

Each row in the list shows what it ranks, then the set, the period and the places, for example "Whole channel · All time · top 10".
A leaderboard with **Visible to viewers** off carries the badge **Private**.
A value of 0 or less in **Places shown** is refused.

## How the bot ranks viewers

| **Ranks by** | What counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Balance** | For a **Whole channel** leaderboard, the viewer's balance now. The **Period** does not change it. |
| **Earned** | For a **Whole channel** leaderboard, currency gained during the period. |
| **Spent** | For a **Whole channel** leaderboard, currency spent during the period. |

**Daily** starts at midnight UTC today.
**Weekly** starts at midnight UTC on the most recent Sunday (today, if it is Sunday).
**Monthly** starts on the first day of the month at midnight UTC.
**All time** counts everything.

A viewer whose leaderboard exclusion is recorded is not ranked.
A moderator can see the exclusion in the viewer's profile, on the **Leaderboard** row of the points section: **Shown**, or **Hidden by their choice**.
See [People](/reference/community/people).

With **Over** set to **One jar**, the ranking totals each viewer's contributions to that jar during the period, whatever **Ranks by** says.

For example, a **Whole channel**, **Earned**, **Weekly** board ranks currency earned since Sunday, not the largest long-term balance.

## Show a leaderboard in chat

A viewer types `!leaderboard` in chat.
The bot answers with the top 5 viewers of the leaderboard chosen as described in **Before you add one**, whatever **Places shown** says.
The reply lists each rank, the viewer's Twitch ID and the amount.

Everyone can use the command.
By default it has a 90-second cooldown.
You can change it or turn it off with the other [built-in commands](/reference/commands/built-in-commands).

Use your channel's command prefix if it differs from `!`.

| Result | What the bot answers by default |
| --- | --- |
| The channel has no leaderboard | "No leaderboard is configured for this channel yet." |
| The leaderboard ranks nobody | "The leaderboard doesn't have any ranked entries yet." |

## Change or delete a leaderboard

1. To change a leaderboard, choose **Edit leaderboard** on its row.
   The **Edit leaderboard** dialog opens with the saved values.
   Change any of the fields from the table above, then choose **Save leaderboard**.
2. To delete a leaderboard, choose **Delete leaderboard** on its row.
   The dialog **Delete this leaderboard?** shows "The ranking stops. Balances stay."
   Choose **Delete leaderboard** to confirm.
