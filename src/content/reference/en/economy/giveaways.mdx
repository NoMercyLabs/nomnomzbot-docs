---
title: Giveaways
description: Make a giveaway, let viewers enter, draw the winners, give a prize and keep a pool of codes for a code prize.
---

You run the channel's giveaways on the **Giveaways** page in the sidebar.
A giveaway lets viewers enter, then the bot draws one or more winners and gives each winner the prize.

## Who can use this

A moderator, a lead moderator, an editor or the broadcaster may see, make, change, run and delete giveaways.
Only the broadcaster sees the **Code pools** panel and may make or delete a code pool (see Keep a pool of codes below).

## Make a giveaway

1. Open **Giveaways** in the sidebar.
   The **Giveaways** list shows "No giveaways yet." when the channel has none.
2. Choose **New giveaway**.
   The **New giveaway** dialog opens.
3. Type a **Title** and set the fields (see the tables below).
4. Choose **Create giveaway**.
   The dialog closes and the giveaway shows in the list with the status **Draft**.

Each row shows the title, then the entries, the number of winners and how viewers enter, for example "0 entries · 1 winner · Keyword !enter".

### Entering

| Label | Choices | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How viewers enter** | **Typing the keyword in chat**, **Active viewers, no keyword** | **Typing the keyword in chat** |
| **Keyword** | Any text. Shows only for **Typing the keyword in chat**. | `!enter` |
| **Entry cost** | A whole number of 0 or more, or empty | Empty (free) |
| **Entries per viewer** | A whole number of 1 or more | 1 |

With **Typing the keyword in chat**, a viewer enters by typing a chat message that is exactly the keyword.
Upper and lower case do not matter.
The bot does not answer in chat when a viewer enters or when an entry is refused.

With **Active viewers, no keyword**, viewers do nothing to enter.
At the draw, the bot takes every viewer who chatted in the last 30 minutes.
This counts chat on every platform the channel has connected.

A viewer can also enter through the pipeline step **Enter giveaway** (see the last section).
That step and the keyword are the two ways a viewer enters.

When a viewer enters, the bot takes the **Entry cost** from their [channel currency](/reference/economy/currency-and-balances).
A viewer who does not have enough currency does not enter.
With **Active viewers, no keyword**, the bot takes no **Entry cost**.

Each viewer holds one entry, and a value above 1 in **Entries per viewer** changes nothing.
An entry holds tickets, and the draw counts the tickets (see Tickets per viewer below).

### Who may enter

| Label | What it does | Where it applies | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Twitch subscribers only** | Only subscribers, VIPs, artists and moderators of the channel may take part. | Keyword, **Enter giveaway** step, active viewers | Off |
| **Leave out moderators** | Moderators may enter, but the draw never picks them. | The draw, for every way in | Off |
| **Viewers must confirm they are 18 or older** | Only a viewer who confirmed they are 18 or older may enter. | Keyword, **Enter giveaway** step | Off |
| **Least standing level** | A viewer needs at least this community standing level. | Keyword, **Enter giveaway** step, active viewers | Empty (no limit) |
| **Least watch minutes** | A viewer needs at least this many minutes of watch time in the channel. | Keyword, **Enter giveaway** step, active viewers | Empty (no limit) |

The draw never picks the broadcaster, for every way in.
A viewer who is muted or shadowbanned by the bot cannot enter with the keyword, and cannot claim a win in chat.

### Tickets per viewer

A ticket is one chance in the draw.
More tickets raise the chance to win.

| Label | Who gets these tickets | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Twitch tier 1 sub** | A tier 1 subscriber, and a VIP, an artist or a moderator without a sub tier | 1 |
| **Twitch tier 2 sub** | A tier 2 subscriber | 1 |
| **Twitch tier 3 sub** | A tier 3 subscriber | 1 |
| **VIP** | A VIP, an artist or a moderator | 1 |

A viewer gets the highest count that fits them, and never fewer than 1.
A count of 1 everywhere means every viewer has the same chance.

### Drawing

| Label | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Number of winners** | How many different viewers the draw picks. | 1 |
| **Claim window (minutes)** | How long a winner has to answer in chat. | Empty |
| **Close entries at** | The time at which entries close on their own. | Empty (you close them) |

With a claim window, a winner claims the win by typing any chat message in the window.
A winner who does not chat in time loses the win, and the badge shows **Forfeited**.
With an empty claim window, every winner counts as **Claimed** at once.

The bot checks the claim window and the close time once a minute.
The close time must be in the future when you save.

### Prize

| **What winners get** | What the bot does for each winner | Extra field |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **An announcement in chat** | Saves the winner in the winners list. | None |
| **Currency** | Adds the channel currency to the winner's balance. | **Currency per winner**, **Pay from the pot** |
| **A command or pipeline runs** | Runs the command, pipeline or action you pick, with the winner as the viewer who ran it. | **Command or pipeline** |
| **A code from a pool** | Takes the next free code from the pool and sends it to the winner in a whisper. | **Code pool** |

The default is **An announcement in chat**.
With this choice, a draw only records the winner in the winners list, and the bot posts nothing in chat.
To have the bot name a winner in chat, choose **A command or pipeline runs** with a command that posts a chat line.

With **Pay from the pot** on, the prize is the entry costs that viewers paid, split evenly over the winners.
The first winner also gets what is left after the split.

A giveaway with **A code from a pool** and an **Entry cost** above 0 needs **Viewers must confirm they are 18 or older** on.
Without it, the bot does not save the giveaway.

## Run a giveaway

1. Choose **Open for entries** on the giveaway's row.
   The status changes to **Open** and viewers can enter.
2. Choose **Close entries** when you want to stop new entries.
   The status changes to **Closed**.
3. Choose **Draw winners**.
   The status changes to **Drawn** and the winners show in the **Winners of** panel.

You can also choose **Draw winners** while the giveaway is still **Open**.
The draw picks fewer winners than **Number of winners** when fewer viewers can win.
When nobody can win, the draw does not happen.

The channel has one active giveaway at a time.
You cannot open a giveaway while another one is **Open**, or **Closed** and not drawn yet.

The page shows the winners and the entries of one giveaway.
It picks the **Open** giveaway first, then the **Closed** one, then a **Drawn** one.
The **Entries of** panel lists each viewer who entered with their tickets.

## Draw a new winner

Choose **Draw a new winner** on a winner's row.
The bot picks a new winner who has not won this giveaway before, and gives them the prize.
The new winner's row carries **Redrawn**.

## Change or delete a giveaway

Only a **Draft** or **Closed** giveaway shows **Edit giveaway**.

1. To change a giveaway, choose **Edit giveaway** on its row.
   The **Edit giveaway** dialog opens with the saved values.
2. Change the fields and choose **Save giveaway**.
   The dialog closes and the row shows the new values.
3. To delete a giveaway, choose the trash button on its row.
   A dialog opens with the title "Delete", the giveaway's title and a question mark.
   It shows "This removes the giveaway for good." and a line for each count that goes with it.
4. Choose **Delete giveaway** to confirm.
   The dialog closes and the giveaway leaves the list.

## Keep a pool of codes

A code pool holds the codes that winners get for the prize **A code from a pool**.
The page shows only how many codes a pool has, never the codes.

1. In the **Code pools** panel, choose **New pool**.
   The **New code pool** dialog opens.
2. Type a **Name**.
   You may add a **Note (optional)**.
3. Paste the codes in **Codes**, one code per line.
   Empty lines and repeated codes are skipped.
4. Choose **Create pool**.
   The pool shows in the list with its count, for example "10 of 10 available".

Each winner takes the oldest free code.
When the pool runs out, the winners after that get no code.
When the whisper cannot be sent, the code stays with that winner and no other winner gets it.

To delete a pool, choose **Delete** on its row, then **Delete pool**.
The pool and its unused codes are removed.
A pool that an active giveaway uses cannot be deleted.

## Run a giveaway from a pipeline

The **Pipelines** page in the sidebar has three giveaway steps in the group **Giveaways**.

| Step | What it does | Which giveaway |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open giveaway** | Opens a giveaway for entries. | The giveaway you pick. |
| **Draw giveaway** | Draws the winners. | The giveaway you pick, or the channel's active giveaway when you leave it blank. |
| **Enter giveaway** | Enters the viewer who started the pipeline. | The giveaway you pick, or the channel's active giveaway when you leave it blank. |

1. Open **Pipelines** in the sidebar and open a pipeline.
   The pipeline's steps show.
2. Choose **Add a step at the end**.
   The list **Steps to add** opens.
3. Type giveaway in **Find a step**.
   The three giveaway steps show.
4. Choose a step, then fill in the giveaway it uses.
   The step shows at the end of the pipeline.
5. Choose **Save**.
   The page shows "Pipeline saved."

When **Draw giveaway** or **Enter giveaway** is blank and no giveaway is active, the step fails.
