YouTube connects to the channel as a second stream platform, beside Twitch.
This page shows where the YouTube connection appears and what the bot does with it.
How you connect it is on [Connect platforms](/reference/getting-started/connect-platforms).

## Who can use this

| Role | What they may do |
| --- | --- |
| **Moderator**, **Lead moderator** | See the YouTube row and its status. |
| **Editor**, **Broadcaster** | Connect, reconnect and disconnect YouTube. |

The roles are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).

## What the connection asks for

**Connect** opens Google's own sign-in page in a new window.
It asks for one permission set, **Manage your channel**.
When you accept, the window closes and the row shows **Connected** with the YouTube account name.

## The YouTube row on the Stream page

The [Stream page](/reference/community/stream-page) has a panel **Where it goes**.
It lists Twitch, Kick, YouTube and X Live.
The YouTube row shows **Connected** with the account name, or **Not connected**.

A person who may change connections sees **Connect** beside **Not connected**.
The panel asks "Open YouTube to connect?" before the sign-in window opens.

## The YouTube row on the Integrations page

Open **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Integrations**.
The **Connected services** list has a **YouTube** row.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The connection works. The row shows "as" and the account name. |
| **Not connected** | No YouTube account is connected. |
| **Sign in again** | The saved YouTube login no longer works. |
| **Sign in again (the saved login is unreadable)** | The bot cannot read the saved login. |

Choose **Details** on the row to see **Permission sets: Manage your channel**.
Choose **Reconnect** to sign in to Google again.

## What the music player does with YouTube

The [music player](/reference/music/music-player) can play YouTube videos.
The video plays in a music player overlay in your streaming software, not on your YouTube account.
That overlay tells the bot what it plays, and whether the video plays, is paused, has ended or failed.

| Report from the overlay | What the bot does |
| --- | --- |
| The video plays or is paused | The bot updates what the music player shows as now playing. |
| A song request ended or failed | The bot starts the next song request. |

Only one overlay plays the channel's YouTube music at a time.
Song requests from YouTube do not need the channel's YouTube connection.

## Disconnect YouTube

1. On the **YouTube** row of **Connected services**, choose **Disconnect**.
   A dialog **Disconnect YouTube?** opens.
   It counts the [pipelines](/reference/commands/pipelines) that use music actions, because they stop working.
2. Choose **Disconnect** in the dialog.
   A message says "YouTube is disconnected."
   The row shows **Not connected**.
