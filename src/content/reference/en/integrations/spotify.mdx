You connect Spotify on the **Integrations** page, under **Settings** in the sidebar.
The connection lets the bot read and control what plays on your Spotify account.
The [music player](/reference/music/music-player) uses it.

## Who can use this page

| Role | What they may do |
| --- | --- |
| **Moderator**, **Lead moderator** | See the page and the connection status. |
| **Editor**, **Broadcaster** | Add the Spotify app, connect, reconnect and disconnect. |

Only the **Broadcaster** sees **Settings** in the sidebar.
A **Moderator**, **Lead moderator** or **Editor** opens the page from a direct link.
The link is the dashboard address followed by `/app/u/` and the channel name, then `/settings/integrations`.
The roles are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).

## Make your Spotify app

The bot always connects to Spotify through the channel's own Spotify app.
You make this app once at Spotify.

1. Create an app at `https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard`.
2. Add this redirect address to the app, with your own dashboard address in front:

   ```
   https://your-dashboard.example.com/api/v1/integrations/spotify/callback
   ```

3. Copy the app's **Client ID** and **Client secret** from Spotify.

## Add your Spotify app

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Integrations**.
   The **Connected services** list shows a **Spotify** row.
   With no app saved, the row says **No Spotify app yet**.
   When Spotify is also not connected, the **Your own Spotify app** dialog opens by itself.
2. If the dialog is closed, choose **Add your Spotify app**.
3. Type the app's client id in **Client ID**.
4. Type the app's client secret in **Client secret**.
5. Choose **Save**.
   The dialog closes and a message says "Your Spotify app is saved."
   The row now says **Your own Spotify app**.
   Under it, **Client ID ending in** shows the last four characters of the client id.

Both fields are required.
The secret is stored encrypted, and the dashboard never shows it again.
To change it, choose **Edit** and type a new secret.

## Connect your Spotify account

1. On the **Spotify** row, choose **Connect**.
   Spotify's own sign-in page opens in a new window.
   When the browser blocks the window, the dashboard tab itself goes to Spotify.
2. Sign in to Spotify and accept the permissions.
   You are back on the dashboard.
   The row now shows **Connected** and the Spotify account name after **as**.

Choose **Details** on the row to see what the connection holds.

| Line | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Permission sets** | The groups of Spotify permissions you accepted. |
| **Can do** | The things the bot has seen work on your account, such as **use Spotify Premium features**. |
| **Last sync** | When the bot last synced with Spotify. |

**Connect** asks for two permission sets.

| Permission set | What it lets the bot do |
| --- | --- |
| **Control playback** | See what plays, and play, pause and skip. |
| **Read your library** | Read your playlists and saved songs, and add to them. |

## Reconnect

When the row shows **Sign in again**, Spotify no longer accepts the saved login.
Choose **Reconnect** on the row.
Sign in to Spotify.
The row shows **Connected** again.

## Disconnect Spotify

1. On the **Spotify** row, choose **Disconnect**.
   A dialog **Disconnect Spotify?** opens.
   It lists the parts of the channel that stop working, with the real counts.
2. Choose **Disconnect** in the dialog.
   A message says "Spotify is disconnected."
   The row shows **Not connected**.

Your Spotify app stays saved, so you can connect again with **Connect**.

## Remove your Spotify app

1. On the **Spotify** row, choose **Edit**.
2. Choose **Remove my app** in the dialog.
   A message says "Your Spotify app is removed."
   The row says **No Spotify app yet**.

Without the app, the channel cannot connect to Spotify.

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**The row shows "Sign in again (the saved login is unreadable)".**
The bot cannot read the saved Spotify login.
Choose **Reconnect** and sign in to Spotify again.

::
