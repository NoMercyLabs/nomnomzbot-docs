You link VTube Studio on the **VTube Studio** page in the sidebar, in the **Live** group.
After the link, the bot can load a model, fire a hotkey and turn an expression on or off.
The page has three tabs: **Connection**, **Hotkeys** and **Expressions**.

## Who can use this page

A **Moderator** or higher may open the page and see the link.
A **Moderator** or higher may also load models, fire hotkeys and set expressions.
Only the **Broadcaster** may change the link, approve it and rotate the bridge token.

For anyone else, the **Connection** form shows but cannot be changed.
The **Load model**, **Trigger**, **Activate** and **Deactivate** buttons show only to someone who may control the model.

## Link VTube Studio

1. Open **VTube Studio** in the sidebar.
   The **Connection** tab opens.
2. Switch on **Link to VTube Studio**.
3. Leave **Connection type** at **Direct** and **Address** at `ws://localhost:8001`.
   **Address** is one field that holds the whole address.
   The default fits VTube Studio on the same computer as the bot, unless the streamer changed its address in VTube Studio.
4. Choose **Save**.
5. Choose **Authorize**.
   The button shows **Waiting for approval**, and the page shows "Click Allow in VTube Studio. This waits up to a minute."
6. In VTube Studio, click **Allow**.
   **Approval** now shows "Approved. The bot keeps the key."

The status at the top of the page and beside **VTube Studio** tells you the state of the link.

| Status | Dot | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The link to VTube Studio is switched off | gray | **Link to VTube Studio** is off. |
| Waiting for your approval in VTube Studio | orange | The link is on, but VTube Studio has not approved the bot. |
| Approved, not connected yet | orange | VTube Studio approved the bot. The bot has not connected yet. |
| Connected | green | The bot is connected. The other two tabs work. |
| The link to VTube Studio failed | red | The bot could not reach VTube Studio. |

When the bot has connected once, **Last connected** shows how long ago that was.

## Settings on the Connection tab

| Label | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Link to VTube Studio** | Switches the link on or off. When it is off, every VTube Studio action is refused. | Off |
| **Connection type** | **Direct**: the bot dials VTube Studio itself at **Address**. **Bridge**: the bot reaches VTube Studio through a browser source in OBS on the streamer's computer. | **Direct** |
| **Address** | The VTube Studio address for **Direct**. It starts with `ws://` or `wss://`. An empty box saves the default. | `ws://localhost:8001` |
| **Events to listen to** | The VTube Studio events the bot listens to. | The first eight events |

The events in **Events to listen to** are **Model loaded**, **Tracking status changed**, **Background changed**, **Model settings changed**, **Hotkey triggered**, **Model clicked**, **Items**, **Post processing**, **Model moved (high volume)**, **Model outline (high volume)** and **Model animation (high volume)**.
The last three send many events per second, so they are off by default.

## Load a model or fire a hotkey

1. Open the **Hotkeys** tab.
2. Pick a model in **Model** and choose **Load model**.
   VTube Studio loads that model.
3. Pick a hotkey in **Hotkey** and choose **Trigger**.
   VTube Studio runs that hotkey.

The **Hotkey** list holds the hotkeys of the model that is loaded now.
Until the link shows **Connected**, the tab shows "This shows once VTube Studio is linked."

## Turn an expression on or off

1. Open the **Expressions** tab.
2. Pick an expression in **Expression**.
3. Choose **Activate** to turn it on, or **Deactivate** to turn it off.

## Control the model from a pipeline

A [pipeline](/reference/commands/pipelines) can control the model with the actions in the **VTube Studio** category.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Load a VTube Studio model | Loads the model with the id you give. |
| Trigger a VTube Studio hotkey | Runs a hotkey of the loaded model, by its id or name. |
| Activate or deactivate a VTube Studio expression | Turns an expression on or off. Default: on. |
| Move, rotate, or resize the VTube Studio model | Sets position, rotation or size. Give at least one of them. Default move time: 0.3 seconds. |
| Apply a color tint to the VTube Studio model | Tints the whole model, or only the parts with a tag you give. Each color channel is 0 to 255. Default opacity: 255. |
| Send a raw VTube Studio API request | Sends any VTube Studio request type with its data. |

When an action fails, the pipeline step fails and the reason is kept in the `vts.last_error` variable.

<details>
<summary>Rotate the bridge token</summary>

With **Connection type** set to **Bridge**, the **Rotate bridge token** button shows.
Choose it, then choose **Rotate token** in the dialog.
The bridge gets a new credential, and the old credential stops working at once.

</details>

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**Authorize shows an error at once.**
Switch on **Link to VTube Studio** and choose **Save** first.
**Authorize** works only with **Connection type** set to **Direct**.
Then check that VTube Studio is running at the **Address**.

**A message says "VTube Studio did not answer. Check the connection."**
The bot could not reach VTube Studio.
Check that VTube Studio is running, and that the status shows **Connected**.

**Authorize stops after a minute.**
Nobody clicked **Allow** in VTube Studio in time.
Choose **Authorize** again and click **Allow**.

::
