title: Supporter sources

You connect the services your supporters pay through on the **Supporter sources** page.
Open **Settings** and find it in the **Connections** group.
The tips and memberships the bot receives then show on the **Supporters** page in the sidebar.

## Who can use this page

A moderator may open the page and read the list.
Only the broadcaster may set up, change or remove a source.
For anyone else the page shows the list without the buttons.

## The services

The list **Services your supporters use** shows every service the bot supports.
Each service sends its supporter data in its own way.
The bot picks that way for you.

| Service | How the bot gets the events | What you do in the dialog |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buy Me a Coffee** | Sends events to the bot by webhook | Paste a secret, then paste the address into the service |
| **DonorDrive** | The bot checks the service on a schedule | Paste a secret |
| **Fourthwall** | Sends events to the bot by webhook | Paste a secret, then paste the address into the service |
| **Ko-fi** | Sends events to the bot by webhook | Paste a secret, then paste the address into the service |
| **Pally** | The bot listens on the service's websocket | Paste a secret |
| **Patreon** | Sends events to the bot by webhook | Pick a connected account |
| **Shopify** | Sends events to the bot by webhook | Pick a connected account |
| **StreamElements** | The bot listens on the service's socket | Paste a secret |
| **Streamlabs** | The bot listens on the service's socket | Paste a secret |
| **Tipeee** | The bot listens on the service's socket | Paste a secret |
| **Treatstream** | The bot listens on the service's socket | Pick a connected account |

The middle column is the text the dashboard shows.
A socket and a websocket are both a line the bot keeps open to the service.
You do not set them up.

Each row carries a state.

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not set up** | The source has no connection yet. |
| **Connected** | The connection is saved and **Receive events** is on. |
| **Paused** | The connection is saved and **Receive events** is off. |

Under the state, a row shows **Last event** with the time of the newest event, or **No events yet**.

## Set up a source with a secret

Use these steps for every service in the table that says "Paste a secret".

1. Choose **Set up** on the row of the service.
   A dialog opens with the title "Set up" and the name of the service.
2. Paste the secret or token from the service into **Secret or token**.
3. Leave **Receive events** on.
   It is on by default.
4. Choose **Save**.
   The dashboard shows "Connection saved." and the row shows **Connected**.

The button on the row is now **Manage**.
The secret goes to the bot and is never shown again.
Afterward the box says "A secret is stored. Type a new one to replace it."

## Give the address to Buy Me a Coffee, Fourthwall or Ko-fi

These three services send their events to an address of the bot.
You paste that address into the service yourself.
The bot makes the address when you save the secret for the first time.

1. Choose **Manage** on the row of the service.
2. Copy the address in **Address to give the service**.
3. Paste it into the webhook settings of the service.

Webhook addresses in general are explained on [Webhooks and event feeds](/reference/integrations/webhooks-and-event-feeds).

## Set up Patreon, Shopify or Treatstream

These three services use an account you connect under **Integrations** in **Settings**.
You paste no secret and no address.

1. Connect the account under **Integrations** first.
2. Choose **Set up** on the row of the service.
3. Pick the account in **Connected account**.
4. Choose **Save**.
   The dashboard shows "Connection saved." and the row shows **Connected**.

For **Patreon** and **Shopify**, the bot registers its own webhook on the service when you save.
**Manage** then shows **Address to give the service**, but you do not need to copy it.
For **Treatstream**, the bot listens on the service with the account, and there is no address.

When no account is connected, the dialog shows "No `{name}` account is connected yet. Connect it under Integrations first.", where `{name}` is the name of the service.
When the bot cannot register its webhook, the dialog shows "The connection could not be saved."
Choose **Save** again to try once more.

## Pause or change a source

1. Choose **Manage** on the row.
2. Turn **Receive events** off to pause the source, or on to start it again.
   You can also type a new secret to replace the stored one.
3. Choose **Save**.

**Save** stays off until you change something.
Turning the switch keeps the stored secret.

## Remove a source

1. Choose **Remove** on the row.
   The dashboard asks "Remove `{name}`?", where `{name}` is the name of the service.
2. Choose **Remove connection**.

The stored secret is deleted.
The bot stops listening to this service.
For a webhook service, the address the bot made for it stops working too.

Tips already recorded stay.
You can set the source up again.

## Read the tips and memberships

Open **Supporters** in the sidebar.
The panel **Tips and memberships** lists what the connected services sent, newest first.
A moderator may read it.

Each row shows the supporter's name, the kind, the amount and the tier when the service sent one, and the time.

| Kind | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Tip** | A one-time tip. |
| **Membership** | A paid membership. |
| **Merch** | A merch order. |
| **Charity** | A charity donation. |

When nothing is recorded, the panel says "No tips or memberships recorded yet."
The bot records each payment once, even when a service sends it twice.

## Answer a supporter in chat

Each recorded event can start an event response, for one kind or for every supporter event.
The bot then answers in chat in the channel's one bot voice.
How to set this up is on [Event responses](/reference/automation/event-responses).

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**The row shows Connected, but no tip ever arrives.**
For Buy Me a Coffee, Fourthwall or Ko-fi, check that the address from **Address to give the service** is pasted into the service.
Check that the secret in the service matches the one you saved.
Save a new secret if you are not sure.

::

::Troubleshooting

**Tips stopped coming in.**
Open **Manage** and check that **Receive events** is on.
A paused source drops new events without a message.

::
