title: Integrations page
description: See every outside service your channel uses, connect or reconnect one, allow more permissions, and disconnect it safely.

The **Integrations** page lists the outside services your channel uses, such as Spotify, YouTube, Kick, Patreon, Treatstream and Shopify.
Each service has one row that shows whether it works, which account it uses, and what it may do for you.
You open it under **Settings**, and its heading reads **Connected services**.

Use this page to connect a service, fix one that stopped working, give it more permissions, or cut it off.
Each service keeps its own setup guide: [Spotify](/reference/integrations/spotify), [YouTube](/reference/integrations/youtube), [Discord](/reference/integrations/discord), [OBS](/reference/integrations/obs), [VTube Studio](/reference/integrations/vtube-studio) and [supporter sources](/reference/integrations/supporter-sources).
This page explains what the rows and buttons have in common.

## Who can use this page

| What | Who may do it |
| --- | --- |
| Open the page and see the rows | A **Moderator** and up |
| Connect, reconnect, allow more permissions, disconnect, and manage the Spotify app | An **Editor** and up |

Without that right, you see the rows but no buttons.
The roles are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).

## Read a row

A row shows the service's logo and name, a short description, a status badge, and the account it is connected as.
The badge tells you what to do next.

| Badge | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The service is linked and working. | Nothing. |
| **Not connected** | The service is not linked to your channel. | Choose **Connect**. |
| **Sign in again** | The service no longer accepts the saved sign-in. | Choose **Reconnect**. |
| **Sign in again (the saved login is unreadable)** | The bot could not read the sign-in it saved. | Choose **Reconnect**. |
| **Signed in only** | You signed in, but the service holds no permissions for the bot to act. | Choose **Reconnect** and accept the permissions. |

Sign in again and the unreadable login both show the row in the attention color.
You can fix both yourself, so they appear here and not only in a log.
Twitch is your main account and always shows as the signed-in account.
It has no **Disconnect** button on this page.

## See the details

When the bot knows more about a service, the row has a **Details** button.
It opens a short list.

- **Permission sets** names the groups of permissions you gave the service, in plain words. For Spotify these are **Control playback**, **Read your library** and **Play in the browser**.
- **Can do** lists what the service allows right now, such as control playback or read your library.
- **Last sync** shows the date and time of the last successful sync.
- **You can also allow** shows buttons for permissions you have not given yet. Today YouTube offers **See your channel members** and **See your YouTube analytics**.

A permission set is a bundle you accept on the service's own page.
Giving only what a feature needs keeps the rest closed.
Choosing one **You can also allow** button sends you to the service to approve that one set.

## Connect a service

1. Find the row and choose **Connect**.
2. A window opens on the service's own sign-in page.
3. Sign in and accept the permissions.
4. Return to the dashboard. The badge changes to **Connected**.

The bot never sees your password.
It only keeps the permission the service gives it.
If something goes wrong, a red line appears under the list with the reason.

### Reconnect

On a connected service the same button reads **Reconnect**.
Use it when the badge says **Sign in again**, or when you want to grant more permissions.
A reconnect asks for the main permission set and every extra one the service offers in one go.
You can still untick a set on the service's own page.

### Shopify needs your shop name

The Shopify row has a **Shop name** field.
Type your shop name first.
**Connect** stays grayed out until the field has text.
The field disappears once the shop is connected.

### OBS shows Set up

OBS does not sign in on a web page.
Its row has **Set up** instead, which opens the OBS page.
See [OBS](/reference/integrations/obs) for the steps.

## Disconnect a service

1. Choose **Disconnect** on the row.
2. The bot counts what depends on the service right now and shows it in a dialog titled **Disconnect** followed by the service name.
3. Read the list. The dialog says these parts of your channel stop working until you connect again.
4. Confirm with **Disconnect**, or close the dialog to keep the service.

The counts come from your channel at that moment, such as how many things use it.
A count that reads "at least" is a minimum, because the bot could not count everything.
After you confirm, a message says the service is disconnected.
You can connect it again at any time.

## The Spotify app panel

The Spotify row has an extra panel for the Spotify app the bot uses to ask for permission.
It is shown only to people who can manage integrations.
Its title tells you which app is in use.

| Title | Meaning | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your own Spotify app** | Your own app is saved. The detail shows the last four characters of its client ID. | **Edit** |
| **The shared Spotify app** | Every channel on this bot uses the operator's app. | **Use your own Spotify app** |
| **No Spotify app yet** | No shared app exists and you saved none. | **Add your Spotify app** |

The button opens a dialog with **Client ID** and **Client secret**.
**Save** replaces both values.
The secret is stored sealed and never shown again, so the field only says a secret is stored.
Leave the secret blank in the field only if you plan to type a new one.
**Remove my app** deletes your own app.
When a shared app exists, the button reads **Use the shared app** and returns you to it.

When Spotify is not connected, no shared app exists and you saved no app, the dialog opens by itself, because nothing else can make Spotify work.
For the Spotify side of setup, see [Spotify](/reference/integrations/spotify).
