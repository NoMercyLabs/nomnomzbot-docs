You run the channel's stream on the **Stream** page in the sidebar.
The header shows **Live** or **Offline**.
When the stream is live, it shows how long it has run and the category, for example "Live for 1h 20m · Just Chatting".

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | **What viewers see**, **Where it goes** and **Before you go live**. |
| **Schedule** | The streams you plan on Twitch. |
| **Raid rules** | **Raid a channel** and the **Raid rules**. |
| **Ads** | **Run an ad**. |

## Who can use this page

A **Moderator**, **Lead moderator**, **Editor** or **Broadcaster** may open the page.
The **Broadcaster** may also open it to a **VIP**.
The roles are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).

| Action | Who may do it |
| --- | --- |
| Change the title, category, tags and content labels | A moderator and up. A VIP sees the form but cannot change it. |
| Connect another platform | An editor or the broadcaster. |
| Change the schedule | A moderator and up. |
| Raid a channel | An editor or the broadcaster. |
| Change the raid rules | An editor or the broadcaster. A moderator sees the rules but cannot change them. |
| Run an ad | An editor or the broadcaster. |

A tab shows to anyone who may read it.
A moderator sees the **Ads** tab, but only an editor or the broadcaster sees **Run an ad**.

## Change what viewers see

The **What viewers see** panel on the **Overview** tab holds "The title, category and tags people see on your channel."

1. Type the title in **Title**.
   The counter beside it shows the length, for example "42 / 140".
2. Search for a category under **Category** in **Search for a category**, and choose one from the list.
   The chosen category shows above the search box.
3. Type a tag in **Tags** and press Enter.
   The tag shows above the box.
4. Switch on each content label your stream has, under **Content labels**.
5. Choose **Save changes**.
   The changes go to the channel.

**Discard** puts every field back as it was saved.
If the save fails, your edits stay in the form.

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Up to 140 characters. **Recent titles** lists the title **Set on your channel now** and up to five titles of earlier streams. Choose one to use it again. |
| **Category** | The category Twitch knows. **Recent categories** lists the categories of earlier streams. |
| **Tags** | Up to 10 tags. The box turns off at 10: "10 of 10 tags. The most Twitch allows." A tag that is already there is not added again. |
| **Language** | Shown only: "Twitch sets the language. Change it in Twitch." |
| **Content labels** | "Twitch shows these to viewers. Switch on what your stream has." |

The content labels are **Politics and sensitive social issues**, **Strong language**, **Violent or graphic scenes**, **Gambling**, **Drugs or alcohol**, **Sexual themes** and **Paid promotion**.
**Paid promotion** "Tells viewers the stream has a paid sponsor or ad."
When the category is a mature-rated game, **Mature-rated game** shows switched on: "Twitch sets this from your category."

The **X** on a tag removes it.
A line under the box then says "Removed" with the tag name, and **Undo** puts the tag back.

### Where it goes

The **Where it goes** panel says "Your stream info goes to every platform you connect."
Twitch is always **Connected**.
Kick and YouTube show **Connected** or **Not connected**.

To connect a platform, choose **Connect** on its row, then **Connect** again in the question that opens.
A window of the platform opens to sign in.
Platforms are explained on [Connect platforms](/reference/getting-started/connect-platforms).

X Live shows **Not available yet**: "X Live support is not built yet."

## Check before you go live

The **Before you go live** panel on the **Overview** tab checks the channel.
Each line has a dot: a good result, a problem, or neutral.

| Check | Good | Problem or neutral |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title and category | "Title and category are set" | "Set a title and a category" |
| Tags | The count, for example "3 tags added. The most Twitch allows is 10." | No tags: the dot is neutral. |
| The bot | "The bot is in chat" | "The bot is not in chat" |
| Channel-point rewards | "All 5 rewards are on" | "Off or paused:" with the reward names and the count that is on. "Channel points are not available on this channel" when the channel has no channel points. |
| Shield mode | "Chat is open to everyone" | "Shield mode is on" |

A reward that is off is your choice, so its dot is neutral.
A check that cannot run says so, for example "Could not check the bot".

## Plan a stream

The **Schedule** tab holds "The streams you plan on Twitch."
A segment is one planned stream.

1. Choose **Add segment**.
   The **Add segment** dialog opens.
2. Pick the **Start time**.
3. Type the **Length in minutes**.
   It is 30 to 1440 minutes. Default: 120 minutes.
4. Type a **Title** and pick a **Category** if you want them.
5. Switch on **Repeat every week** for a weekly stream.
6. Choose **Save segment**.
   The segment shows in the list with its time and category.

A weekly segment is marked **Weekly**.
A segment with no title and no category shows as "Untitled stream".
When the list is empty, the tab shows "No segments yet."

### Change, skip or delete a segment

| Button on the row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Opens **Edit segment** with the saved values. The weekly switch is not in it. |
| **Cancel** | Skips the stream. The row is marked **Canceled**. |
| **Restore** | Puts a canceled stream back. |
| **Delete** | Asks "Delete" with the name. "The segment is removed from your Twitch schedule. Cancel keeps it and only skips one stream." Choose **Delete segment**. |

For a weekly segment, the delete question adds "Every week of this repeating segment is removed."

### Take a vacation

1. Switch on **Vacation mode**.
   "Twitch hides your schedule during a vacation."
2. Pick **Vacation starts** and **Vacation ends**.
3. Choose **Save vacation**.

**Save vacation** shows only when the vacation differs from what Twitch holds.
The end must come after the start.
If not, the tab shows "Pick a start and an end after it to save."

## Raid a channel

**Raid a channel** on the **Raid rules** tab sends your viewers to another channel.

1. Type the name in **Channel name** and choose **Raid**.
   The bot looks the channel up on Twitch.
2. The dialog asks "Raid" with the channel name: "Your viewers go to this channel when the countdown ends, and your stream ends for them."
3. Choose **Start raid**.
   A message says "The raid to" the channel "is counting down."

**Cancel raid** stops the raid until the countdown ends.
A message then says "The raid is canceled."
When no raid is running, it says "There is no raid to cancel."

If Twitch has no channel with that name, the panel says so in red, for example "Twitch has no channel named "examplechannel"."

## Set the raid rules

The **Raid rules** panel holds the point rules for raid targets on this channel.
Its text reads "How the bot ranks channels to raid. Your rules, only for this channel."
Each rule holds **Points** and a **Reason**: a short text that names the rule.

A new channel starts with no rules and every point at 0.

| Group | What the rule sets |
| --- | --- |
| **Preferred categories** | Points for each category you pick. **Points for other categories** holds the points for any other category. |
| **Keywords** | Points for words, separated by commas, matched in a channel's **Tags**, **Title** or both. |
| **Viewer bands** | Points for a viewer count between **Minimum viewers** and **Maximum viewers**. |
| **Time since your last raid** | **Points if never raided**, points for each step of **Within days** since your last raid to a channel, and **Points for older raids**. |
| **Who owes whom a raid** | **Points for one raid owed**, **Extra points per further raid owed**, and **Points when level**. |
| **Points if you follow them** | Points for a channel you follow. |

**Add category**, **Add keyword**, **Add band** and **Add step** add a row.
**Remove** on a row removes it.
Choose **Save rules** to save the whole set.

The bot refuses a set that breaks a limit, and keeps the old rules.
The reason shows in red beside the field.

| Limit | The red text |
| --- | --- |
| Points stay between -1000 and 1000. | "Points must stay between -1000 and 1000." with the value. |
| A category rule needs a category. | "Pick a category for this rule." |
| A keyword needs at least one word, and no empty word. | "Add at least one word to this keyword." or "One of the words is empty. Remove it." |
| A keyword matches tags, the title, or both. | "Turn on tags or title, or this keyword never matches." |
| A band starts at 0 viewers or more, and its minimum is not above its maximum. | "The minimum viewers cannot be below 0." or "The minimum (…) is above the maximum (…)." |
| A step is at least 1 day. | "A step needs at least 1 day." |

### Raids into your channel

The bot checks every raid into your channel.
A raid from a channel you banned, or from someone the bot's operator blocked on every channel, is marked hostile.
Moderators deal with hostile raids in the chat moderation tools, on [Moderation overview](/reference/moderation/overview).

## Run an ad

The **Ads** tab holds **Run an ad**: "Starts an ad break on your channel."

1. Choose a length under **Length**: 30, 60, 90, 120, 150 or 180 seconds.
   Default: 30 seconds.
2. Choose **Run ad**.
   A line says "The ad started.", or shows the message Twitch sent.
