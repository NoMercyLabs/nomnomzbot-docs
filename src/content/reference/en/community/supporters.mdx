---
title: Supporters
description: See who subscribed, gifted subs, cheered bits or tipped your channel recently, and who gave the most.
---

**Supporters** puts the people who back your channel in one place.
You can thank a new subscriber, see who gifted subs during a raid of generosity, or check who cheered the most bits, without scrolling through chat or the event log.
Twitch subscriptions, gifts and cheers sit on the left side of the page.
Tips and memberships from services such as Ko-fi or Streamlabs sit in the last panel.

Open **Supporters** in the **Loyalty** group of the sidebar.

## Who can use this page

By default, a **Moderator**, **Lead moderator**, **Editor** or **Broadcaster** may open the page.
The sidebar shows **Supporters** only to people who may open it.
The roles are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).

Two panels have their own requirement.
The number tiles, **Twitch subscriptions and gifts**, **Cheers** and **Top supporters** need the right to read the dashboard.
**Tips and memberships** needs the right to read supporters.
Someone who lacks one of them sees the page without that part.

## Read the numbers at the top

The row of tiles gives a quick count.
A tile for YouTube members appears only when the bot can read your YouTube members.
When it cannot, the tile is left out instead of showing 0.

| Tile | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Twitch subscribers** | The subscribers your channel has on Twitch right now. |
| **YouTube members** | The members your channel has on YouTube right now. |
| **Gift events** | How many gift events appear in the recent activity. One event can hold several subs, so this counts gifts, not gifted subs. |
| **Bits cheered** | The bits added up from the cheers in the recent activity. |

The first two tiles are live totals from the platforms.
The last two cover only the **recent activity** the page loaded, so they are not all-time totals.
Connect YouTube first if you want the members tile (see [YouTube](/reference/integrations/youtube)).

## See subscriptions and gifts

**Twitch subscriptions and gifts** lists the newest first.
Each row shows an icon, the name of the person and the time.

| Icon | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Star | A new Twitch subscription. The line reads **Subscribed on Twitch** with the tier. |
| Repeat | A resubscription. The line adds the number of months when it is more than 1. |
| Gift card | A gift. For someone who gives, the line reads **Gifted** with the number of subs and the tier. For someone who gets one, the name is followed by **received as a gift** and **from** the person who gave it. |

A viewer who resubscribes and shares a message can send Twitch two events for one action.
The page shows one row when both come from the same person within a minute.
A gift sent anonymously shows **Anonymous**, and the name has no profile to open.
Choose a name to open that person's profile.

## See cheers

**Cheers** lists each cheer with its **bits** count, newest first.
An anonymous cheer shows **Anonymous**.

## See the top supporters

**Top supporters** ranks the five people who gave the most in the recent activity.
The panel has two lists.

- **Most bits** adds up each person's cheers.
- **Most gifted Twitch subs** adds up the subs each person gifted.

Anonymous givers are left out of the ranking, because the page cannot tell whether two anonymous gifts came from the same person.
A list with nobody to rank stays empty.

## Read tips and memberships

**Tips and memberships** lists the events the bot received from your connected supporter services, newest first.
Each row shows the name, the kind (**Tip**, **Membership**, **Merch** or **Charity**), the amount in the currency the service sent, the tier when there is one, and the time.
The panel loads up to 50 events.

This panel stays empty until you connect a service.
Set one up under **Settings**, then **Supporter sources**, as described on [Supporter sources](/reference/integrations/supporter-sources).
To have the bot thank people when these events arrive, use an [event response](/reference/automation/event-responses).

## Long lists and errors

Each list shows 10 rows at first.
Choose the button at the bottom of a panel to show 10 more.
If a panel cannot load, it says so and offers a retry.
Each panel retries on its own, so one failure does not hide the rest.

## What the page does not show

The Twitch panels read recent activity, not your whole history.
An older subscription or cheer falls out of view as newer ones arrive.
For the full record, use the [event journal](/reference/settings/event-journal).
