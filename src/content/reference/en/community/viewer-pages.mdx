title: Viewer pages
description: See what a viewer finds on their own channel page and account page, and which viewer pages are not built yet.
---

A viewer has their own pages in the dashboard, apart from the pages the streamer manages.
They show the viewer's own data in one channel.

The two chat pages are the exceptions: they show a channel's chat, with the messages of other people.
These are the **Chat** page and the chat of a channel under **More on Twitch**.

Anyone who has signed in may open them.
[Sign in](/reference/getting-started/sign-in) explains how to sign in.

## Find a channel

After sign-in, a viewer lands on **My channels**.
It lists the channels where the viewer was active, with **Live now** or **Last seen** and a date beside each.

1. Choose **Open** beside a channel.
   The channel page **My channel** opens for that channel.
2. To open a channel that is not on the list, use **Find a channel** at the top of the page.
   Type a channel name and pick a row.
   A channel under **Visit a channel** opens that channel's viewer pages.

A channel under **More on Twitch** is not served by this bot.
It opens only its chat.
That chat shows the live lines and lets you send a message, with no history and no moderation.

The first live channel has the filled **Open** button.
The other channels have an outline button.

A viewer who manages channels also sees **Channels you manage**.
Each name there opens the management dashboard of that channel.

When the list is empty, the page shows "You have not watched a channel here yet".

## The seven viewer pages

A row of links under the header holds the seven pages of one channel.
On a phone, the links sit in a bar along the bottom edge, with one-word labels.

| Page | Phone label | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **My channel** | **Home** | Your stats in the channel and your songs in the queue. |
| **Chat** | **Chat** | The channel's chat, as a viewer sees it. |
| **Now playing** | **Music** | "Coming soon". |
| **Leaderboards** | **Ranks** | "Coming soon". |
| **Points and store** | **Store** | "Coming soon". |
| **Games** | **Games** | "Coming soon". |
| **Me** | **Me** | Your account in the channel. |

Four of the seven pages open a **Coming soon** page.
It says the page is planned and not built yet.
The streamer's own leaderboards and games are on [Leaderboards and games](/reference/economy/leaderboards-and-games).

## Your stats on My channel

**My channel** is titled "Your stats in" and the channel name.
**Open chat** at the top opens the **Chat** page.

| Tile | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Messages** | Your chat messages in the channel. |
| **Commands used** | The commands you ran. |
| **Song requests** | Your song requests. Shown only when the channel has song requests on. |
| **Points** | Your points. Shown only when the channel has points on. |
| **Following since** | The date you followed, or "Not following". |
| **Twitch subscription** | Your tier, or "Not subscribed on Twitch". |
| **Channel** | **Live now** or **Offline**. |

**Your songs in the queue** lists only your own song requests.
Each song shows its place, counted from the next song.
The panel footer shows the number of songs in the whole queue.

When the channel takes no requests now, the page shows "Song requests are closed right now."
When the channel has song requests off, the queue panel does not show.

## Your account on Me

**Me** is titled "You in" and the channel name.
A badge shows your standing in that channel.

| Standing | Shown as |
| --- | --- |
| Everyone | **Viewer** |
| Subscriber | **Twitch subscriber** |
| VIP | **VIP** |
| Artist | **Artist** |
| Moderator | **Moderator** |

The page has four panels.

### Pronouns

**Pronouns** are shown beside your name wherever you chat.
Only you change them.

1. Choose a value in **Your pronouns**.
   The choice saves at once.
2. Choose a second value in **Also uses** if you want one.

Choose **Not set** to clear a field.

### Linked accounts

**Linked accounts** lists the sign-ins that lead to this one account.
Each row shows its platform and the date it was linked.
The account you first signed in with carries **Main sign-in** and has no **Unlink** button.

1. Choose **Unlink** beside a linked account.
   A dialog asks to confirm, and warns: "You can no longer sign in with this account."
2. Choose **Unlink** in the dialog.
   The account is removed from the list.

You can link it again later.

### Your voice

**Your voice** is used only when the bot reads your own messages aloud in this channel.
Until you choose one, the panel shows "The channel voice is used for you."

1. Type in **Search voices** to filter the list.
2. Choose **Use this voice** beside a voice.
   The voice now carries the **Your voice** badge.

Choose **Use the channel voice** to go back to the channel voice.

The streamer decides if viewers may choose a voice.
When they may not, the panel shows "The streamer has not turned on voice choice for viewers, or you cannot browse voices here."

### My data

**Open my data** opens the page where you export what the bot holds about you, or ask it to erase it.
That page is [Your data and privacy](/reference/settings/your-data-and-privacy).

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**The page says "This channel is not set up here."**
The channel name in the address does not match a channel that this bot serves.
Choose **Try again**, or pick the channel from **My channels**.

::
