title: People

You browse the people of your channel on the **People** page in the sidebar.
The list holds real people the bot has seen in your chat, and real Twitch data.
Pick a person to open their profile beside the list.

## Who can use this page

A **Moderator**, **Lead moderator**, **Editor** or **Broadcaster** may open the page.
The roles are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).
Some actions on a profile need a higher role (see [Act on a person](#act-on-a-person)).

## Browse the list

1. Open **People** in the sidebar.
   The **All** tab shows everyone the bot has seen in your chat.
2. Choose a tab above the list to see one group.
   The line under **People** shows the count and what the tab holds.
3. Choose **Last seen** or **A to Z** to change the order.
   The page keeps your choice the next time you open it.
4. Scroll down.
   The next part of the list loads when you reach the end.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Everyone the bot has seen in your chat. |
| **Moderators** | People who moderate your channel. |
| **VIPs** | People who hold the VIP badge on Twitch. |
| **Twitch subscribers** | People who subscribe to your channel on Twitch. |
| **Regulars** | People with at least 50 chat messages, first seen 30 days ago or more. |
| **New** | People first seen in the last 90 days, from the part of the list loaded so far. |

The **New** tab shows no count, because it counts only what is loaded.
Scroll to load more people into it.

Each row shows the avatar, the name, and **Last seen** with the time, or **Not seen yet**.

| Mark on a row | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| A gift icon | **Gifted Twitch subscription** |
| A **Banned** badge | The person is banned. |
| A shield icon | **Moderator** |
| A gem icon | **VIP** |

## Find a person

1. Type a name in **Search people**.
   The list changes to the matches, with name, avatar and login.
2. Press Enter to open the first match, or choose a match.
   The profile opens beside the list.

The search looks at every person the bot knows, also from other channels.
A match that your channel has never seen shows **Not in this channel**.
When nothing matches, the list shows "Nobody matches" with your text.

### Look up a name on Twitch

The bot may not know a person yet.
While you type, choose **Look up on Twitch** under the list.
The bot asks Twitch for that exact name and opens the person.

| Result | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Twitch has no account with that name | "Twitch has no account named" with the name. |
| Twitch did not answer | "Twitch could not be asked. Try again." |

## Open a profile

Choose a row to open the person's profile.
On a wide screen it opens beside the list.
On a phone it fills the screen, with **Back to the list** above it.

To close it, choose the close button, press Escape, or go back in the browser.
The address holds the person, so you can share a link that opens them.

The same profile opens when you choose a name elsewhere in the dashboard, in a dialog.
The dialog has **Open full page**, which opens the person on this page.

| Profile tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The person at a glance. |
| **Chat** | Their chat messages. |
| **Emotes** | The emotes their own channel offers. |
| **Moderation** | Notes, history, **Actions taken** counts, and **Ban history**. |
| **Points** | Their points. Shows once the bot has a stored profile for them. |
| **Data** | What the bot stores about them. Shows once the bot has a stored profile for them. |
| **Settings** | How the bot treats them, and their text to speech voice. |

**Ban history** lists each ban with its reason, who banned, the date, and the date it was lifted.
With no bans, it shows "No bans stored for this person."

## Act on a person

The action buttons sit at the bottom of the profile.
You see only the buttons your role may use.
Your own channel's account has no action buttons.

| Button | What it does | Who may use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shout out** | Sends a shoutout for the person. | A moderator and up |
| **Warn** | Asks for a reason, then sends a Twitch warning. The person must accept it before they chat again. | A moderator and up |
| **Timeout** | Opens **Time out for** with four lengths: 1 minute, 10 minutes, 1 hour and 1 day. | A moderator and up |
| **Ban** | Asks you to confirm, then bans the person. | A moderator and up |
| **Unban** | Lifts the ban. Shows in place of **Ban** for a banned person. | A moderator and up |
| **Make VIP** or **Remove VIP** | Gives or takes the VIP badge on Twitch. | The broadcaster |
| **Make moderator** or **Remove moderator** | Gives or takes the moderator role on Twitch. | The broadcaster |

A message at the bottom of the screen confirms each result, such as "is now a moderator."
**Warn**, **Timeout** and **Ban** never show on your own profile.
On a moderator's profile they show only to the broadcaster.
**Make VIP** is grayed out with **VIP status unknown** when Twitch could not tell the bot.

### Ban a person

1. Choose **Ban** on the profile.
   A dialog asks "Ban" with the name, and says the person cannot chat until you unban them.
2. Choose the **Ban** button with the name in the dialog.
   The bot bans the person on Twitch.

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**The profile pane says "Nobody in the loaded lists has the login".**
The person in the link is not in any loaded list.
Type the name in **Search people**, or choose **Look up on Twitch**.

::

::Troubleshooting

**The Twitch subscribers tab is empty.**
It shows "Twitch lists no subscribers for your channel."
The list comes from Twitch, so Twitch has no subscribers for the channel.

::
