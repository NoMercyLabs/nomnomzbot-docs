title: Events
description: Look back through every follow, sub, raid, cheer and reward from your last streams, and replay one for chat and your overlays.

The **Events** page lists everything that happened on your channel during your current stream and the two streams before it.
Use it when the short list on **Home** has scrolled past the thing you want, for example a raid from an hour ago or a gifted sub you missed.
You can also replay an event, so chat, the voice and your overlays show it again.

Open it by choosing **All events** on the **Happening now** panel of **Home**.
The address is `/app/events`.

## Who can use this page

By default, a **Moderator** or higher may open the page, and the broadcaster may let a VIP open it too.
The roles are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).
The replay button needs its own permission, which a **Moderator** has by default (see [Replay an event](#replay-an-event)).

## What the page shows

The page has two cards.
The top card holds the newest stream, and the card below it holds the stream before that.
The card titles follow whether you are live.

| State | Top card | Second card |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You are live | **This stream** | **Last stream** |
| You are offline | **Last stream** | **The stream before** |

Each card lists every event of its stream, with the newest event first.
The bot counts a stream from the moment it started, so a stream that never started has no events on this page.
If the bot has never recorded a stream, both cards stay empty.

An empty top card says **No events yet.**
An empty second card says **No events from an earlier stream.**
If a card cannot load, it says **Events could not load.** and offers a retry.

The page has no search box and no filter.
It shows these streams and nothing older, so use [Analytics](/reference/community/analytics) when you need a longer look back.

## What counts as an event

The list holds channel milestones.
Chat messages are not in it, because those live on the [Chat page](/reference/community/chat-page).

Twitch also repeats a follow notification now and then.
The list shows each follower once, so you never see the same person follow twice.
Cheers, subs, raids and reward redemptions can happen more than once, and each shows as its own row.

Each stream card shows at most 1,000 events.
A normal stream stays well under that.

## Read a row

A row has an icon on the left, a line of text, and the time on the right.
The time is a short weekday and a 24-hour clock time, such as Sat 21:40.
The name in a row opens that person's profile, the same one you open from chat.

The text under the name says what happened.

| Event | What the row says |
| --- | --- |
| Follow | **Followed** |
| New sub | **Subscribed on Twitch · Tier 1** (the tier varies) |
| Resub | **Resubscribed on Twitch**, or the tier and the number of months when the sub is more than one month old |
| Gift subs | **Gifted 5 Twitch subs · Tier 1** (the count and tier vary) |
| Gift received | **Got a Twitch gift sub** |
| Sub ended | **Twitch sub ended · Tier 1**, or **Twitch gift sub ended** for a gifted one |
| Cheer | **Cheered 100 bits** |
| Raid in | The raider's name, **raided you**, and the viewer count |
| Raid out | **You raided** and the channel's name, with how many viewers went along |
| Raid starting | **Raid to** a channel, **starting**, and the viewer count |
| Reward redeemed | **Redeemed** and the reward's name |
| Ban, unban | **Banned**, **Unbanned** |
| Timeout | **Timed out for 10 min** (rounded to whole minutes, at least 1) |
| Moderator added or removed | **Became a moderator**, **Is no longer a moderator** |
| Hype train | **Hype train started**, **Hype train ended** |
| Poll | **Poll started**, **Poll ended** |
| Prediction | **Prediction started**, **Prediction locked**, **Prediction ended** |
| Ad break | **Ad break started** |
| Chat milestones | **First seen by the bot**, **Came back to chat**, **Watch streak**, **Moderator anniversary** |
| Shoutouts | **Shoutout waiting**, **Auto shoutout waiting**, **Shoutout tried**, **Shoutout sent**, **Shouted you out** |

A row for an event the page has no wording for says **Channel event**.
When an event arrives without the detail the page needs, such as a missing tier on a sub, the row falls back to the plain label from the table instead of showing a guess.

## Replay an event

Each row has a **Replay** button on the right.
It shows again what viewers saw at the time: the bot's chat reply, the voice, and the alerts on your overlays.
A gift bomb replays its whole chain, the gifter's line first and then each recipient's, in the original order.

Replay only shows things again.
It does not pay out currency or points, fulfil a reward, change a counter or stat, or touch hype train state, and it does not add a new row to the list.
So you can use it to test an alert, or to show a moment again, without giving anyone anything twice.

Only people who hold the replay permission see the button.
A **Moderator** has it by default.
The broadcaster can change that on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).

After you choose **Replay**, a message tells you the result, for example **Replayed 1 event: 1 chat messages, 0 voice, 1 overlays, 0 widgets.**
The numbers are what was sent out.
Two other messages mean nothing went out.

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Found the event, but there was nothing to show again: no response is set up for it and no alert was saved.** | The event exists, but no chat reply, voice or alert belongs to it. Set one up on [Event responses](/reference/automation/event-responses) first. |
| **Nothing to replay for this event.** | The bot can no longer find the event. |

If something else goes wrong the message is **Could not replay this event.**
Replay is rate limited, so choose it once and wait for the message.

## Where the page gets its data

The page reads the events the bot recorded for your channel.
It shows what the bot saw, so a stream where the bot was offline has gaps.
Your **Home** page reads the same events, limited to the newest stream.
