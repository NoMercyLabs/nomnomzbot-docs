**Your data** lets you inspect your personal data, change consent choices, or request erasure.
It is in the **Account** group of the settings sidebar and acts on the signed-in person, including when that person is a viewer rather than a streamer.
An opt-out keeps your data and records the choices described below; erasure is the irreversible choice.

## Who can use this page

Anyone who is signed in may open the page.
You [sign in](/reference/getting-started/sign-in) as a broadcaster, a moderator or a viewer.
Each action on the page works only on your own data, never on another person's data.
Viewers open the page from **My data** on their Account page, with the **Open my data** button.
The chat commands `!mydata`, `!forgetme` and `!gdpr` do the same download and erasure from chat; a chat erasure always uses the widest scope, **Whole deployment**.
See [Built-in commands](/reference/commands/built-in-commands).

## What the page holds

The page has four panels, in this order.

| Panel | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Download your data** | Saves a JSON export of your profile, recorded chat and history, connected-service details and consents. |
| **Your consents** | Lists what you agreed to. You can withdraw each one. |
| **Your requests** | Lists every download, erasure and opt-out you asked for, newest first. |
| **Erase your data** | Holds two rows: **Opt out of extra processing**, with the **Opt out** button, and **Erase all my data**, with the **Erase my data** button. |

## Download your data

1. Choose **Download my data** at the top of the **Download your data** panel.
   Your browser saves a file named `nomnomzbot-my-data-` with the date and `.json` at the end.
2. Read the line under the button.
   It says how much the file holds, for example "Saved 120 records, 48000 bytes."

The file is JSON, a format a computer can read.
Each download also shows as **Download my data** under **Your requests**.

The export includes at most your 10,000 newest stored chat messages, alongside the other exported records.
It includes connection details such as provider names and permissions, rather than the secret sign-in tokens themselves.
Keep the downloaded file somewhere private, since it contains your personal history.

## Read and withdraw your consents

**Your consents** lists your whole consent record, from every channel and the consents not tied to a channel.
Consents you withdrew stay in the list with the switch off.
The same consent can show more than once, one row per channel, and a row does not say which channel it belongs to.
The switch is on while the consent is given.
Under each name, a line starts with "Legal basis:" and names the basis for that consent.

| Consent | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Terms of service and privacy policy** | Your agreement to the terms and the privacy policy. |
| **I am 18 or older (gambling features)** | Your statement of age, for the gambling features. |
| **Show my pronouns** | Showing your pronouns. |
| **Appear on leaderboards** | Showing you on leaderboards. |
| **Product news and marketing** | Product news and marketing. |

Turn a switch off to withdraw that consent.
The row stays in the list, so you can turn it on again to agree again.
The change saves at once, with no **Save** button.

When you gave no consent yet, the panel says "You have not given any consent yet."

## Opt out of extra processing

An opt-out withdraws saved marketing and leaderboard consents in its scope and marks your existing viewer profiles as opted out of analytics.
Your data stays, and this is not a blanket stop on all recording or ranking.

1. Choose **Opt out** in the **Erase your data** panel.
   The dialog asks "Opt out of extra processing?": "Nothing is deleted."
2. Choose **Opt out** in the dialog.
   The panel shows "Your opt-out is saved."

The opt-out withdraws your **Product news and marketing** and **Appear on leaderboards** consents.
There is a current limitation: currency leaderboards use a separate exclusion setting, which this button and the consent switches do not update.
Do not rely on **Opt out** or withdrawing **Appear on leaderboards** alone to hide you from those rankings; [Leaderboards and games](/reference/economy/leaderboards-and-games#how-the-bot-ranks-viewers) explains that distinction.
The analytics flag excludes marked profiles from the top-viewer ranking and stops the periodic chatter-presence poll from updating them, but it does not erase recorded chat or remove all channel totals.

When you have a channel open, the opt-out is for that channel only.
It turns off only the switches of consents you gave in that channel.
Consents for other channels, and consents not tied to a channel, keep their switches.

The opt-out then shows under **Your requests** as **Opt out of processing** with the scope **One channel**.

When you have no channel open, the opt-out covers every channel.
It withdraws these consents in every channel and marks all your viewer profiles.
It shows with the scope **Whole deployment**.

## Erase your data

Erasing cannot be undone.
It removes or clears the data described below, disables your profile and marks its saved sign-in sessions and renewal tokens as revoked.
It does not physically delete every record associated with you.

1. Choose **Erase my data** in the **Erase your data** panel.
   The bot counts what it would destroy first.
   The dialog "Erase all your data?" lists the counts, for example "3 sessions".
2. Read the list.
   When the bot holds nothing, the list says "The bot holds nothing to destroy."
3. Choose **Erase my data** in the dialog.
   The panel shows "Your erasure is requested. It shows under your requests."

If the bot cannot count your data, the dialog does not open.
The panel shows the reason in red text.

| Items the list can name |
| --- |
| profile, chat messages, records, viewer data rows |
| service accounts, connections |
| sign-in tokens, sessions |
| consents, encryption keys |

An item with a count of zero does not show in the list.
The bot destroys the keys used to unlock the encrypted data included in this erasure.

### What erasure removes and retains

The erasure process replaces your displayed name with **Deleted User** and clears profile details such as pictures, email and pronouns.
It deletes stored chat and history matched to your Twitch identity, and your per-viewer custom data across channels.
It revokes connected-service credentials and withdraws active consents.
The profile row and linked platform identifiers remain; erasure does not delete your account on Twitch, Kick, YouTube or another connected service.

Balances, currency transactions, historical rankings and viewer analytics rows also remain.
The bot replaces their copied Twitch IDs and clears or replaces their copied names, rather than removing those rows or undoing past transactions.
The erasure request and its audit record remain too.

The current chat and history deletion matches your Twitch ID; it does not also look up your linked Kick and YouTube identities.
If you need those histories removed, ask the deployment operator to investigate them separately.
The confirmation's counts describe the categories it checks, rather than a complete count of every retained record.
Erasure also does not prevent new data from being collected if you chat again.
New messages can be stored and the bot can update your name from the incoming chat profile.

Erasure prevents those saved sessions from renewing their sign-in, but it does not immediately invalidate every access token already issued to an open dashboard.
An existing token can remain usable until it expires.
Sign out of your open dashboards yourself as well; erasure is not an immediate sign-out control for every device.

## Your requests

**Your requests** lists each request with its date, its scope and its status.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | The request is not started yet. |
| **Done** | The request is finished. |
| **Failed** | The request did not finish. The reason shows in red under it. |
| **running** | The request is running again after a failure. The row shows this word in lowercase, as the bot writes it. |

A request can also end as cancelled.
That row shows the raw word **cancelled**.

The request label says what you asked for.
A download shows as **Download my data**, an erasure as **Erase my data** and an opt-out as **Opt out of processing**.

| Scope | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Whole deployment** | Every channel on this bot. |
| **This instance** | Comes from a request made on the operator side. |
| **One channel** | Only the channel you had open. |

**Download my data** and **Erase my data** on this page always show as **Whole deployment**.

When you made no request yet, the panel says "You have not made a request yet."

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**A panel shows "Your consents could not be loaded." or "Your requests could not be loaded."**

Choose it to load the list again.
