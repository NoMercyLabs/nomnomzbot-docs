You see and control the data the bot holds about you on the **Your data** page.
It is in the settings sidebar, in the **Account** group.
The page is about you, not about a channel.

## Who can use this page

Anyone who is signed in may open the page.
You [sign in](/reference/getting-started/sign-in) as a broadcaster, a moderator or a viewer.
Each action on the page works only on your own data, never on another person's data.

## What the page holds

The page has four panels, in this order.

| Panel | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Download your data** | Saves one file with everything the bot holds about you. |
| **Your consents** | Lists what you agreed to. You can withdraw each one. |
| **Your requests** | Lists every download, erasure and opt-out you asked for, newest first. |
| **Erase your data** | Holds two rows: **Opt out of extra processing**, with the **Opt out** button, and **Erase all my data**, with the **Erase my data** button. |

## Download your data

1. Choose **Download my data** at the top of the **Download your data** panel.
   Your browser saves a file named `nomnomzbot-my-data-` with the date and `.json` at the end.
2. Read the line under the button.
   It says how much the file holds, for example "Saved 120 records, 48000 bytes."

The file is JSON, a format a computer can read.
Each download also shows as **Download my data** under **Your requests**.

## Read and withdraw your consents

**Your consents** lists each consent you gave, with a switch.
The switch is on while the consent is given.
Under each name, a line starts with "Legal basis:" and names the basis for that consent.

| Consent | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Terms of service and privacy policy** | Your agreement to the terms and the privacy policy. |
| **I am 18 or older (gambling features)** | Your statement of age, for the gambling features. |
| **Show my pronouns** | Showing your pronouns. |
| **Appear on leaderboards** | Showing you on leaderboards. |
| **Product news and marketing** | Product news and marketing. |

Turn a switch off to withdraw that consent.
The row stays in the list, so you can turn it on again to agree again.
The change saves at once, with no **Save** button.

When you gave no consent yet, the panel says "You have not given any consent yet."

## Opt out of extra processing

An opt-out stops the use of your data for marketing, leaderboards and analytics.
Your data stays.

1. Choose **Opt out** in the **Erase your data** panel.
   The dialog asks "Opt out of extra processing?": "Nothing is deleted."
2. Choose **Opt out** in the dialog.
   The panel shows "Your opt-out is saved."

The opt-out withdraws your **Product news and marketing** and **Appear on leaderboards** consents.

When you have a channel open, the opt-out is for that channel only.
It turns off only the switches of consents you gave in that channel.
Consents for other channels, and consents not tied to a channel, keep their switches.

The opt-out then shows under **Your requests** with the scope **One channel**.

## Erase your data

Erasing cannot be undone.
It destroys your personal data and ends your sign-in.

1. Choose **Erase my data** in the **Erase your data** panel.
   The bot counts what it would destroy first.
   The dialog "Erase all your data?" lists the counts, for example "3 sessions".
2. Read the list.
   When the bot holds nothing, the list says "The bot holds nothing to destroy."
3. Choose **Erase my data** in the dialog.
   The panel shows "Your erasure is requested. It shows under your requests."

If the bot cannot count your data, the dialog does not open.
The panel shows the reason in red text.

| Items the list can name |
| --- |
| profile, chat messages, records, viewer data rows |
| service accounts, connections |
| sign-in tokens, sessions |
| consents, encryption keys |

An item with a count of zero does not show in the list.
The bot ends every sign-in you have and destroys the keys that lock your stored data.

## Your requests

**Your requests** lists each request with its date, its scope and its status.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | The request is not started yet. |
| **Done** | The request is finished. |
| **Failed** | The request did not finish. The reason shows in red under it. |

| Scope | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Whole deployment** | Every channel on this bot. |
| **One channel** | Only the channel you had open. |

When you made no request yet, the panel says "You have not made a request yet."

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**A panel shows "Your consents could not be loaded." or "Your requests could not be loaded."**
The panel offers a retry.
Choose it to load the list again.

::
