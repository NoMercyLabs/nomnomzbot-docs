The broadcaster sets who may do what on the **Permissions** page, which is under **Settings** in the sidebar.
The page has three tabs: **Team**, **Actions** and **Per person**.
Each tab shows a count beside its name.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Team** | The people who help run the channel, and the role of each person. |
| **Actions** | Every bot action, grouped by area, with the lowest role that may use it. |
| **Per person** | Dangerous capabilities that one person holds. |

## Who can use this page

The **Broadcaster** sees **Settings** in the sidebar, and only the **Broadcaster** may change anything on this page.
A **Moderator**, **Lead moderator** or **Editor** does not see **Settings** in the sidebar, but may open this page from a direct link.
They see the same lists, with the controls switched off or hidden.

## The role ladder

A role is a rung on one ladder.
Each rung may do everything the rungs below it may do.
The ladder, lowest first:

| Role | Who has it |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone** | Any viewer. |
| **Twitch subscriber** | A subscriber of the channel. |
| **VIP** | A VIP of the channel. |
| **Artist** | A rung between **VIP** and **Moderator**. |
| **Moderator** | A moderator of the channel. |
| **Lead moderator** | A team member the broadcaster gives this role. It may use every action set to **Lead moderator** or lower. |
| **Editor** | A team member the broadcaster gives this role. It may use every action set to **Editor** or lower. |
| **Broadcaster** | The streamer who owns the channel. |

A command, a reward and other features use the same ladder in their own **Who can use it** field.
That field is on [Cooldowns and permissions](/reference/commands/cooldowns-and-permissions).

## Add a person to your team

The **Team** tab shows the **Your team** card: "Who helps run this channel, and the role each person has."

1. Open the **Team** tab.
2. Type a name in the **Person to add** box.
   A list of people the bot knows opens under the box.
3. Choose the person.
   The name replaces the search box.
4. Choose a role in **Role for the new person**: **Moderator**, **Lead moderator** or **Editor**.
   The role starts as **Moderator**.
5. Choose **Add to team**.
   The person shows in the list with the role.

To pick a different person before you add, choose **Pick someone else** (the **X** beside the name).
If the bot refuses the change, red text under the card names the reason.

### Change or remove a team member

Each row has a role picker and a trash button.

- Choose a new role in the row's picker. The change saves at once.
- Choose the trash button to remove the person from the team.

**Role for the new person** does not offer **Broadcaster**.
A row's picker lists **Broadcaster**, but it is switched off.
The owner's row cannot be changed or removed.

## Set the lowest role for an action

The **Actions** tab sets which role may use each bot action in this channel.
The bot ships a default role for every action.

1. Open the **Actions** tab.
   The **Areas** list shows each area with its number of actions, such as **Moderation** or **Music**.
2. Choose an area.
   Its actions open beside the list: "The lowest role that may use each action in this channel."
3. Find the action.
   The actions are in two groups: **Chat and community** and **Channel management**.
4. Choose a role in the action's picker.
   The change saves at once.
   The area row in the list now shows "1 changed".

**Search actions** above the list finds an action by its name or its description in every area at once.
When nothing fits, the list shows "No action matches your search."
On a phone, the area opens over the list, and **All areas** takes you back.

### Protected actions

Some actions carry a **Protected** badge.
A protected action has a lowest allowed role.
The roles under it are switched off in the picker.

### Go back to the default

An action you changed has a reset button at the end of its row.
Choose it to put the action back to the bot's default role.
An action at its default has no reset button.

## Give one person a capability

A capability is one bot action, given to one person.
Some actions cover many kinds of work: **Bot manager** is one capability that lets the person create and edit many kinds of items (see the table below).
The **Per person** tab shows the **Personal capabilities** card: "A dangerous capability goes to one person, never to a whole role."

1. Open the **Per person** tab.
2. Type a name in the **Person to give a capability** box, and choose the person from the list.
3. Choose the action in **Capability**.
   The list is grouped by area.
   Only actions the bot allows for one person are in it.
4. Choose **Give capability**.
   The person shows in the list, with the capability under the name.

**OBS control** and **Bot manager** ask you to confirm first.
The dialog names the person and the capability, and lists what the person can and cannot do.
Choose **Give it** to give it.

| Capability | The person can | The person cannot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **OBS control** | Switch scenes and turn sources on and off in your OBS. | Start or stop your broadcast, or see your OBS password. |
| **Bot manager** | Create and edit commands, timers, pipelines, event responses, triggers, widgets, sounds, quotes, and voice and music setup. | Moderate chat, change channel settings, control OBS, or manage roles. |

When nobody holds a capability, the card shows "Nobody has a personal capability."
To take a capability back, choose the trash button on the person's row.
