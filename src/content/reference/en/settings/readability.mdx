You change how text and controls look in the dashboard on the **Readability** page.
You open it from the account menu under your name (see [Dashboard tour](/reference/getting-started/dashboard-tour)).
The page card is called **Text and display**.

## Who can use this page

Anyone who is signed in may open the page.
The options belong to you, not to a channel.
They are saved in this browser on this device, so another device keeps its own options.

## Open the page

You can open the page from three places.

| Surface | Who sees it | What you choose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The account menu under your name | Anyone who is signed in | **Readability** |
| The **Display** panel in chat | Anyone on the chat page | **Change**, next to **Font and readability** |
| The settings sidebar, group **You** | People who may change the channel setup, who open **Settings** from the main sidebar | **Readability** |

## Change an option

1. Open **Readability**.
   The page shows a preset choice, a text sample and the options under it.
2. Choose a value for an option.
   The change applies at once on every page, and the sample shows it.
3. Leave the page.
   The page says: "Changes apply at once and are saved on this device."

There is no save button.

## Start from a preset

A preset under **Start from a preset** sets all the options at once.
You can change any option after you choose a preset.
When your options no longer match a preset, no preset is selected.

| Preset | Font | Text size | Line spacing | Letter and word spacing | High contrast | Reduce motion | Size of controls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Default** | **Inter** | 14 px | **Normal** | **Normal** | Off | Off | **Compact** |
| **Dyslexia-friendly** | **Lexend** | 16 px | **Wide** | **Wide** | Off | On | **Comfortable** |
| **High contrast** | **Inter** | 16 px | **Normal** | **Normal** | On | Off | **Comfortable** |

## The options

| Option | Group | Choices | What it changes | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Font** | **Text** | **Inter**, **Legible**, **Lexend** | The font of the dashboard. **Legible** is Atkinson Hyperlegible. | **Inter** |
| **Text size** | **Text** | A slider from 12 px to 20 px, in steps of 1 px | Scales all text on every page. | 14 px |
| **Line spacing** | **Text** | **Normal**, **Wide**, **Wider** | The space between lines of text. | **Normal** |
| **Letter and word spacing** | **Text** | **Normal**, **Wide** | The space between letters and words. **Wide** follows WCAG 1.4.12 text spacing. | **Normal** |
| **High contrast** | **Contrast and motion** | A switch | Quiet text becomes full strength and edges get stronger. | Off |
| **Reduce motion** | **Contrast and motion** | A switch | Off follows the setting of your computer or phone. On reduces motion everywhere. | Off |
| **Size of controls** | **Density** | **Compact**, **Comfortable**, **Touch** | **Compact** fits the most on a desktop. **Touch** makes every button, tab and row at least 44 px tall. | **Compact** |

Each option also shows its default under its name, for example "Default: Inter."

::Callout

These options change the dashboard pages only.
Chat has its own layout options in its **Display** panel.

::
