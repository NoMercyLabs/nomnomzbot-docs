You connect your channel's own bot account on the **White-label bot** page.
Open **Settings** in the sidebar, then **White-label bot** in the **Bot** group.
Which account the bot types from, and why you do not need a bot account, is on [Bot account](/reference/getting-started/bot-account).

## Who can use this page

The broadcaster may open the page.
Only the broadcaster may connect, change or disconnect the bot account.

The broadcaster may also let other roles open the page on the **Permissions** settings page.
They then see which account the channel uses, and no buttons.

## Read the status

The card **Your channel's own bot** shows which account chats for your channel.

| Badge | What the card shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | Your own bot account: its name, its `@` handle and its picture. |
| **Shared bot** | "Your channel uses the shared bot" with the shared bot's name, or **Shared bot** when it has no name. |

When the status cannot load, the card shows "The bot account could not be loaded." and lets you try again.

## Connect your own bot

Use a Twitch account that you made for the bot.
Sign in to Twitch as that account, not as yourself.
Use a private window if you are signed in to Twitch as yourself.

1. Open the **White-label bot** page and choose **Connect your own bot**.
   The card shows "Starting the sign-in..." and then a sign-in code.
2. Choose **Open** followed by the Twitch site name.
   The Twitch page opens in a new tab.
3. Sign in there as the bot account and type the code.
   The card shows "Waiting for you to approve the code..."
4. Approve the code on Twitch.
   A message pops up: "Your bot account is connected."
   The badge changes to **Connected**.

Viewers now see your bot's name and picture in chat.
The bot still speaks in the channel's one bot voice, now from your own bot account.

Choose **Cancel** to stop the sign-in.
The button shows next to the code, and under a sign-in that failed.

<details>
<summary>Sign in with the browser instead of the code</summary>

Under the code, the card shows "Can't use the code? Sign in on the Twitch page in this browser instead."
Choose **Sign in with the browser**.
The Twitch sign-in page opens in a new tab.

</details>

## Use another bot account

1. On the **White-label bot** page, find the row **Use another account**.
2. Choose **Change account**.
   The same sign-in code steps start as in [Connect your own bot](#connect-your-own-bot).
3. Approve the code as the new bot account.
   The new account replaces the current one.

## Go back to the shared bot

1. On the **White-label bot** page, find the row **Go back to the shared bot**.
2. Choose **Disconnect**.
   A dialog asks "Disconnect" followed by the bot's name.
3. Choose **Disconnect** in the dialog.
   A message pops up: "Your bot account is disconnected. The shared bot speaks again."

The shared bot answers commands and events in your channel again.
The Twitch account itself stays as it is.
You can connect it again at any time.

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**The card shows "The code ran out of time. Start again to get a new one."**
The code was not approved in time.
Choose **Try again** for a new code.

::

::Troubleshooting

**The card shows "The sign-in was denied on Twitch."**
The approval was refused on Twitch.
Choose **Try again** and approve the code.

::

When the sign-in stops for another reason, the card shows "The sign-in did not finish. Try again." and a **Try again** button.
