You manage the bot's place in your channel on the **Channel management** page in the settings sidebar.
Here you let the bot join or leave, reset the settings, and delete the channel.
A deleted channel can be brought back for a while.

| Card | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **The bot in your channel** | Shows whether the bot is in the channel, and lets it join or leave. |
| **Reset the channel settings** | Puts the settings of this channel back to the defaults. |
| **Delete this channel** | Removes the channel and everything in it from the bot. |

## Who can use this page

The **Broadcaster** may open the page and use every button on it.
The roles are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).
The way around the dashboard is on the [Dashboard tour](/reference/getting-started/dashboard-tour).

## Let the bot join or leave

The card **The bot in your channel** shows one of two states.

| State | Text under it | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The bot is in your channel** | "It joined on" and the date and time. | **Make the bot leave** |
| **The bot is not in your channel** | "It does not answer chat or follow events until you let it join." | **Let the bot join** |

To let the bot join, choose **Let the bot join**.
The state changes to **The bot is in your channel**.

To make the bot leave:

1. Choose **Make the bot leave**.
   The dialog **Make the bot leave your channel?** opens.
2. Read the two results it lists.
   The bot stops answering commands in your chat.
   The bot stops listening to your channel events, so event responses do not run.
3. Choose **Make the bot leave**.
   The state changes to **The bot is not in your channel**.

You can let the bot join again at any time.
Your settings stay as they are.

## Reset the channel settings

A reset clears the stored settings of the channel.
The channel keeps its commands, viewers and history.
A reset cannot be undone.

1. Choose **Reset settings** on the card **Reset the channel settings**.
   The dialog **Reset the settings of** your channel name opens.
2. Read the list of what the reset clears, with a count for each kind.
3. Type the channel name in **Type the channel name to confirm**.
   The confirm button stays off until the name matches exactly.
4. Choose **Reset settings**.
   The settings go back to the defaults.

| Kind in the list | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **chat settings** | The chat settings of the channel. |
| **text-to-speech settings** | The text to speech settings. |
| **moderation settings** | The moderation settings. |
| **music settings** | The music settings. |
| **raid settings** | The raid settings. |
| **saved keys and sign-ins** | Saved keys and sign-ins. |
| **other settings** | Every other stored setting. |

When nothing is stored, the list shows "No settings are stored. A reset changes nothing."

## Delete the channel

A delete removes the channel and everything in it from the bot.
The bot stops serving the channel at once.
You can bring the channel back for 30 days.

1. Choose **Delete channel** on the card **Delete this channel**.
   The dialog **Delete** your channel name opens.
2. Read the list of what the delete removes.
   Each line is a count and a kind, such as commands, timers or widgets.
3. Read the lines about what stops outside the bot.
   Each line starts with a count, such as "2 overlay sources in OBS go blank".
4. Type the channel name in **Type the channel name to confirm**.
5. Choose **Delete channel**.
   The card now shows **The channel is deleted** with the date until which you can bring it back.

When nothing else depends on the channel, the list shows "Nothing else depends on this channel."
Some lists end with "These numbers are a minimum. Some links can only be counted when they run."

<details>
<summary>What stops outside the bot</summary>

| Line in the dialog, after the count |
| --- |
| channel point rewards on Twitch stop working |
| overlay sources in OBS go blank |
| Twitch event subscriptions are removed |
| Discord notifications stop |
| outbound webhooks stop sending |
| connected accounts are disconnected |

Each line starts with a count.
The dialog shows a line only when its count is above zero.

</details>

## Bring a deleted channel back

After a delete, the card **Delete this channel** shows the deleted channel.
It says "You can bring it back until" and the date.
After that date, the channel is gone for good.

1. Choose **Bring the channel back**.
2. The channel and everything in it come back.

The card also lists the other channels you own that are deleted and can still come back.
Each one has its own **Bring the channel back** button.
Only the owner of a channel sees it in this list.

::Troubleshooting

**Bring the channel back shows an error.**
The 30 days are over.
The channel cannot come back.

::
