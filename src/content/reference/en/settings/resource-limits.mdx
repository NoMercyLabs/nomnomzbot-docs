---
title: Resource limits
description: Read the limits that apply to your channel, how much of each you use, and what the bot does when you reach one.
---

Resource limits explain why the bot may accept one upload or new command and refuse the next.
The **Resource limits** page shows what the channel can use and how much is already in use, so you can make room before a limit interrupts your setup.
You can read the limits here, but you change the items that use them on their own pages.

The settings sidebar is described on the [dashboard tour](/reference/getting-started/dashboard-tour).

## Who can use this page

Only the broadcaster may open the page.

## What the page shows

The page has three cards, from top to bottom.

| Card | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan** | "The plan that sets your limits." |
| **Limits** | "How much of each limited resource this channel uses right now." |
| **Usage this period** | Metered use in the current period, with its start and end dates. |

## Plan

The **Plan** card has three lines.

| Line | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan** | The name of the channel's plan. Without a name, it shows **Your plan**. |
| **Custom bot name** | **Included** or **Not included**. |
| **Priority support** | **Included** or **Not included**. |

The **Change plan** button opens the [Billing](/reference/settings/billing) page.

Running it for yourself, your friends and your community is free. You may not earn money with it: no selling it, and no paid hosting or paid service built on it.

## Limits

Read the kind of limit before comparing the numbers.
Storage adds up across files, a monthly allowance adds up over a period, and a per-message cap applies separately to each message.
Shortening one text-to-speech message can resolve a per-message refusal; deleting a sound clip affects storage instead.

The **Limits** card puts each resource in one of four groups.
A group with no resource in it does not show.

| Group | What it means on the page | Resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Storage** | "Disk space for your uploads. Deleting a file frees its space at once." | **Sound clip storage**, **Channel asset storage** |
| **Monthly allowance** | "Time and characters you can use each month. It starts again every period." | **Script run time per month**, **TTS characters this month** |
| **Per message** | "The longest a single message can be. It is a cap, not a total." | **Length of one TTS line** |
| **Abuse protection** | "The same generous limit for every channel. It only stops runaway use." | **Custom commands**, **Timers** |

### Read a meter

Each resource outside **Per message** has a row with its name and the amount used.
A resource with a limit also has a bar.
The amount reads "used of limit", for example "3 of 200".
Storage shows as a file size, and script time as a length of time.

| How full | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Under 80% | A muted bar. |
| 80% or more | A darker bar. |
| At or over the limit | A red bar and red figures. |
| No limit | The amount and "no limit", with no bar. |
| Not included | The resource is excluded, rather than a positive allowance you have used up. |

A **Per message** row has no bar.
It shows the cap as a number of characters per message, or **No cap**.
The maximum length in your text-to-speech settings also applies, with 500 characters as the default.
The stricter of the two wins, so **No cap** here does not mean a line has no length limit.
A viewer's message that is too long is refused. A bot reply that is too long is read out shortened.

### Abuse protection limits

Changing plans does not raise these safety baselines.
Each row also shows "Safety limit for every channel:" and the value.

| Resource | Safety limit |
| --- | --- |
| **Custom commands** | 1,500 |
| **Timers** | 200 |

Storage and the monthly allowance normally come from the plan.
On a bot you host yourself, those defaults have no limit.

The installation's operator can apply a channel-specific override, including an override to a safety limit.
That override takes precedence while it is active, so the amount shown and enforced for your channel can differ from the defaults above.
Use the channel's displayed limit when checking a refusal; changing plans does not remove an operator override.

## Usage this period

The **Usage this period** card lists metered use in the current period that is not already a limit.
Its description shows the start date and the end date of the period, for example "Nov 1, 2026 to Dec 1, 2026".
A resource already shown under **Limits** is not shown here again.

The monthly TTS characters and the script run time already show under **Limits**.
So the card normally shows "Nothing is metered yet this period."

## What happens at a limit

The bot refuses the next save that would go over the limit.
Nothing is made, and the page where you saved shows the reason.

| You try to | The reason reads |
| --- | --- |
| Make a command past the limit | "Your plan allows up to 1500 custom commands. Upgrade your tier or remove one first." |
| Make a timer past the limit | "Your plan allows up to 200 timers. Upgrade your tier or remove one first." |
| Add a response variation past the limit | "Your plan allows up to 100 response variations per command. Upgrade your tier or remove one first." |
| Add a message variation to a timer past the limit | "Your plan allows up to 100 message variations per timer. Upgrade your tier or remove one first." |
| Use text-to-speech after the monthly allowance is spent | "This channel has used its TTS characters for this month." |
| Upload a sound clip past the storage limit | "This upload would use … MB of your … MB sound clip storage limit." |
| Upload a channel asset past the storage limit | "This upload would use … MB of your … MB channel asset storage limit." |

The command and timer reasons say "your plan", but their limits are the abuse protection limits.
A different plan does not raise them.
The limit of 100 variations per command or per timer applies too, but it does not show on this page.

A command or a timer that already exists keeps working.
To make room, delete a command, a timer or a file.
A deleted file frees its storage at once.

Deleting an upload does not restore a monthly allowance already spent.
The monthly TTS total counts accepted text over the period, separately from the maximum length of one line.
Text-to-speech with your own provider key does not count toward the monthly total.
If scripts are excluded, the page shows **Not included** rather than a running-time allowance you can use.

Some features have their own switch on [Features](/reference/settings/features).

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**A card shows an error.**
The card shows "The limits could not be loaded.", "The plan could not be loaded." or "The usage could not be loaded."
Choose **Try again** on that card to load it again.

::
