---
title: Resource limits
description: Read the limits that apply to your channel, how much of each you use, and what the bot does when you reach one.
---

You read the channel's limits on the **Resource limits** page in the settings sidebar.
The page shows each limited resource, how much of it the channel uses, and the plan behind the limits.
The page only shows limits; you cannot change them here.

The settings sidebar is described on the [dashboard tour](/reference/getting-started/dashboard-tour).

## Who can use this page

Only the broadcaster may open the page.

## What the page shows

The page has three cards, from top to bottom.

| Card | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan** | "The plan that sets your limits." |
| **Limits** | "How much of each limited resource this channel uses right now." |
| **Usage this period** | Metered use in the current period, with its start and end dates. |

## Plan

The **Plan** card has three lines.

| Line | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan** | The name of the channel's plan. Without a name, it shows **Your plan**. |
| **Custom bot name** | **Included** or **Not included**. |
| **Priority support** | **Included** or **Not included**. |

The **Change plan** button opens the [Billing](/reference/settings/billing) page.

Running it for yourself, your friends and your community is free. You may not earn money with it: no selling it, and no paid hosting or paid service built on it.

## Limits

The **Limits** card puts each resource in one of four groups.
A group with no resource in it does not show.

| Group | What it means on the page | Resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Storage** | "Disk space for your uploads. Deleting a file frees its space at once." | **Sound clip storage**, **Channel asset storage** |
| **Monthly allowance** | "Time you can use each month. It starts again every period." | **Script run time per month** |
| **Per message** | "The longest a single message can be. It is a cap, not a total." | **Text-to-speech message length** |
| **Abuse protection** | "The same generous limit for every channel. It only stops runaway use." | **Custom commands**, **Timers** |

### Read a meter

Each resource outside **Per message** has a row with its name and the amount used.
A resource with a limit also has a bar.
The amount reads "used of limit", for example "3 of 200".
Storage shows as a file size, and script time as a length of time.

| How full | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Under 80% | A muted bar. |
| 80% or more | A darker bar. |
| At or over the limit | A red bar and red figures. |
| No limit | The amount and "no limit", with no bar. |

A **Per message** row has no bar.
It shows the cap as a number of characters per message, or **No cap**.

### Abuse protection limits

These limits do not change with the plan.
Each row also shows "Safety limit for every channel:" and the value.

| Resource | Safety limit |
| --- | --- |
| **Custom commands** | 1,500 |
| **Timers** | 200 |

Storage and the monthly allowance come from the plan.
On a bot you host yourself, they have no limit.

## Usage this period

The **Usage this period** card lists metered use in the current period, such as **Text-to-speech characters**.
Its description shows the first and last day of the period.
A resource already shown under **Limits** is not shown here again.

When nothing is metered yet, the card shows "Nothing is metered yet this period."

## What happens at a limit

The bot refuses the next save that would go over the limit.
Nothing is made, and the page where you saved shows the reason.

| You try to | The reason reads |
| --- | --- |
| Make a command past the limit | "Your plan allows up to 1500 custom commands. Upgrade your tier or remove one first." |
| Make a timer past the limit | "Your plan allows up to 200 timers. Upgrade your tier or remove one first." |
| Upload a sound clip past the storage limit | "This upload would use … MB of your … MB sound clip storage limit." |
| Upload a channel asset past the storage limit | "This upload would use … MB of your … MB channel asset storage limit." |

The command and timer reasons name your plan.
Their limits are the abuse protection limits.
A different plan does not raise them.

A command or a timer that already exists keeps working.
To make room, delete a command, a timer or a file.
A deleted file frees its storage at once.

Some features have their own switch on [Features](/reference/settings/features).

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**A card shows an error.**
The card shows "The limits could not be loaded.", "The plan could not be loaded." or "The usage could not be loaded."
Choose **Try again** on that card to load it again.

::
