# Reference

This section has no area pages yet.

The area pages are generated from the bot's capability ledger.
The ledger lists what the bot can do.
Each page will be written from that list, so no page can describe something the bot does not do.

When the generator runs, the pages appear in the sidebar.
Until then, read the [home page](/) for a short overview of what NomNomzBot is.
