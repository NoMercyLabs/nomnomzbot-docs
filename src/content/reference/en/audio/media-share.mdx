---
title: Media share
description: Let viewers share Twitch clips and YouTube videos to the stream, and approve, order, skip and play what they share.
---

Media share lets viewers contribute Twitch clips and YouTube videos for you to watch together on stream.
The **Media share** page keeps those submissions in a queue, where a moderator can approve them and decide what plays next.
Approval starts enabled for ordinary viewer submissions. A moderator or higher bot role using `!media`, or anyone allowed to use `!play`, submits without that review step.

Playback happens in the **Media share** browser source in OBS.
Add it from **Required overlays** on the **OBS** page, using the [browser-source setup](/reference/overlays/obs-browser-source), before opening submissions.
The dashboard controls the player, but the source is what your viewers see and hear.

## Who can use this page

Four permissions control this page. All four default to Moderator.

| Permission | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **See shared media** | Opens the page. |
| **Approve or skip shared media** | **Approve**, **Reject**, **Skip**, **Remove**, **Move up** and **Move down**. |
| **Change the media share settings** | The settings panel. |
| **Create and change pipelines** | The **On stream** player, and the **Play now** and **Replay** buttons. |

The broadcaster can change who holds each of these on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).
Someone who may open the page but not change the settings sees the settings, but cannot change them.
**Play now** and **Replay** also show for someone who has **Approve or skip shared media**, but the bot refuses them unless they also have **Create and change pipelines**.

## Turn media share on

Media share is off until you turn it on.
Read this before you set **Cost per item (points)**.
A plain Twitch clip link in chat is charged the same **Cost per item** as `!media`.
A refund pays the channel's **Cost per item** at the moment of the refund, not the amount the viewer paid.

1. Open **Media share** in the sidebar and choose **Settings**.
   The settings panel opens at the side of the page.
2. Turn on **Accept media from viewers**.
3. Change any other setting from the table below, or keep the defaults.
4. Choose **Save settings**.
   The values stay in the panel, and viewers can now share media.

**Discard** puts back the saved values before you save.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Accept media from viewers** | Turns media share on. When it is off, the bot refuses every shared item. | Off |
| **Approve each item before it plays** | Ordinary submissions wait for review. Moderator-or-higher `!media` submissions and `!play` bypass review. When off, all accepted submissions enter the play order directly. | On |
| **Allow Twitch clips** | Accepts Twitch clip links. | On |
| **Allow YouTube videos** | Accepts YouTube video links, including Shorts. | On |
| **Twitch subscribers only** | Only Twitch subscribers can share media. Everyone else is turned away. | Off |
| **Longest item (seconds)** | The bot refuses a clip or video that is longer than this. It must be at least 1. | 180 seconds |
| **Cost per item (points)** | The channel currency a viewer pays to share one item. 0 means free. | 0 (free) |
| **Longest queue** | The most items that can wait, play or be up next at the same time. It must be at least 1. | 20 |
| **Wait between items per viewer (seconds)** | How long one viewer waits before they can share the next item. 0 means no wait. | 60 seconds |

The cost is paid in the channel currency, explained on [Currency and balances](/reference/economy/currency-and-balances).
When a charged item is rejected, removed or skipped, the bot attempts to refund the viewer.

When **Cost per item** is 0 at the moment of the refund, the refund is 0.
The reject, remove or skip always goes through, even when the refund does not.
The bot tries the refund once and does not try again later.
The dashboard shows no message when a refund fails, so check the viewer's balance if you charge for items.

## Try the whole flow with one video

After turning media share on above, leave **Cost per item (points)** at 0 and keep approval enabled for this first check.
Ask a viewer below moderator to send `!media` followed by a Twitch clip or YouTube video link in Twitch chat.
Test while the channel is live, or turn on **Keep commands and rewards on while offline** in [Bot basics and personality](/reference/settings/bot-basics-and-personality).
Offline, a viewer's command does nothing, so the test gives no item and no reply. Moderators and the broadcaster still get an answer offline.
Their submission appears under **Waiting for approval**. Your own `!media` submission is approved automatically, so it does not test that review step.

Choose **Approve** to move it into the play order. If nothing is playing and the enabled Media share browser source is connected, playback starts automatically.
Otherwise, make sure OBS is running with the **Media share** browser source, then use **Play** in **On stream** to start the first approved item.
Check the OBS preview and audio mixer, then try **Pause** and **Stop playback** so you know where the controls are before viewers submit more items.
Once that works, adjust the duration, queue and per-viewer limits for your stream.

## How viewers share media

A viewer can explicitly submit a link with the command below, or through a reward or pipeline you prepare.
Use Twitch chat for the command workflows described here. `!media` and `!play` are reliable only in Twitch chat. On Kick or YouTube, the name shown as the viewer, the subscriber check and charging points are not reliable.

| Way | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Chat command | The viewer types `!media` and the link, for example `!media https://clips.twitch.tv/...`. |
| Moderator command | Type `!play` and a link to submit it approved. In Twitch chat, you can also reply `!play` to a message containing the link. |
| Channel-point reward or other pipeline | A pipeline with a **Submit media** step shares the link for the viewer who started it. |

The bot also notices the first Twitch clip link in an ordinary chat message and submits it through the same rules.
That background submission is silent: a refused clip produces no chat reply.
YouTube links need an explicit submission; they are not collected by this passive scan.
The passive scan and **Submit media** pipeline step follow the approval setting even when the person submitting is a moderator; the command bypass described above is specific to `!media` and `!play`.

The `!media` command is a built-in command, open to everyone by default.
You change its replies and who may use it on [Built-in commands](/reference/commands/built-in-commands).

`!play` requires **Moderator** by default. On Twitch it credits the person running the command, including when they reply to another viewer's message.
It submits an item to the approved play order; it does not interrupt the current item. Use **Play now** in the dashboard when you want to interrupt it.
Both commands still check whether media share is enabled, which sources and viewers are allowed, the duration, per-viewer wait, queue length and cost.
If you lower `!play`'s required role, everyone you admit can submit without review.

To share from a [channel-point reward](/reference/automation/rewards), add a **Submit media** step to the reward's [pipeline](/reference/commands/pipelines).
Put the link in its URL field.
The field takes [template variables](/reference/commands/variables-and-templates), so it can hold the text the viewer typed.

### What the bot answers

While the channel is live, or while commands are kept active offline, the bot replies in chat to every `!media` message.
Offline, it answers only moderators and the broadcaster.

| Result | What the bot says |
| --- | --- |
| No link after `!media` | How to use the command. |
| Approval is on and the submission needs review | The item is shared and a moderator reviews it. |
| Approval is off, or this command submission bypasses review | The item is added to the play order and may start automatically. |
| Media share is off | Media share is not on in this channel. |
| The link is not an allowed Twitch clip or YouTube video | Only the allowed clips and videos can be queued. |
| The clip or video does not exist | It could not find it. |
| The item is longer than **Longest item (seconds)** | The clip is too long. |
| The viewer is not a subscriber while **Twitch subscribers only** is on | The viewer cannot share media now. |
| The viewer shares again within the wait | The viewer must wait before the next item. |
| The queue is at **Longest queue** | The queue is full. |
| The bot cannot look up the link right now | Try again in a moment. |
| Any other failure, such as too few points | The item could not be shared. |

A refused item costs the viewer nothing.

## Work the queue

The **Queue** card lists the shared items in three sections.
A section with no items is not shown.
The card heading shows how many items still wait, are up next or play.

| Section | Items in it | Actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Waiting for approval** | Items a moderator has not decided on yet. | **Approve**, **Reject** |
| **Up next** | Approved items, in play order. | **Play now**, **Skip**, **Remove**, and **Move up** or **Move down** in **More actions** |
| **History** | Items that played, were rejected or were skipped. | Played or skipped: **Replay**, **Play now**, **Remove**. Rejected: no actions. |

Each row shows the title, "Shared by" and the viewer's name, the length and the source.
An item in **Up next** also shows its place in the play order.
When nothing was shared yet, the card shows "No media has been shared yet."

### Approve or reject an item

Choose **Approve** on an item in **Waiting for approval**.
The item moves to the end of **Up next**.
When nothing is playing and the enabled browser source is connected, the first approved item starts automatically.

Choose **Reject** to turn the item down.
The item moves to **History**, and the bot requests the refund if the viewer paid.

### Change the play order

Open **More actions** on an item in **Up next**, then choose **Move up** or **Move down**.
The item changes place by one, and the positions of the other items follow.

Choose **Play now** to play an item at once.
The item moves to the top of **Up next**.

When nothing plays, the player starts with this item.
When something plays, the bot skips it and plays this item.

The skipped item moves to **History** as skipped, with a refund attempted if its viewer paid.
**Play now** is also available on the first item in **Up next**.

### Play something from history again

Choose **Replay** on a played or skipped item to put it at the front of **Up next**.
The current item keeps playing; if nothing is playing, the replay starts.
Choose **Play now** to interrupt the current item and start the selected historical item instead.

Replaying does not charge the viewer again. **Remove** marks a played or skipped item as rejected without issuing another refund, and removes its replay controls; it does not erase the history row.

### Skip or remove an item

Choose **Skip** on an item in **Up next**.
The item moves to **History** as skipped, and the bot attempts the refund if the viewer paid.

Choose **Remove** to take an item out of the queue.
The dialog **Remove this item?** shows the title.
Choose **Remove** in the dialog: the item moves to **History** as rejected, with a refund attempted if the viewer paid.

## Play items on stream

The **On stream** card at the top of the page shows what the media share overlay plays now.
It shows the title, the viewer who shared it, **Playing** or **Paused**, and the time in the item.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Play** | Starts the first item in **Up next**, or goes on with a paused item. |
| **Pause** | Pauses the item on stream. |
| **Skip to the next item** | Skips the current item, attempts any applicable refund and starts the next one. |
| **Stop playback** | Ends the item that plays and moves it to **History** as played. The next item does not start. |
| **Place in the item** | Moves to another point in the item. |
| **Overlay volume** | How loud the overlay plays, from 0 to 100. |

When an item ends on the overlay, it moves to **History** as played and the next item starts.
When the overlay cannot play an item, the bot moves it to **History** as skipped, attempts the refund if the viewer paid, and starts the next item.
When nothing is approved, the card shows "Approve an item to play it on stream."
The player needs the **Create and change pipelines** permission, which a moderator and every role above it hold by default.

A **Media playback** step in a [pipeline](/reference/commands/pipelines) runs the same controls.
It can also play a finished item again, or play an approved item at once.
The [Pipelines](/reference/commands/pipelines) page explains how to set up a step.

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**The On stream card says "The Media share browser source is not loaded in OBS, so nothing plays on stream."**
The media share overlay is not in OBS.
Choose **Set up in OBS** and add the overlay as a browser source, as [OBS browser source](/reference/overlays/obs-browser-source) explains.

::
