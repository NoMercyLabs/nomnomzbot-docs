---
title: Media share
description: Let viewers share Twitch clips and YouTube videos to the stream, and approve, order, skip and play what they share.
---

You run media share on the **Media share** page in the sidebar.
Viewers share a Twitch clip or a YouTube video, and the bot puts it in a queue.
You approve each item and set the play order.

The media share overlay plays the items on stream.

The overlay plays only when its browser source is loaded in OBS.
The setup is on [OBS browser source](/reference/overlays/obs-browser-source).

## Who can use this page

By default a moderator and every role above it may open the page, work the queue, change the settings and use the player.
The broadcaster can change who may do each of these on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).
Someone who may open the page but not change the settings sees the settings, but cannot change them.

## Turn media share on

Media share is off until you turn it on.

1. Open **Media share** in the sidebar and choose **Settings**.
   The settings panel opens at the side of the page.
2. Turn on **Accept media from viewers**.
3. Change any other setting from the table below, or keep the defaults.
4. Choose **Save settings**.
   The values stay in the panel, and viewers can now share media.

**Discard** puts back the saved values before you save.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Accept media from viewers** | Turns media share on. When it is off, the bot refuses every shared item. | Off |
| **Approve each item before it plays** | A shared item waits for a moderator before it can play. When it is off, a shared item goes straight to the play order. | On |
| **Allow Twitch clips** | Accepts Twitch clip links. | On |
| **Allow YouTube videos** | Accepts YouTube video links, including Shorts. | On |
| **Twitch subscribers only** | Only Twitch subscribers can share media. Everyone else is turned away. | Off |
| **Longest item (seconds)** | The bot refuses a clip or video that is longer than this. It must be at least 1. | 180 seconds |
| **Cost per item (points)** | The channel currency a viewer pays to share one item. 0 means free. | 0 (free) |
| **Longest queue** | The most items that can wait, play or be up next at the same time. It must be at least 1. | 20 |
| **Wait between items per viewer (seconds)** | How long one viewer waits before they can share the next item. 0 means no wait. | 60 seconds |

The cost is paid in the channel currency, explained on [Currency and balances](/reference/economy/currency-and-balances).
When an item the viewer paid for is rejected, removed or skipped, the bot gives the viewer a refund.

The refund is the channel's **Cost per item** at the moment of the refund, not the amount the viewer paid.
When **Cost per item** is 0 by then, the refund is 0.

## How viewers share media

A viewer shares an item in two ways.

| Way | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Chat command | The viewer types `!media` and the link, for example `!media https://clips.twitch.tv/...`. |
| Channel-point reward or other pipeline | A pipeline with a **Submit media** step shares the link for the viewer who started it. |

The `!media` command is a built-in command, open to everyone by default.
You change its replies and who may use it on [Built-in commands](/reference/commands/built-in-commands).

To share from a [channel-point reward](/reference/automation/rewards), add a **Submit media** step to the reward's [pipeline](/reference/commands/pipelines).
Put the link in its URL field.
The field takes [template variables](/reference/commands/variables-and-templates), so it can hold the text the viewer typed.

### What the bot answers

The bot replies in chat to every `!media` message.

| Result | What the bot says |
| --- | --- |
| No link after `!media` | How to use the command. |
| Approval is on | The item is shared and a moderator reviews it. |
| Approval is off | The item is added to the queue. |
| Media share is off | Media share is not on in this channel. |
| The link is not an allowed Twitch clip or YouTube video | Only the allowed clips and videos can be queued. |
| The clip or video does not exist | It could not find it. |
| The item is longer than **Longest item (seconds)** | The clip is too long. |
| The viewer is not a subscriber while **Twitch subscribers only** is on | The viewer cannot share media now. |
| The viewer shares again within the wait | The viewer must wait before the next item. |
| The queue is at **Longest queue** | The queue is full. |
| The bot cannot look up the link right now | Try again in a moment. |
| Any other failure, such as too few points | The item could not be shared. |

A refused item costs the viewer nothing.

## Work the queue

The **Queue** card lists the shared items in three sections.
A section with no items is not shown.
The card heading shows how many items still wait, are up next or play.

| Section | Items in it | Actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Waiting for approval** | Items a moderator has not decided on yet. | **Approve**, **Reject** |
| **Up next** | Approved items, in play order. | **Play now**, **Skip**, **Remove**, and **Move up** or **Move down** in **More actions** |
| **History** | Items that played, were rejected or were skipped. | None |

Each row shows the title, "Shared by" and the viewer's name, the length and the source.
An item in **Up next** also shows its place in the play order.
When nothing was shared yet, the card shows "No media has been shared yet."

### Approve or reject an item

Choose **Approve** on an item in **Waiting for approval**.
The item moves to the end of **Up next**.

Choose **Reject** to turn the item down.
The item moves to **History**, and a viewer who paid gets the refund.

### Change the play order

Open **More actions** on an item in **Up next**, then choose **Move up** or **Move down**.
The item changes place by one, and the positions of the other items follow.

Choose **Play now** to play an item at once.
The item moves to the top of **Up next**.

When nothing plays, the player starts with this item.
When something plays, the bot skips it and plays this item.

The skipped item moves to **History** as skipped, and its viewer gets the refund if they paid.
The first item in **Up next** has no **Play now**.

### Skip or remove an item

Choose **Skip** on an item in **Up next**.
The item moves to **History** as skipped, and a viewer who paid gets the refund.

Choose **Remove** to take an item out of the queue.
The dialog **Remove this item?** shows the title.
Choose **Remove** in the dialog: the item moves to **History** as rejected, and a viewer who paid gets the refund.

## Play items on stream

The **On stream** card at the top of the page shows what the media share overlay plays now.
It shows the title, the viewer who shared it, **Playing** or **Paused**, and the time in the item.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Play** | Starts the first item in **Up next**, or goes on with a paused item. |
| **Pause** | Pauses the item on stream. |
| **Skip to the next item** | Skips the item that plays and starts the next one. A viewer who paid gets the refund. |
| **Stop playback** | Ends the item that plays and moves it to **History** as played. The next item does not start. |
| **Place in the item** | Moves to another point in the item. |
| **Overlay volume** | How loud the overlay plays, from 0 to 100. |

When an item ends on the overlay, it moves to **History** as played and the next item starts.
When nothing is approved, the card shows "Approve an item to play it on stream."
By default a moderator and every role above it may use the player.

A **Media playback** step in a [pipeline](/reference/commands/pipelines) runs the same controls.
It can also play a finished item again, or play an approved item at once.
The [Pipelines](/reference/commands/pipelines) page explains how to set up a step.

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**The On stream card says "The Media share browser source is not loaded in OBS, so nothing plays on stream."**
The media share overlay is not in OBS.
Choose **Set up in OBS** and add the overlay as a browser source, as [OBS browser source](/reference/overlays/obs-browser-source) explains.

::
