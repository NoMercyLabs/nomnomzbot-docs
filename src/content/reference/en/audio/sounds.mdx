title: Sounds
description: Add sound clips, tune their volume, and play them from a chat word, a command, a reward or a pipeline.
---

You keep the channel's sound clips on the **Sounds** page in the sidebar.
A sound clip is a short audio file the bot plays on your overlay.
Chat can play it with a word, and a [pipeline](/reference/commands/pipelines) can play it as a step.

Sound clips play through the overlay in your OBS browser source.
The setup is on [OBS browser source](/reference/overlays/obs-browser-source).

## Who can use this page

A **Moderator**, **Lead moderator**, **Editor** or **Broadcaster** may open the page and change clips.
The roles are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).

The **Broadcaster** may also let a **VIP** open the page.
A VIP then sees the list only.
**New sound clip**, the on/off switch, **Edit** and **Delete** show only to someone who may change clips.

## Add a sound clip

1. Open **Sounds** in the sidebar and choose **New sound clip**.
   The **New sound clip** dialog opens.
2. Choose a file in **Audio file**.
3. Type a **Short name**, for example `airhorn`.
   Commands and pipelines find the clip by this name, and it cannot change later.
4. Type a **Display name**, the name you see in the list.
5. Set **Volume (0 to 100)**, or leave it at the default.
6. Choose **Add sound clip**.
   The dialog closes and the clip shows in the **Sound clips** list, switched on.

| Field | Rule | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Audio file** | An mp3, ogg or wav file of at most 10 MB and at most 3 minutes. | None |
| **Short name** | Must not be empty. No other clip in the channel may have the same name. | None |
| **Display name** | Must not be empty. | None |
| **Volume (0 to 100)** | A whole number from 0 to 100. | 80 |

The bot reads the file itself to find its type.
A file with another name ending is refused when its content is not mp3, ogg or wav.
A refusal shows in the dialog, and nothing is saved.

A channel holds at most 100 clips.
Sound clip storage also counts toward the channel's limits on [Resource limits](/reference/settings/resource-limits).

When the list is empty, the page shows "No sound clips yet."

## Read the list

Each row shows the display name in bold, with a line of details under it.

| Detail | Example |
| --- | --- |
| The short name | `airhorn` |
| The chat word, when the clip has one (see [Play a clip from chat](#play-a-clip-from-chat)) | chat word `airhorn` |
| The volume | volume 80 |
| The length in seconds | 2.5 s |

The list shows 50 clips at a time; **Show more sound clips** loads the next 50.

## Change a clip

Choose **Edit** on the row.
A dialog named **Edit** and the clip's display name opens.
Each field shows the clip's current value.
The audio file and the short name stay as uploaded.

| Field | What it does | A new clip starts with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Display name** | The name in the list. It must not be empty. | The name you typed when you added it |
| **Volume (0 to 100)** | How loud the clip plays. | The volume you set when you added it |
| **Cooldown in seconds** | How long the clip waits before chat can play it again with its chat word. 0 means no wait. | 0 seconds |
| **Who may play it** | The lowest role that may play the clip with its chat word. | **Everyone** |
| **Chat word** | A word that plays the clip when a viewer types it as the whole message. Leave it empty for none. | Empty |

Choose **Save sound clip**.
The dialog closes and the row shows the new values.

The cooldown can be at most 86400 seconds (one day).
The cooldown and **Who may play it** apply only to the chat word.
A pipeline step plays the clip without them.

## Play a clip from chat

A chat word plays a clip when a viewer's whole message is that word.
The word has no prefix, so `airhorn` plays the clip and `!airhorn` does not.
Case does not matter.

| Rule for **Chat word** | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| It has a space | The dialog shows "The chat word cannot contain spaces." |
| It is longer than 50 characters | The dialog shows "The chat word may have at most 50 characters." |
| Another clip in the channel has the same word | The bot refuses it, and nothing is saved. |

A viewer below **Who may play it** gets no sound and no reply.
During the cooldown the word plays nothing.
The broadcaster and moderators skip the cooldown.

A message that matches a chat word only plays the clip.
It does not also start a chat trigger with the same word.

## Play a clip from a command, a reward or a pipeline

A command or a [channel-point reward](/reference/automation/rewards) plays a clip through a pipeline.
Add a **Play sound** step to a [pipeline](/reference/commands/pipelines).
Then pick that pipeline in the command's **Pipeline** field, or in the reward's settings on [Rewards](/reference/automation/rewards).

| **Play sound** field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Sound clip** | The clip to play. |
| **Volume** | A volume from 0 to 100 for this step only. Leave it blank to use the clip's own volume. |
| **Wait for finish (true/false)** | Pauses the rest of the pipeline until the clip ends. The wait is at most 60 seconds. |
| **Synced** | Starts the clip at the visual cue, outside the shared audio queue. |
| **Handle** | A name for this playback, so a later **Stop sound** step can stop this clip only. |

The **Stop sound** step stops clips that are playing.
With **Handle** set, it stops that playback.
With **All** on, it stops every clip that is playing.
A **Play sound** step fails when the clip is switched off or does not exist.

## Turn a clip off

Use the switch at the end of the row.
A clip that is off does not play from its chat word or from a pipeline step.
Turn the switch on again to play it.

## Set the overall loudness

Each clip plays at its own volume times the channel's master volume.
The master volume is set under **Audio levels** on the **Overlays** page, as explained on [Set up overlays](/reference/overlays/set-up-overlays).
Its default is 100, so a clip plays at its own volume until you change it.

## Delete a clip

1. Choose **Delete** on the row.
   The bot counts the pipelines and other places that play the clip.
2. Read the dialog that asks **Delete** with the clip's display name.
   It lists each kind of place that uses the clip, with a count and the names.
   When nothing uses the clip, it shows "No command, pipeline or reward plays this clip."
3. Choose **Delete sound clip**.
   The clip and its audio file are gone for good, and the row leaves the list.

A pipeline step that named the clip stays in the pipeline.
It fails when it runs, because the clip no longer exists.

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**A chat word plays nothing.**
Check these, in order:

1. The switch in the row. A clip that is off does not play.
2. **Who may play it**. The viewer's role must be at least that role.
3. **Cooldown in seconds**. The word plays nothing until the cooldown ends.
4. The message. It must be the chat word alone, with no prefix.
5. The overlay. It must be open in your OBS browser source (see [OBS browser source](/reference/overlays/obs-browser-source)).

::
