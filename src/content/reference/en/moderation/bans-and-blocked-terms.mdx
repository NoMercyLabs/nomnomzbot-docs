---
title: Bans and blocked terms
description: See who is banned in your channel and unban them, and block or unblock the words and phrases Twitch holds back from chat.
---

Use a ban when a person should no longer be able to chat, and a blocked term when a word or phrase needs Twitch's moderation check wherever it appears.
NomNomzBot shows Twitch's current lists on **Banned people** and **Blocked terms**, both in the **Access** group of **Settings**.
These pages manage what Twitch enforces, rather than a separate list kept only by the bot.

You apply a new ban from a person's [profile](/reference/community/people) or the [Chat page](/reference/community/chat-page).
Return to **Banned people** when you want to review the list or let someone chat again.
For rules that delete messages or trigger a timeout automatically, use [chat filters](/reference/moderation/automod-and-chat-filters).

| Page | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Banned people** | Everyone permanently banned in your channel, as Twitch holds the list. |
| **Blocked terms** | The words and phrases Twitch holds back from your chat. |

## What must be connected first

**Banned people** reads the list through your own Twitch connection as broadcaster.
When that connection is stale or missing, the panel shows an error instead of an empty list.

**Unban**, **Block**, **Unblock** and the **Blocked now** list run on the Twitch account of the person who uses them.
Twitch must have made that person a moderator of your channel, or Twitch refuses the action.

## Who can use these pages

The roles below are the defaults and can be adjusted on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).

| Who | What they may do |
| --- | --- |
| A **Moderator** | Open both pages, and unban people. |
| A **Lead moderator** | All of the above, and block and unblock words and phrases. |
| An **Editor** | All of the above. |
| The **Broadcaster** | All of the above. |

Someone who may not unban sees the list without the **Unban** button.
Someone who may not block sees the **Blocked now** list only, without the **Block a word or phrase** panel and without **Unblock**.

## See who is banned

1. Open **Banned people** in the settings.
   The page shows the **Banned people** panel.
2. Read each row.
   A row shows the person's name and a line like "Banned by Stoney_Eagle 2 hours ago", with the name of the moderator who banned them.
   When the ban has a reason, the line shows the reason, then a dot, then "Banned by Stoney_Eagle 2 hours ago".

The list shows permanent bans only.
A timeout does not appear here.
The newest ban is at the top.

When nobody is banned, the panel shows "Nobody is banned in your channel."

## Unban a person

1. On **Banned people**, find the person's row.
2. Choose **Unban**.
   The person can chat again, and the row leaves the list.

**Unban** asks for no confirmation.
Banning the person again undoes it.
If the unban fails, the page shows "`{name}` could not be unbanned." with the person's name in place of `{name}`.

## Block a word or phrase

For example, you can add an unwanted promotional phrase here instead of deleting each occurrence by hand.
This adds the phrase to Twitch's blocked terms; it does not create one of NomNomzBot's custom filters.

1. Open **Blocked terms** in the settings.
   The page shows the **Block a word or phrase** panel and the **Blocked now** list.
2. Type the word or phrase in the field.
   **Block** becomes active.
3. Choose **Block**, or press Enter.
   The term appears in **Blocked now**, and the field empties.

Spaces at the start and end of the text drop out.
A field with only spaces keeps **Block** off.
If Twitch refuses the term, the page shows "The term could not be blocked:" with the reason.

When the list is empty, it shows "Nothing is blocked yet."

## Unblock a word or phrase

1. On **Blocked terms**, find the term in **Blocked now**.
2. Choose **Unblock**.
   The term leaves the list, and Twitch no longer holds it back.

**Unblock** asks for no confirmation.
Blocking the term again undoes it.
If it fails, the page shows "The term could not be unblocked:" with the reason.

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**A list shows an error instead of rows.**
The page shows "The banned people could not be loaded." or "The blocked terms could not be loaded."
Choose **Try again** in the panel to load the list from Twitch again, which helps when Twitch was briefly unreachable.
Trying again does not help when the broadcaster's Twitch connection is stale or missing, or when the Twitch account you use is not a Twitch moderator of the channel.
In the first case, the broadcaster signs out and signs in again with **Continue as broadcaster**, as [Sign in](/reference/getting-started/sign-in) explains.
The **Connections** panel on **Home** shows the Twitch row for that connection, as [Connect platforms](/reference/getting-started/connect-platforms) explains.
In the second case, have the broadcaster make that account a moderator on Twitch.

::
