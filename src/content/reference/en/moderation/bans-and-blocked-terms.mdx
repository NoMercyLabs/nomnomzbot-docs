---
title: Bans and blocked terms
description: See who is banned in your channel and unban them, and block or unblock the words and phrases Twitch holds back from chat.
---

You see and unban banned people on the **Banned people** settings page.
You block words and phrases on the **Blocked terms** settings page.
Both pages are in the **Access** group of the settings sidebar.
At the top of each page, the title line reads **Moderation** / and then the page name.

| Page | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Banned people** | Everyone banned in your channel, as Twitch holds the list. |
| **Blocked terms** | The words and phrases Twitch holds back from your chat. |

Twitch keeps both lists.
What you see on these pages is what Twitch enforces.

## Who can use these pages

The roles below are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).

| Who | What they may do |
| --- | --- |
| A **Moderator** | Open both pages, and unban people. |
| A **Lead moderator** | All of the above, and block and unblock words and phrases. |
| An **Editor** | All of the above. |
| The **Broadcaster** | All of the above. |

Someone who may not unban sees the list without the **Unban** button.
Someone who may not block sees the **Blocked now** list only, without the **Block a word or phrase** panel and without **Unblock**.

## See who is banned

1. Open **Banned people** in the settings.
   The page shows the **Banned people** panel.
2. Read each row.
   A row shows the person's name and a line like "Banned by a moderator 2 hours ago".
   When the ban has a reason, the line shows the reason, then a dot, then "Banned by a moderator 2 hours ago".

When nobody is banned, the panel shows "Nobody is banned in your channel."

## Unban a person

1. On **Banned people**, find the person's row.
2. Choose **Unban**.
   The person can chat again, and the row leaves the list.

**Unban** asks for no confirmation.
Banning the person again undoes it.
If the unban fails, the page shows "`{name}` could not be unbanned." with the person's name in place of `{name}`.

## Block a word or phrase

1. Open **Blocked terms** in the settings.
   The page shows the **Block a word or phrase** panel and the **Blocked now** list.
2. Type the word or phrase in the field.
   **Block** becomes active.
3. Choose **Block**, or press Enter.
   The term appears in **Blocked now**, and the field empties.

Spaces at the start and end of the text drop out.
A field with only spaces keeps **Block** off.
If Twitch refuses the term, the page shows "The term could not be blocked:" with the reason.

When the list is empty, it shows "Nothing is blocked yet."

## Unblock a word or phrase

1. On **Blocked terms**, find the term in **Blocked now**.
2. Choose **Unblock**.
   The term leaves the list, and Twitch no longer holds it back.

**Unblock** asks for no confirmation.
Blocking the term again undoes it.
If it fails, the page shows "The term could not be unblocked:" with the reason.

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**A list shows an error instead of rows.**
The page shows "The banned people could not be loaded." or "The blocked terms could not be loaded."
Choose **Try again** in the panel to load the list from Twitch again.

::
