---
title: Linked channels
description: Choose what this channel shares with other trusted NomNomzBot servers, and whether mass bans and network blocks reach it.
---

You choose how bans reach your channel from outside it on the **Federation** settings page.
The page is in the **Connections** group of the settings sidebar.
A linked channel is a channel on another NomNomzBot server that this server trusts.

| Card | What it decides |
| --- | --- |
| **Sharing with other bots** | What this channel accepts from, or shares with, other trusted NomNomzBot servers. |
| **Choices for one bot** | Choices that name one other server instead of every trusted one. The card shows only when such a choice exists. |
| **Network blocks** | Whether an admin network block also bans its blocked actor in this channel. |
| **Moderator mass bans** | Whether a mass ban from one of your moderators also bans its accounts in this channel. |

Everything on the page is off until you turn it on.

## Who can use this page

The roles below are the channel roles of the dashboard.

| Who | What they may do |
| --- | --- |
| A **Lead moderator** | Open the page, and change the **Accept** and **Share** switches. |
| An **Editor** | All of the above. |
| The **Broadcaster** | All of the above, and change the **Network blocks** and **Moderator mass bans** switches. |

A **Moderator** cannot open the page.

Which other servers count as trusted is not set on this page.
An admin of your NomNomzBot server sets it.

## Accept or share a kind of data

The **Sharing with other bots** card has one row for each kind of data.
Each row has two switches: **Accept** and **Share**.

1. Open the **Federation** settings page.
2. Find the row, for example **Shared chat bans**.
3. Turn on **Accept**, **Share**, or both.
   The choice is saved at once and holds for every trusted server.
4. To stop, turn both switches off.

| Row | What the bot does with it |
| --- | --- |
| **Shared chat bans** | With **Accept** on, a ban sent by a trusted server bans the same account in your channel. **Share** is saved, but no server code reads it. |
| **Shared ban list** | The choice is saved, but no server code reads it. |
| **Shared trust list** | The choice is saved, but no server code reads it. |
| **Shared savings** | The choice is saved, but no server code reads it. |

An accepted ban is placed with your channel's own Twitch connection.
The bot records it in the moderation history as a ban that came from another server.
When Twitch refuses the ban, the bot tells you in the inbox of things that need your action.

A message from a server that is not trusted never reaches your channel.

## Turn off a choice for one bot

The **Choices for one bot** card lists choices that name one other server.
Each row shows the kind of data, **Accept** or **Share**, and the id of that server.
These choices are made outside this page.

1. Find the row.
2. Choose **Turn this choice off** (the trash icon).
   The row goes away, and that server no longer acts on your channel for that kind of data.

## Network blocks

An admin network block bans a known bad actor on every channel of the network that says yes.

| Label | Default | When on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ban blocked actors here** (switch **Ban here**) | Off | A network block also bans its blocked actor in this channel. |

A block reaches your channel when an admin applies it while the switch is on.

## Moderator mass bans

A moderator can ban a list of accounts in every channel they moderate at once.

| Label | Default | When on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Accept moderator mass bans** (switch **Accept**) | Off | A mass ban from one of your moderators also bans its accounts in this channel. |

When your channel is live, the mass ban does not ban at once.
It waits for an approval in your channel before it bans.

<details>
<summary>Other ways a mass ban can reach your channel</summary>

The switch is not the only way in.
A mass ban also reaches your channel when the moderator names it as a channel under attack.
It also reaches your channel when the moderator added your channel to their own mass ban list.
A channel the moderator excludes is never banned in.

</details>

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**The page shows "The sharing choices could not be loaded."**
The bot did not answer.
Choose **Try again** on the card.

::

::Troubleshooting

**A switch does not move when you click it.**
Your role may not change it.
Only the broadcaster changes **Ban here** and the mass ban **Accept** switch.

::
