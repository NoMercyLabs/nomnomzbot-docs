title: Viewer reports

You read and sort the reports viewers file about a chatter on the **Viewer reports** page.
The page sits in the **Access** group of the settings sidebar.

## Who can use this page

| Who | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| A **Moderator** or higher | Open the page and read every report. |
| A **Lead moderator** or higher | Also **Dismiss** or **Escalate** an open report. |
| A viewer in the channel | File a report. |

## How a report arrives

A report names one chatter and gives a reason.
The dashboard has no form to file a report.
A report comes in through the bot's API.

| Rule | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| The reason is empty | The bot refuses the report: "A report needs a reason." |
| The reason is longer than 500 characters | The bot refuses the report. |
| No chatter is named | The bot refuses the report: "A report needs a reported user." |

A new report has the status **Open**.
The chatter in the report is saved as a user of the bot, even if they never chatted before.

## Read the reports

1. Open **Viewer reports** in the **Access** group of the settings sidebar.
   The **Viewer reports** list shows the open reports, newest first.
2. Choose a status in **Show reports that are**: **Open**, **Escalated** or **Dismissed**.
   The list shows only the reports with that status.

Each row shows the name of the reported chatter.
Under the name, the row shows the reason and who filed it, for example "Filed by a viewer 5 minutes ago".
When the bot does not know the reporter, the row says "a viewer".

| Status | Text when the list is empty |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | "No open reports. Nobody has asked for help." |
| **Escalated** | "No escalated reports." |
| **Dismissed** | "No dismissed reports." |

## Dismiss or escalate a report

1. Show the **Open** reports.
2. On the row, choose **Dismiss** for a report that needs no action.
   Choose **Escalate** for a report that a moderator should look into.
3. The page shows "The report about (name) is dismissed." or "The report about (name) is escalated."
   The report leaves the **Open** list and moves to the list of its new status.

The bot records who made the choice and when.
The buttons show only on open reports.

::Callout
**Escalate** does not ban or time out anyone.
It only marks the report.
The bans of the channel are on [Bans and blocked terms](/reference/moderation/bans-and-blocked-terms).
::

::Troubleshooting
**The page shows "The report about (name) could not be changed."**
The bot did not save the choice.
The report stays as it was. Try again.
::

::Troubleshooting
**The page shows "The reports could not be loaded."**
The list did not load.
Choose **Try again** on the card to load it again.
::

## The safety review queue

The automatic actions of spam defense wait for a human decision in a separate queue.
This queue is not on the channel's settings.
It is the **Review queue** on the **Trust and safety** page, in the admin area of the dashboard.
Only an operator of the deployment with the right to review trust and safety sees it.

<details>
<summary>Work the review queue</summary>

1. Type why you open the page in **Reason for access**.
   Nothing loads until you do. Every read and action is saved in the audit log with this reason.
2. The **Review queue** shows the automatic account actions, newest first.
   Each row shows the account, the channel, the reason, the message, the confidence, the action and the time.
3. Choose **Confirm** or **Overturn** on a row.

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirm** | The action stays as it is and the review closes. |
| **Overturn** | The bot removes the Twitch timeout or ban it gave, then marks the action overturned. |

When the bot cannot remove the timeout or ban, the row stays as it was.
The same page also shows **Cross-channel signals**: accounts flagged in two or more channels.

</details>
