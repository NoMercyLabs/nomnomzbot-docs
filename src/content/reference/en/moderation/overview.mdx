You find moderation in three places on the dashboard.
The review queue is on the **Home** page.
The moderation history of one person is in the profile dialog.

The profile dialog is the window that opens when you choose a person's name.
The settings for filters, bans and trust are in the **Access** group of the settings.

| Where | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Review queue** on **Home** | Allow or deny the chat messages and flags that wait for a moderator. |
| **Moderation** tab of the profile dialog | Read what moderation did to one person in this channel. |
| The **Access** group of the settings | Set the filters, the bans, the trust rules and the reports. |

The dashboard is explained on [Dashboard tour](/reference/getting-started/dashboard-tour).

## Who can use these tools

A **Moderator**, **Lead moderator**, **Editor** or **Broadcaster** may see the review queue.
The same roles may allow and deny items in the queue.
The same roles may read the moderation history.

A role that may see the queue, but may not decide on it, sees the list without **Allow** or **Deny** buttons.
A role that may not see the queue gets "The review queue could not load." in the panel.
In the profile dialog, a role that may not read the history sees "Your role cannot see the moderation history."

## Review queue

The **Review queue** panel on the **Home** page lists the items that wait for a decision.
The panel shows the count, for example "3 waiting".

An item comes into the queue in these cases:

| Source | What happened to the message |
| --- | --- |
| Twitch AutoMod | AutoMod holds the message. It is not in chat yet. |
| A chat filter set to hold | The bot removed the message from chat. |
| A chat filter set to flag | The message stays in chat. |
| The newcomer gate | The bot removed the message of an account or follow younger than the channel's limit. |
| A suspicious user on Twitch | Twitch marks the chatter as restricted or monitored. The bot leaves the message alone. |
| The heat limit | A viewer crossed the channel's heat limit, and the bot did not time the viewer out by itself. This item has no message. |

The platform of an item depends on its source:

- AutoMod, the chat filters and suspicious users put only Twitch messages into the queue.
- The newcomer gate also queues Kick and YouTube messages, but only when the bot could remove the message there.
- The heat limit queues a viewer from any platform the viewer chats on.

Each row shows the sender's name and the message.
A row without a name shows "Unknown sender".

The panel hint says: "Twitch drops a held message after a while. Allowing one teaches the spam filter."

### Allow an item

1. Open **Home** in the sidebar.
2. Find the item in **Review queue**.
3. Choose **Allow**.
   The item leaves the queue.

For a message that AutoMod holds, **Allow** sends the message to chat.
For every other item, **Allow** only records your decision in the bot.
A removed message does not come back to chat.

### Deny an item

1. Open **Home** in the sidebar.
2. Find the item in **Review queue**.
3. Choose **Deny**.
   The row shows **Denied** and an **Undo** button.
4. Do nothing to keep the denial.
   After 6 seconds the bot sends the denial, and the item leaves the queue.

For a message that AutoMod holds, the denial tells Twitch to drop the message.
For every other item, the denial only records your decision in the bot.

Choose **Undo** within the 6 seconds to take the denial back.
The item stays in the queue.

If you leave the page before the 6 seconds end, the bot sends the denial at once.

### Panel states

| The panel shows | When |
| --- | --- |
| "Nothing waits for you right now." | The stream is live and nothing waits. |
| "Nothing waits for you. Held messages and spam checks show up here during a stream." | The stream is offline and nothing waits. |
| **Shield mode on** | Twitch shield mode is on for the channel, and your role may see shield mode. The list works as usual. |
| "The review queue could not load." | The queue failed to load, or your role may not see it. |

## Moderation history

The moderation history lists everything moderation did to one person in this channel, newest first.

1. Choose a viewer's name anywhere in the dashboard, for example in chat.
   The profile dialog opens.
2. Choose the **Moderation** tab.
   The **Moderation history** section lists the actions.

The **People** page shows the same profile beside its list.

Each entry has three lines:

- What happened, for example **Banned**, or "Timed out for" and the length.
- The reason, when one was given.
- Who did it and the date. A moderator's name, or **Automatic** when the bot did it by itself, such as a filter or AutoMod.

| Entry | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Banned** | The person was banned. |
| **Unbanned** | The ban was lifted. |
| **Timed out** | The person was timed out. With a length, it shows "Timed out for" and the length. |
| **Warned** | The person got a warning. |
| **Accepted the warning** | The person accepted a warning. |
| **Message deleted** | A message of the person was deleted. |
| **Message held by AutoMod, then denied** | AutoMod held a message, and the message was denied. |
| **Caught by a chat filter** | A chat filter caught a message. |
| **Report confirmed** | A report about the person was confirmed. |
| **Note** | A note was added. |
| **Moderation action** | Any other action. |

**Show older** at the bottom loads older entries.
A person with no entries shows "Moderation has done nothing to this person here yet."

## Where the other tools live

Each of these pages is in the **Access** group of the settings.

| Settings page | What it holds | Guide page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AutoMod filters** and **Chat filters** | The bot's filters, the Twitch AutoMod levels and your own filters. | [AutoMod and chat filters](/reference/moderation/automod-and-chat-filters) |
| **Blocked terms** and **Banned people** | The words Twitch blocks, and the people who are banned. | [Bans and blocked terms](/reference/moderation/bans-and-blocked-terms) |
| **Spam defense** | The spam policy and what it found. | [Spam defense](/reference/moderation/spam-defense) |
| **Trust policy** and **Escalation** | The rules for new chatters, and the warn, timeout and ban steps. | [Trust and escalation](/reference/moderation/trust-and-escalation) |
| **Viewer reports** | The reports viewers send about chat. | - |

You can moderate one chat line on the **Chat** page.
You can moderate one person from the profile dialog.
