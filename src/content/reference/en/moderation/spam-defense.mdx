You set the channel's spam defense policy on the **Spam defense** page.
Open **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Spam defense** in the **Access** group.
Each setting on the page shows a short explanation and what moving it costs.

## Who can use this page

| Who | What they may do |
| --- | --- |
| A **Moderator**, **Lead moderator**, **Editor** or **Broadcaster** | Open the page and read every value. |
| The **Broadcaster** | Change the values and save them. |

The roles are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).

The **Save** button shows only to someone who may change the page.
Anyone else sees the number boxes locked, and an on or off badge in place of each switch.

## Change the policy

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Spam defense** in the **Access** group.
   The page shows one card for each group of settings.
2. Change a number or flip a switch.
   The header shows "Unsaved changes", and **Save** becomes active.
3. Choose **Save**.
   The bot stores every setting at once and shows "Spam defense saved."

**Save** stays inactive while a value is out of range.
The reason shows in red under the setting, for example "Enter a value from 0 to 365."

## Where the values come from

A channel that never saved this page uses the bot's default values.
After the first **Save**, the channel keeps its own values.

The tables below show the default values the bot starts with.
The person who runs your bot can change these default values.
Then a channel that never saved uses the changed values, not the ones in the tables.

## Start in watch-only mode

**Watch only, do not act** is on by default.
In this mode the bot finds spam and records it, and it acts on none of it.
The dashboard has no screen that shows these records yet.

Each channel also has a first watch period of seven days.
During these seven days the bot only watches, even with **Watch only, do not act** off.
The seven days start when the channel joins the bot.

If spam defense was off when you first saved it on, the seven days start at that save.
The bot acts on real viewers only when both are true: **Watch only, do not act** is off, and the seven days have passed.
Read the explanation of each setting before you turn it off.

## Settings and defaults

Some settings are a share.
A share is a number from 0 to 1, so 0.8 means 80 percent.

### Master

This group turns spam defense on or off and sets watch-only mode.

| Setting | Default | Allowed values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Spam defense enabled** | On | On or off |
| **Watch only, do not act** | On | On or off |

**Spam defense enabled** off means no detection and no records at all.
With **Watch only, do not act** on, the bot records and does not act.
With it off, the bot acts once the channel's first seven days have passed.

### Trust

| Setting | Default | Allowed values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Days around before a viewer counts as established** | 90 days | 0 or more |
| **Messages before a viewer counts as established** | 300 messages | 0 or more |
| **Separate active days before a viewer counts as established** | 30 days | 0 or more |
| **Strike-free days before a viewer counts as established** | 180 days | 0 or more |
| **Watch hours here that earn protection** | 10 hours | 1 to 200 |
| **Watch hours across all channels that earn protection** | 25 hours | 1 to 500 |

An established viewer is a viewer who has reached all four "established" values.
A moderator or VIP of your channel is always established.
"Days around" counts the days since the viewer's first message in your channel.

The trust levels are explained on [Trust and escalation](/reference/moderation/trust-and-escalation).
On the page, the four "established" values sit together under the label **Trust ladder thresholds**.

The bot counts the hours each viewer watches.
A viewer who reaches either watch-hours value earns protection.

**Watch hours here that earn protection** counts the hours in your channel only.
**Watch hours across all channels that earn protection** counts the hours in every channel on this bot server.

A protected viewer is never banned or timed out automatically.
The bot may still remove a message of that viewer and flag it.
A moderator then decides the rest.

### New accounts and new followers

| Setting | Default | Allowed values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Newest account age that can chat freely** | 0 days (off) | 0 to 365 |
| **Newest follow that can chat freely** | 0 days (off) | 0 to 90 |
| **Keep gated messages for a moderator** | On | On or off |

A gated message is a message that one of the two age limits above removed.
With **Keep gated messages for a moderator** on, the message waits in the moderation queue for a moderator.
With it off, the message is removed and nothing is kept.

### Message content

| Setting | Default | Allowed values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How similar counts as the same spam** | 0.6 | 0 to 1 |
| **Shortest message that can be matched** | 8 characters | 2 to 50 |
| **Restrict other alphabets to established viewers** | Off | On or off |

"Other alphabets" means writing that does not use Latin letters, for example Japanese, Korean, Cyrillic or Arabic.
With **Restrict other alphabets to established viewers** off, anyone may write in another alphabet.
With it on, a message in another alphabet from a viewer who is not yet established looks more suspicious to the bot.

The bot then rates that message as medium risk at most, never higher because of the alphabet alone.
An established viewer is never affected.

### Coordinated campaigns

A campaign is a group of accounts that post the same message at almost the same time.
A stranger is an account with no standing in your channel.
A group is exonerated when your regulars join in and the share of strangers drops, so the bot judges it a community joke.

| Setting | Default | Allowed values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Share of strangers that makes it a campaign** | 0.8 | 0.5 to 1 |
| **Share at which the group is exonerated** | 0.65 | 0.3 to 0.95 |
| **Fewest accounts that can be a campaign** | 5 accounts | 2 to 100 |
| **How long a group is watched** | 600 seconds | 30 to 3600 |
| **Longest a group can stay open** | 1800 seconds | 60 to 7200 |
| **Head start before acting** | 8 seconds | 0 to 120 |
| **Undo automatically when a group is exonerated** | On | On or off |

Two pairs in this group must stay in order:

- **Share at which the group is exonerated** must stay below **Share of strangers that makes it a campaign**.
- **Longest a group can stay open** must be at least **How long a group is watched**.

The page names a broken pair under the setting, and **Save** stays inactive.

### Sudden activity

| Setting | Default | Allowed values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Follow rate that counts as unusual** | 5 times | 1.5 to 50 |
| **Join rate that counts as unusual** | 4 times | 1.5 to 50 |

Each value is a number of times your channel's normal rate.

### Raid lockdown

To tighten the room means to turn on stricter chat rules during a raid, such as slow mode and followers-only, and later put them back as they were.
The dashboard marks all three settings in this group **Not yet active**.
Nothing tightens the room automatically during a raid yet, and the dashboard has no button that tightens it.

The bot reads these three settings only for a lockdown that is started by hand, outside the dashboard.
Then the room stays tightened for **How long the room stays tightened**.

A second lockdown request can arrive while the room is still tightened.
With **Keep it tightened while the attack continues** on, the bot then extends the time.
It never extends past **Longest the room can stay tightened**, counted from the start.

| Setting | Default | Allowed values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How long the room stays tightened** | 15 minutes | 1 to 240 |
| **Keep it tightened while the attack continues** | On | On or off |
| **Longest the room can stay tightened** | 60 minutes | 5 to 480 |

**Longest the room can stay tightened** must be at least **How long the room stays tightened**.

### Shared spam list

The shared spam list is meant to hold spam patterns and bad links that other bot servers found.
The dashboard marks **Use the shared spam list** and **Share what you catch** **Not yet active**.
The bot stores their values and does not read them yet.

| Setting | Default | Allowed values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Use the shared spam list** | On | On or off |
| **Share what you catch** | Off | On or off |
| **Reports needed before a shared pattern acts** | 3 reports | 1 to 20 |

The page explains that **Share what you catch** is meant to send only the patterns your channel confirms, never message text or viewer names.

The bot reads **Reports needed before a shared pattern acts** each time it checks a message.
A pattern that is held back is used in that check only after it has at least this many reports.

Today the bot holds back no pattern: the patterns it uses come from your own bot server, and those are never held back.
So this number changes the result only when held-back patterns arrive from the shared spam list.

## Settings marked Not yet active

These settings carry the badge **Not yet active**:

- **Join rate that counts as unusual**
- **How long the room stays tightened**
- **Keep it tightened while the attack continues**
- **Longest the room can stay tightened**
- **Use the shared spam list**
- **Share what you catch**

The bot stores **Join rate that counts as unusual**, **Use the shared spam list** and **Share what you catch**, and does not read them yet.
The three lockdown settings are read only for a lockdown started by hand, as **Raid lockdown** above explains.

## Guarantees with no switch

The card **Guarantees with no switch** at the bottom of the page lists rules that no setting can turn off.
For example, an established viewer of your channel is never acted on automatically by any part of spam defense.
