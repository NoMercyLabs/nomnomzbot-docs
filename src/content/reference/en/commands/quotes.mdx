---
title: Quotes
description: Keep, add, edit, delete and read back the channel's quotes on the Quotes page.
---

You keep the channel's quotes on the **Quotes** page in the sidebar.
A quote is a line someone said, saved with a number.
Chat reads quotes back with the `!quote` command, described on [Built-in commands](/reference/commands/built-in-commands).

## Who can use this page

| Action | Who may do it, out of the box |
| --- | --- |
| Open the page, search and pick a random quote | **VIP** and up |
| Add and edit a quote | **VIP** and up |
| Delete a quote | **Moderator** and up |

The roles are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).
The edit button and the delete button each show only to someone who may use them.

## What a quote holds

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Quote** | The line. Up to 500 characters. |
| **Said by** | A name, with or without the `@`. Optional. |
| **Game** | The game at the time. Optional. |
| **Said at** | The date and time. When you add a quote, an empty field uses the time you save. When you edit a quote, an empty field keeps the date the quote already has. |

The bot gives each quote its number.
A number never changes, and it is never given to another quote.

## Add a quote

1. Open **Quotes** in the sidebar and choose **New quote**.
   The **New quote** dialog opens.
2. Type the line in **Quote**.
3. Fill in **Said by**, **Game** and **Said at**, or leave them empty.
4. Choose **Add quote**.
   The dialog closes and the quote shows in the list with its number.

An empty **Quote** shows "Type the quote."

## Edit a quote

1. Choose the edit button at the end of the quote's row.
   The **Edit quote** dialog opens with the quote's fields filled in.
2. Change the fields.
3. Choose **Save quote**.
   The dialog closes and the row shows the new text.

The number stays the same.

## Delete a quote

1. Choose the delete button at the end of the quote's row.
   A dialog asks "Delete quote" with the number.
2. Choose **Delete quote**.
   The quote leaves the list.

The quote is removed for good.
Its number is not given to another quote, so the numbers in the list can have a gap.

## Find a quote

Type in **Search the quotes** above the list.
The list keeps the quotes whose line or **Said by** name holds your text, in any letter case.
When nothing matches, the list shows "No quote matches your search."

The list shows the newest number first, 50 quotes at a time.
Choose **Show more quotes** at the bottom to see the next 50.
Each row shows the number, the line, and then the name, the game and the date.

## Pick a random quote

Choose **Random quote** at the top of the page.
One quote, picked at random, shows above the search box.
On a narrow screen the button shows its dice icon only.

When the channel has no quotes, the list shows "No quotes yet."

## Changes from chat

By default, everyone in chat may read a quote with `!quote`.
Adding and editing a quote in chat need the same role as on this page, **VIP** and up by default.
Deleting a quote in chat needs **Moderator** and up by default.

A viewer without the role who tries to add, edit or delete gets a line from the bot that they may not do it.
A quote added, edited or deleted with `!quote` in chat also shows on this page.
The list updates by itself, with no reload.
