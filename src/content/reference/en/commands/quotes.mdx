---
title: Quotes
description: Keep, add, edit, delete and read back the channel's quotes on the Quotes page and with !quote in chat.
---

Your channel's quotes live on **Quotes** in the dashboard sidebar, and chat reaches the same collection with `!quote`.
The bot numbers each quote for you, and that number stays with the quote for good, so `!quote 12` keeps finding the same line after you fix a typo in it.

## Who can use quotes

By default, everyone in chat can read a quote with `!quote`, and the **Quotes** page is for **VIP** and up.
For someone who may add quotes, **VIP** and up by default, a `!quote` sent as a reply to a chat message saves that message as a new quote instead of reading one.
Three permissions set who may view quotes on the page, who may add and change them, and who may remove them.
Adding, changing and removing need the same role on the page and in chat.

| Action | Who may do it, out of the box | Name on the Permissions page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open the page, search and pick a random quote | **VIP** and up | **See quotes** |
| Add and edit a quote | **VIP** and up | **Add and change quotes** |
| Delete a quote | **Moderator** and up | **Remove quotes** |

You can make any of these stricter in the **Quotes** area on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions#set-the-lowest-role-for-an-action), never looser.
The **New quote** button and the buttons on each row show only to someone who may use them.

## What a quote holds

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Quote** | The line. Up to 500 characters. |
| **Said by** | A name, with or without the `@`. Optional. Up to 100 characters. |
| **Game** | The game at the time. Optional. Up to 100 characters. |
| **Said at** | The date and time, picked in the field's date and time picker. Optional. |

**Said at** is stored as one moment in time: you pick it in your own computer's time zone, and each person who opens the page sees the date in their own time zone.
Chat replies do not show the date.
An empty **Said at** uses the time you save when you add a quote, and keeps the date the quote already has when you edit one.

When **Said by** is the name of a chatter in your channel, the quote also shows under **Quotes** on that viewer's profile.

## Add a quote

1. Open **Quotes** and choose **New quote**.
2. Type the line in **Quote**.
   An empty **Quote** shows "Type the quote." when you try to save.
3. Fill in **Said by**, **Game** and **Said at**, or leave them empty.
4. Choose **Add quote**.

The dialog closes, and the quote shows at the top of the list with its new number.

## Edit a quote

Each row ends with a pencil button to edit the quote and a trash button to delete it.
Both buttons name the quote's number when you point at them, such as "Edit quote 12".
The list shows only the newest 100 quotes, so for an older quote, [search for it](#find-a-quote) first to bring up its row.

Choose the pencil button on the quote's row.
The **Edit quote** dialog opens with the quote's fields filled in and the number in its title.
Change what you need and choose **Save quote**, and the row shows the new text under the same number.

## Delete a quote

A deleted quote is gone for good.
Its number is never given to another quote, so the list keeps a gap where it was, and anyone who asks for that number in chat gets a line that the bot couldn't find it.

1. Choose the trash button on the quote's row.
   A dialog opens and asks "Delete quote 12?".
2. Choose **Delete quote** in the dialog to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep the quote.

The quote leaves the list.

## Find a quote

Type in **Search the quotes** above the list.
The search looks through every quote of the channel, not only the ones on screen.
The list keeps the quotes whose line or **Said by** name holds your text.

The search does not look up a number, and the page has no way to open one quote by its number.
To read quote 12, type `!quote 12` in chat.

The newest number comes first, and the list starts with the newest 50 quotes.
Choose **Show more quotes** at the bottom to see the next 50.
The list holds at most the newest 100 quotes.
To reach an older quote on the page, search for its line or its **Said by** name.
Each row shows the number and the line, then the name, the game and the date.

| The list shows | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "No quotes yet." | The channel has no quotes. |
| "No quote matches your search." | No quote holds the text you typed. |
| "The quotes could not be loaded." with **Try again** | The page could not get the quotes from the bot. Choose **Try again** to ask again. |

## Pick a random quote

Choose **Random quote** at the top of the page, and one quote, picked at random, shows above the list.
On a narrow screen the button shows only its dice icon.
The random pick shows on the page only, and nothing is posted to chat.

In a channel with no quotes, the list shows "No quotes yet.", and **Random quote** shows this notice above the list instead of a quote: "That could not be found. This channel has no quotes yet."

## Use quotes in chat

Type `!quote` for a random quote, or add a number to ask for one, such as `!quote 12`.
Use your channel's command prefix if it isn't `!`.

When someone who may add quotes saves a reply with `!quote`, the writer of the replied-to message becomes **Said by**.
For everyone else, a `!quote` sent as a reply reads a random quote.

| Message | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `!quote` | Reads a random quote. As a reply, from someone who may add quotes, it saves the replied-to message. |
| `!quote 12` | Reads quote 12. This also holds when it is sent as a reply. |
| `!quote add That was closer than expected.` | Saves a new quote with that line and no **Said by** name. |
| A reply with `!quote add` | Saves the message you replied to, with its writer as **Said by**. |
| `!quote edit 12 That was much closer than expected.` | Replaces quote 12's line and keeps its **Said by**, **Game** and **Said at**. `update` also works. |
| `!quote delete 12` | Deletes quote 12. `del` and `remove` also work. |

A quote added with text after `add` has no **Said by** name.
You can fill in **Said by** later in the **Edit quote** dialog.
To save someone's line with their name, reply to their chat message with `!quote add` and nothing after it.

A **VIP** can save a quote but cannot delete one.
The quote's number shows in the bot's reply to the save, such as **#12**, and at the start of the quote's row on the **Quotes** page.
If a VIP saves a reply by mistake, a moderator can remove it with `!quote delete` and its number, such as `!quote delete 12`, and the VIP can fix the line with `!quote edit` in the meantime.

Someone without the role who tries to add, edit or delete gets a line from the bot that they may not do it.
A change made in chat shows on the **Quotes** page right away, with no reload.

## Chat cooldown and reading aloud

`!quote` has a 45-second chat cooldown by default.
You change it in **Chat cooldown (seconds)** when you [change `!quote` on Built-in commands](/reference/commands/built-in-commands#change-a-built-in-command).
The cooldown covers every form of `!quote`: reading, adding, editing and deleting.
The broadcaster and the channel's moderators skip it.

To have the bot also speak each quote it reads, turn on **Read it aloud** for `!quote` in the same place.
It speaks only when text-to-speech is set up for your channel, and the chat reply goes out either way.
