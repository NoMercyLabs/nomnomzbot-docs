A platform connection links the channel to a streaming platform account.
Twitch comes first, then you can add Kick and YouTube.
You read the state of each connection on the **Home** page and on the **Stream** page.

## Connect Twitch

Twitch connects when the broadcaster signs in with **Continue as broadcaster**, as [Sign in](/reference/getting-started/sign-in) shows.
There is no separate connect step for Twitch.
The dashboard shows Twitch as connected for the channel.

You can stop here if you stream on Twitch only.

## Who can connect a platform

Anyone who may read the channel's connections sees them.
By default that is a **Moderator** and every role above it.

Only a person who may change the channel's connections sees the **Connect** button.
By default that is an **Editor** and the **Broadcaster**.
A moderator sees "Not connected" with no button.

The roles are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).

## Connect Kick or YouTube

You can connect from the **Home** page or from the **Stream** page.
Both start the same connection.

1. Open **Home** in the sidebar.
   The **Connections** panel lists Twitch, Kick and YouTube.
2. Choose **Connect** on the row of the platform.
   The panel asks "Connect Kick to this channel?" or "Connect YouTube to this channel?".
3. Choose **Connect** again to confirm.
   A new window opens on the platform's site.
4. Sign in on the platform and approve the connection.
   The window closes, and the row shows the account name.

On the **Stream** page, the same button is in the **Where it goes** panel of the **Overview** tab.
There the panel asks "Open Kick to connect?" or "Open YouTube to connect?".

When the browser blocks the new window, the whole tab goes to the platform instead.
When the connection fails, the dashboard shows "The connect did not work:" with the reason, and "Try again."

What the bot does with a YouTube connection is on [YouTube](/reference/integrations/youtube).

## Read the connection health on the Home page

The **Connections** panel on **Home** has two groups.

### The platforms

Each platform row has a dot, the platform name and one line.

| Line | Dot | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Not connected" | gray | The channel has no connection to this platform. |
| the account and "· Live" | green | The platform is connected and the channel is live there. |
| the account and "· Offline" | gray | The platform is connected and the channel is not live there. |
| the account only | gray | The stream state has not loaded yet. |

### Bot and services

The **Bot and services** group shows status only, with no button.

| Row | Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bot account** | "Connected" | The bot account is connected. The row names the account. |
| **Bot account** | "Not in chat" | The bot account is not connected. |
| a service, for example Spotify | "Connected" | The service is connected and works. |
| a service | "Sign in again" | The service is connected, but it needs a new sign-in. |
| a service | "Not connected" | The service is not connected. |

Which account the bot types from is on [Bot account](/reference/getting-started/bot-account).
You connect a service on the **Integrations** settings page.

When the panel cannot load, it shows "Connections could not load." and a way to try again.

## Read the platforms on the Stream page

The **Where it goes** panel is on the **Overview** tab of the **Stream** page.
It says "Your stream info goes to every platform you connect."
The title, category and tags you set there are on [Stream page](/reference/community/stream-page).

| Row | Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Twitch | "Connected" | Always connected. The row names the channel's account. |
| Kick or YouTube | "Connected" | Connected. The row names the account. |
| Kick or YouTube | "Not connected" | Not connected. A person who may change connections sees **Connect** beside the badge. |
| X Live | "Not available yet" | "X Live support is not built yet." |

When the panel cannot load, it shows "The connections could not load."

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**You do not see the Connect button.**
Your role may not change the channel's connections.
Ask the broadcaster or an editor to connect the platform.

::

::Troubleshooting

**A service shows "Sign in again".**
The service is connected, but it needs a new sign-in.
Choose **Reconnect** for it on the **Integrations** settings page.

::
