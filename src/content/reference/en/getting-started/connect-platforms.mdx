**Home** is the first page in the sidebar.
Its **Connections** panel lists Twitch, Kick and YouTube for your channel, and under them the bot account and the services.
Twitch is already connected, because you signed in with it, as [Sign in](/reference/getting-started/sign-in) explains.
Kick and YouTube each connect from a **Connect** button on their own row.

To see that button, you need the role that may connect and disconnect integrations.
By default that is an **Editor** or the **Broadcaster**.
The broadcaster can change that role on the **Permissions** page in **Settings**.
You also need your own account on the platform you connect.

## Connect Kick or YouTube

1. On **Home**, find the platform's row in the **Connections** panel. Its line reads "Not connected", and the row has a **Connect** button.
2. Choose the row's **Connect** button. The row now asks "Connect YouTube to this channel?", or the same for Kick. Beside the question are a second **Connect** button and a **Cancel** button.
3. Choose the **Connect** button beside the question. The platform's sign-in opens in a new window. While that window is open, the row shows a **Done** button. You do not need to press it.
4. In the new window, sign in to the platform and approve the connection. The window says "Connected" and closes by itself. On Home, the **Done** button goes away, and the row shows your account name on that platform.

If you close the window before you approve, nothing connects.
Choose **Done** on the row to bring the **Connect** button back.

If the browser blocks the new window, the dashboard tab itself goes to the platform.
After you approve, the tab comes back to the page you started from, and the row shows your account name.

## Read the status on Home

Each platform row in the **Connections** panel has a dot, the platform name and one line:

- "Not connected" with a gray dot: the channel has no connection to this platform.
- Your account name and Live, with a green dot: you are live on that platform now.
- Your account name and Offline, with a gray dot: you are not broadcasting there. The connection is fine.
- Only your account name, with a gray dot: connected, and the stream state has not loaded yet.

The Twitch row works the same way, with the name of the channel you have open.

Under the platform rows, the **Bot and services** group has a badge on each row and no button:

- **Bot account** with "Connected": the bot can chat in this channel, and the row names the account it types from.
- **Bot account** with "Not in chat": the bot has no working account to chat with in this channel. Only the broadcaster may read the bot's status, so for every other role it always reads "Not in chat". [Bot account](/reference/getting-started/bot-account) explains how to connect one.
- A service, such as Spotify, with "Connected" or "Not connected".
  A service whose saved sign-in no longer works also reads "Not connected", so Home never says "Sign in again". Renew it as [Renew a service](#renew-a-service) shows.

## Other routes

### Renew a service

An Editor reaches **Integrations** only through **Open connected services** on the **YouTube** tab of **Analytics** or the **YouTube members** tab of **People**.
That link shows while YouTube is not connected, or while the bot may not read your YouTube analytics (on **Analytics**) or your YouTube members (on **People**).
Then an Editor renews the service like this:

1. Choose **Analytics** in the sidebar.
2. Choose the **YouTube** tab.
3. Choose **Open connected services**. The **Integrations** page opens.
4. Choose **Reconnect** on the service's row, and sign in again in the new window.
5. When the window closes, choose **Back to dashboard** in the sidebar. Home opens, and the service's badge reads "Connected".

At any other time, the broadcaster renews it, because only the broadcaster sees **Settings** in the sidebar:

1. Choose **Settings**, the last entry in the sidebar. The sidebar changes to the list of settings pages.
2. Under the **Connections** heading of that list, choose **Integrations**.
3. Choose **Reconnect** on the service's row, and sign in again in the new window.
4. When the window closes, choose **Back to dashboard**, the first entry in the sidebar. Home opens, and the service's badge reads "Connected".

### Connect from Stream

**Stream** is the page after **Chat** in the sidebar.
On its **Overview** tab, the **Where it goes** panel has Kick and YouTube rows with the same **Connect** button and steps.
There the question reads "Open YouTube to connect?", and a load error reads "The connections could not load."

Each platform has a badge instead of a dot: "Connected" next to the account name, or "Not connected".
Twitch always reads "Connected".
This panel does not say whether you are live.

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**You do not see the Connect button.**
Your role may not connect integrations.
Ask the broadcaster or an editor to connect the platform.

**The row shows "That did not work." after you confirm.**
The bot could not start the connection, so no window opened.
Choose **Done** on the row, then start again from step 2.

**A message starting "The connect did not work:" shows as the window closes.**
The platform did not finish the connection, and the message names the reason.
Choose **Done** on the row, then start again from step 2.

**The panel says the connections could not load.**
Choose the panel's **Try again** button.

::
