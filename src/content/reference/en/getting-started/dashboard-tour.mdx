After you [sign in](/reference/getting-started/sign-in), you move between the pages of your channel with the sidebar.
**Home** is the first page in the sidebar.

## The sidebar

The sidebar lists the pages of the channel in groups.
The channel switcher is at the top of the sidebar.
Your account menu is at the bottom of the sidebar.
On a narrow window, both are in a bar at the top (see [On a smaller screen](#on-a-smaller-screen)).

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| (no heading) | **Home** |
| (no heading) | **People** |
| **Live** | **Chat**, **Viewer chat**, **Stream**, **Analytics**, **Polls**, **OBS**, **VTube Studio** |
| **Music and voice** | **Music**, **Text to speech**, **Media share**, **Sounds** |
| **Build** | **Commands**, **Triggers and timers**, **Voice triggers**, **Pick lists**, **Event responses**, **Pipelines**, **Overlays**, **Scripts**, **Library**, **Gallery** |
| **Loyalty** | **Channel points**, **Economy**, **Games**, **Giveaways**, **Quotes**, **Supporters** |
| (no heading) | **Settings** |

A group with two or more pages has a heading.
Click the heading to fold or unfold the group.
The browser remembers which groups you folded.

The group that holds the open page always stays open.

### Pages that are not built yet

**Library**, **Gallery** and **Games** are in the sidebar.
They are not built yet.
Each one opens a page that says **Coming soon**.

### Who sees which page

You see a page in the sidebar only when your role may open it.
The table shows the defaults for the main pages.

| Page | Who may open it by default |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | A moderator and up. The broadcaster may let a VIP open it too. |
| **Chat** | A moderator and up. The broadcaster may let a VIP open it too. |
| **Viewer chat** | Any signed-in viewer. It leaves the sidebar for someone who may open **Chat**. |
| **People** | A moderator and up. |
| **Settings** | The broadcaster only. |

Every other page has its own default.
The roles and their order are on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).
The broadcaster changes who may open each page there.

Right after the dashboard loads, every page can show for a moment.
The pages you may not open then leave the sidebar.

### The settings sidebar

**Settings** replaces the sidebar with a list of settings pages.

| Group | What it holds, for example |
| --- | --- |
| **You** | **Readability** |
| **Bot** | **Bot basics**, **Bot personality**, **Features** |
| **Connections** | **Integrations**, **Discord** |
| **Developers** | **API and devices**, **Webhooks** |
| **Access** | **Permissions**, **Spam defense**, **Banned people** |
| **Account** | **Resource limits**, **Your data**, **Channel management** |

**Back to dashboard** at the top leaves the settings.
It opens **Home**, or the first page your role may open.

Anyone who is signed in may open **Readability**, from the account menu.

### On a smaller screen

The sidebar changes with the width of the window.

| Window width | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| 840 px and wider | The full sidebar. |
| 600 to 839 px | A narrow column of icons. Hover an icon to read its name. A bar at the top holds the channel switcher and the account menu. |
| Under 600 px | A bar of pages at the bottom of the screen. A bar at the top holds the channel switcher and the account menu. |

The bottom bar shows **Home**, **Chat**, **Stream** and **Commands** first, when your role may open them.
When you may open more than five pages, the bar shows four pages and **More**.
**More** opens a drawer with the other pages, in their groups.

## The Home page

**Home** shows what happens in the channel now.
Its header says **Live on** and the platforms, or **Offline**.

| When the channel is | The header shows | The main button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live | How long you are live, the viewers, the followers and the **Category** | **Open chat** opens the [Chat page](/reference/community/chat-page). |
| Offline | When the last stream ended, and how long it was | **Edit stream info** opens the [Stream page](/reference/community/stream-page). |

Under the header are five panels.
Each panel shows only to someone whose role may read its data.

| Panel | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Review queue** | Chat messages that Twitch holds for review. **Allow** lets a message into chat. **Deny** keeps it out. |
| **Needs attention** | Things that need you. **Open** goes to the page that fixes the item. **Dismiss** clears it. |
| **Connections** | Each platform, with its account and **Live** or **Offline**. A platform that is not connected shows **Not connected** and a **Connect** button. [Connect platforms](/reference/getting-started/connect-platforms) explains this. |
| **Quick actions** | Live tools for the stream (see the next table). |
| **Happening now** | The newest channel events, such as follows, subscriptions, cheers and raids. When the channel is offline, this panel is **Last stream**. |

The review queue is part of the [moderation tools](/reference/moderation/overview).

### Quick actions

| Row | What it shows | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Next ad break** | When the next Twitch ad break starts, and how many times you may still push it back | **Snooze 5 min** pushes the ad break back |
| **Song queue** | How many songs wait | **Open queue** |
| **Timers** | How many timers are on, and which one fires next | **Open timers** |
| **Poll or prediction** | The poll or prediction that runs now | **Start a poll** or **Start a prediction** |
| **Suggested shoutouts** | Chatters of this stream who may be worth a [shoutout](/reference/moderation/shoutouts) | **Shoutout** or **Not now** |

Polls and predictions are explained on [Polls and predictions](/reference/economy/polls-and-predictions).

<details>
<summary>Connections: the bot and its services</summary>

Under **Bot and services**, the **Connections** panel shows the [bot account](/reference/getting-started/bot-account) and each service the bot uses.
The bot account shows **Connected** or **Not in chat**.
A service shows **Connected**, **Sign in again** or **Not connected**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Replay a past event</summary>

Each event in **Happening now** has a **Replay** button.
It shows the event again: the bot's chat line, the voice and the overlay alerts.

A moderator and up may replay an event.
**All events** at the top of the panel opens the full list.

</details>

## The header tools

Two tools sit in the page header.
They show to a moderator and up.

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Shield on** / **Shield off** | Turns Twitch Shield mode on or off for the channel. Shield mode applies the protections you set up for it on Twitch. |
| **Action required** | Opens the inbox. A number shows how many items wait. |

### Shield mode

The button shows the current state: **Shield on** or **Shield off**.
Click it to switch.
On a window 768 px or wider, the change goes to Twitch at once.

On a window under 768 px wide, a dialog asks first.
Choose **Turn on** or **Turn off**.

By default, a moderator can see the button.
Only a lead moderator and up may change it.
The broadcaster changes this on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).

### The Action required inbox

The inbox lists things that need you.
Each item has **Dismiss**.
Most items also have **Open**.
When nothing waits, it says "Nothing needs your attention."

| Kind of item | What it is | How long it stays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A notice from the bot | For example, a connection that needs a new sign-in, or messages held for review | Until you dismiss it. It comes back when the problem happens again. |
| **Not saved** | A change you made that did not save. The screen went back to the earlier value. | Until you dismiss it or reload the page. |

For a **Not saved** item, choose **Open** to go back to the page, and make the change again.

## The channel switcher

The channel switcher at the top of the sidebar shows the open channel, with **Live** or **Offline**.

1. Click the channel name.
   A list opens with **Your channel** and **Channels you moderate**.
   Live channels come first.
2. Type in **Find a channel**.
   The list keeps the channels that match.
3. Choose a channel.
   The same page opens for that channel.

You may have one item open, such as one [pipeline](/reference/commands/pipelines) in its editor.
Then the new channel opens on **Home** instead.

<details>
<summary>Find a channel you do not manage</summary>

Under your own channels, the list shows **Recent channels**.
When you type in **Find a channel**, it also shows **Visit a channel** and **More on Twitch**.

| Group | What it holds | When you choose one |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Recent channels** | Up to 5 channels you opened last on this device | The channel opens. |
| **Visit a channel** | Channels this bot serves | The channel opens as a viewer sees it, in the [viewer pages](/reference/community/viewer-pages). |
| **More on Twitch** | Twitch channels this bot does not serve | The chat of that channel opens on a viewer chat page of the dashboard. |

</details>

## The account menu

The account menu at the bottom of the sidebar shows your name.
Under it is **Broadcaster** in your own channel, or **Moderator** in a channel you moderate.

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Readability** | Opens the [Readability](/reference/settings/readability) settings for this device. |
| **View as moderator** | Shows the dashboard as a moderator sees it. |
| **View as viewer** | Opens the viewer pages of your channel, as a viewer sees them. |
| **Language** | Switches the language of the dashboard. |
| **Fullscreen** | Hides the browser bars. It shows only when the browser can do this. |
| **Sign out** | Signs you out and opens the sign-in page. |

Only the broadcaster sees **View as moderator** and **View as viewer**.

### View as another role

When you view as another role, a bar at the top of every page says "Viewing as a moderator" or "Viewing as a viewer".
It shows on the dashboard and on the viewer pages.
Choose **Exit** in the bar to go back to your own view.

For **View as viewer**, **Exit** also returns you to the page you left.

The view stays on after a reload, until you choose **Exit**.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press `?` to open **Keyboard shortcuts**.
It lists the keys that the open page answers to.
Press `?` again to close it.

The list changes with the page.
`?` does nothing while you type in a text field.

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**A page is missing from the sidebar.**
Your role may not open it.
The broadcaster can change that on the **Permissions** settings page.

::

::Troubleshooting

**The page says "No access to" a channel.**
You do not manage that channel.
Pick one of your channels in the switcher, or ask the streamer to make you a moderator.

::

::Troubleshooting

**Shield on or Shield off does not show in the header.**
The button shows only after the dashboard has read the Shield mode state from Twitch.
Until then, the header has no **Shield on** or **Shield off** button and no message.

::
