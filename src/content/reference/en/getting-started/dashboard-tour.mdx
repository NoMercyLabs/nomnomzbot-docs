After you [sign in](/reference/getting-started/sign-in) for the first time, the dashboard opens on your own channel.
If you have no channel of your own, it opens on a channel you moderate.
After that, the browser remembers the last channel you picked in the channel switcher.

The sidebar lists only the pages your role in that channel may open, so a friend with another role sees a different list.

For a first visit, open **Home** to see the channel right now, then go to **Commands** to [make your first command](/reference/getting-started/first-command).
Both pages open for a moderator and up by default.

## The sidebar groups

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| (no heading) | **Home** |
| (no heading) | **People** |
| **Live** | **Chat**, **Viewer chat**, **Stream**, **Analytics**, **Polls**, **OBS**, **VTube Studio** |
| **Music and voice** | **Music**, **Text to speech**, **Media share**, **Sounds** |
| **Build** | **Commands**, **Triggers and timers**, **Voice triggers**, **Pick lists**, **Event responses**, **Pipelines**, **Overlays**, **Scripts**, **Library**, **Gallery** |
| **Loyalty** | **Channel points**, **Economy**, **Games**, **Giveaways**, **Quotes**, **Supporters** |
| (no heading) | **Settings** |

A group shows a heading only when two or more of its pages are open to you.
So **Home**, **People** and **Settings** never have one, and a group such as **Loyalty** loses its heading when your role opens only one of its pages.

Click a heading to fold or unfold its group.
A group with a heading starts folded, unless it holds the page you have open.
The group of the open page stays open, and the browser remembers which groups you opened or folded.

**Settings** swaps the sidebar for a list of settings pages.
**Back to dashboard** at the top of that list brings you back.

## Why a page is missing

Each sidebar page needs one permission in the selected channel.
You see the page only when your role holds that permission.
A page that needs no permission shows to everyone.

By default:

- A moderator and up opens **Home**, **Chat**, **Commands** and **People**.
- A VIP opens no page beyond **Viewer chat**, because the dashboard holds only your own channel and channels you moderate.
- Everyone opens **Viewer chat**, which leaves the sidebar once you may open **Chat**.
- Only the broadcaster opens **Settings**.

The broadcaster changes these on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions), so the same role can see different pages in two channels.

Until your permissions for the channel have loaded, the sidebar shows every page.
Then the pages your role may not open drop out.
If a page vanishes, check the channel in the channel switcher first.

## Home

**Home** shows the channel now.
While you are streaming, its header says **Live on** and the platforms, with how long you have been streaming, the viewers and the followers.
Offline, it says **Offline** and when the last stream ended.
The main button is **Open chat** while you are streaming, for the [Chat page](/reference/community/chat-page).
Offline, it is **Edit stream info**, for the [Stream page](/reference/community/stream-page).

Below the header are the **Review queue**, **Needs attention**, **Connections**, **Quick actions** and **Happening now** panels.
Offline, the last panel is **Last stream**.
Each panel shows only when your role may read its data, so a moderator and the broadcaster can see different panels.

## The header tools

A moderator and up sees two tools in the page header, the row with the page title.
They sit at the end of that row, after the page's own buttons, on every page.

### Shield mode

The Shield mode button shows the Shield mode state of the channel on Twitch, and its label is the state now, not the action.
**Shield off** means Shield mode is off, and a click turns it on.
**Shield on**, with a check mark on the shield icon, means it is on, and a click turns it off.
When the page header is narrower than 448 px, only the icon shows.
That limit is the width of the page header, not of the window, because the header shares the window with the sidebar or the column of icons.

The button appears only after the dashboard has read the state from Twitch.
After you switch channels, it can be gone for a moment while the dashboard reads the state of the new channel.

Changing it takes a lead moderator and up by default.
A lead moderator is a moderator whom the broadcaster gave the **Lead moderator** role on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions), under **Team**.
The broadcaster can also give this change to one person on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions), under **Per person**.
The account menu says **Moderator** for a lead moderator too, so ask the broadcaster whether you may change Shield mode.
A plain moderator can still click the button, but the bot refuses the change.
No message shows, and the label stays as it was.

On a window 768 px or wider, one click switches Shield mode on Twitch at once.
On a narrower window, where the top bar and the page header stay at the top while you scroll, a dialog asks first and names the channel.
Click **Turn on** or **Turn off** to make the change, or **Cancel** to leave Shield mode as it is.
A plain moderator gets this dialog too, and the bot still refuses the change.

### Action required

The **Action required** button sits next to the Shield mode button and shows an inbox icon.
It opens the **Action required** list, such as a connection that needs a new sign-in or chat messages held for review.
When items wait, a number next to the inbox icon shows how many.
Each item has **Dismiss**, and most also have **Open**, which goes to the page that fixes it.
A dismissed item comes back only when the same problem happens again.
A change that did not save shows here too, as **Not saved**, so you can open the page and try again.
When the list is empty, it says "Nothing needs your attention."

The **Needs attention** panel on **Home** shows the same items, apart from **Not saved**, so an item you dismiss in one place is gone from the other.
The **Review queue** panel on **Home** is where you **Allow** or **Deny** each held message.
The **Action required** list only tells you that messages are held.

## The channel switcher

The channel switcher shows the open channel.
On a window 840 px or wider, it also says **Live** or **Offline**, which tells whether that channel is streaming now.
While you view as another role, the channel switcher shows only the channel name and opens no list, so you cannot switch channels.
On **Chat** at 1080 to 1279 px, an open **Display** panel folds the sidebar to the column of icons and hides the channel switcher and the account menu, so close **Display** to get them back.

1. Click the channel switcher.
   On a window 840 px or wider, it is at the top of the sidebar.
   On a narrower window, it is at the left of the top bar.
   The list shows **Your channel** and **Channels you moderate**, with the channels that are streaming first.
   Below them, the list can also hold **Recent channels**, **Visit a channel** and **More on Twitch**.
2. Optional: type part of a name in **Find a channel** to narrow the list.
3. Choose a channel.
   For a channel under **Your channel** or **Channels you moderate**, the same page opens for that channel.
   For a channel in one of the other groups, the viewer chat of that channel opens.
   When you have one item open, such as one pipeline in its editor, the new channel opens on **Home** instead.
   When your role in the new channel may not open that page, the first page of your sidebar opens instead.

## On a smaller window

| What you see | Window width |
| --- | --- |
| The full sidebar, with the channel switcher at its top | 840 px and wider |
| A column of icons at the left | 600 to 839 px |
| A bar of pages at the bottom | Under 600 px |

Below 840 px, a top bar sits above the page header and holds the channel switcher and the account menu.
Under 768 px, the top bar and the page header stay at the top while you scroll, and the channel switcher list rises from the bottom of the window.

The column of icons shows every page of your sidebar at once.
A line separates the groups, and there are no headings to fold.
Point at an icon to read its name.
On a touch screen, a tap opens the page at once and shows no name.

The bottom bar holds up to five pages.
When your role opens more, it holds four and the last slot is **More**, which opens the rest.
The bar keeps **Home**, **Chat**, **Stream** and **Commands** first, as far as your role may open them.
**More** lists the other pages under the same group headings, with no folding.
Choose a page to open it, or click **Close**, the X at the top right of **More**, to put it away.

**Settings** swaps the pages for the settings list in the column and the bottom bar too.
**Back to dashboard** is then the first icon at the top of the column and the first slot at the left of the bottom bar.

## The account menu

The account menu at the bottom of the sidebar shows your name and your role in the channel.
It says **Broadcaster** in your own channel and **Moderator** in every other channel.
Below 840 px, the account menu is your picture at the right of the top bar.

The menu holds:

- **Readability**, which sets how text looks for you on every page, saved on this device.
- **Documentation**, which opens this guide.
- **Viewer pages**, which opens the pages a viewer sees, outside the dashboard.
- **Language**.
- **Fullscreen**, when your browser supports it.
- **Sign out**.

Only the broadcaster also sees **View as moderator** and **View as viewer**.
They show the dashboard the way that role sees it, with a bar at the top that says "Viewing as a moderator" or "Viewing as a viewer".
Choose **Exit** in that bar to go back.

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**The page says "No access to" and a channel name.**
This shows in place of the page when you open a link to a channel you do not moderate.
Under the lock icon, it says: "You do not manage this channel. Pick one of your channels in the switcher, or ask the streamer to make you a moderator."

::
