Helpers on this page: `{schedule.*}`.

These helpers read the channel's stream schedule.
Use them to tell viewers when the next stream starts.

Each row is its own example, so the rows on this page do not all come from one moment.

| Helper | What it gives | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `{schedule.ical}` | The link to the channel schedule as a calendar file (iCalendar) | `https://api.twitch.tv/helix/schedule/icalendar?broadcaster_id=123456` |
| `{schedule.link}` | The link to the channel schedule page | `https://twitch.tv/samplechannel/schedule` |
| `{schedule.list}` | The next scheduled streams with their start times, for example schedule.list:3: Fri 20:00 Bot night · Sun 14:00 Chill | `{schedule.list:3}` gives `Fri 20:00 Bot night · Sun 14:00 Chill · Tue 20:00 Bot night` |
| `{schedule.next.at}` | The day and start time of the next scheduled stream: Friday 20:00 | `Friday 20:00` |
| `{schedule.next.date}` | The day and date of the next scheduled stream: Friday 30 October | `Friday 30 October` |
| `{schedule.next.duration}` | How long the next scheduled stream lasts: 3h | `3h` |
| `{schedule.next.full}` | The next scheduled stream in one line: next stream in 4 days, Friday 20:00 | `next stream in 4 days, Friday 20:00` |
| `{schedule.next.game}` | The category of the next scheduled stream | `Software and Game Development` |
| `{schedule.next.in}` | How long until the next scheduled stream: 4 days | `4 days` |
| `{schedule.next.isRecurring}` | true when the next scheduled stream repeats every week, false when it is a one-off | `true` |
| `{schedule.next.relative}` | How long until the next scheduled stream, as a phrase: in 4 days | `in 4 days` |
| `{schedule.next.title}` | The title of the next scheduled stream | `Bot night` |
| `{schedule.next.viewerTime}` | The day and start time of the next scheduled stream in another time zone, for example schedule.next.viewerTime:America/New_York: Fri 15:00 | `{schedule.next.viewerTime:America/New_York}` gives `Fri 15:00` |
| `{schedule.today}` | The scheduled streams of today with their start times; empty when there is none: 21:00 Quick one | `20:00 Bot night` |
| `{schedule.vacation}` | The schedule break that is planned or running; empty when there is none: Fri 6 Nov - Sun 15 Nov | `Fri 6 Nov - Sun 15 Nov` |
| `{schedule.vacation.until}` | The day the schedule break ends; empty when there is no break: Sunday 15 November | `Sunday 15 November` |
| `{schedule.week}` | The days this week (Monday to Sunday) with a scheduled stream: Fri, Sun | `Fri, Sun` |
