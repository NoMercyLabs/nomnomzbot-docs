Helpers on this page: `{ad.*}`.

These helpers read the ad breaks of the channel.
Use them to tell chat when the next ad starts.

Each row is its own example, so the rows on this page do not all come from one moment.

| Helper | What it gives | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `{ad.automatic}` | Whether the ad break was automatic ("true") or requester-triggered ("false") | `true` |
| `{ad.duration}` | The ad break length (e.g. "3 minutes") | `3 minutes` |
| `{ad.isScheduled}` | true while an ad break is planned, false when none is | `true` |
| `{ad.last.ago}` | How long ago the last ad break ran: 48m | `48m` |
| `{ad.last.at}` | The clock time of the last ad break in the channel time zone: 14:42 | `14:42` |
| `{ad.next.at}` | The clock time of the next ad break in the channel time zone: 15:42 | `15:42` |
| `{ad.next.duration}` | How long the next ad break lasts: 1m 30s | `1m 30s` |
| `{ad.next.in}` | How long until the next ad break, rounded to the minute: 12m | `12m` |
| `{ad.next.in.long}` | How long until the next ad break in words: 12 minutes | `12 minutes` |
| `{ad.next.seconds}` | How many seconds until the next ad break: 720 | `720` |
| `{ad.prerollFree}` | How much pre-roll free time is left: 20m | `20m` |
| `{ad.seconds}` | The length of the coming ad break in seconds (e.g. "90") | `300` |
| `{ad.snoozes.left}` | How many ad snoozes are left: 2 | `2` |
| `{ad.snoozes.refreshIn}` | How long until the ad snoozes are refilled: 34m | `34m` |
| `{ad.when}` | When the next ad starts (e.g. "in ~3 minutes") | `in ~5 minutes` |
