Helpers on this page: `{lookup.*}`.

These helpers look up facts outside the channel: a game, the weather, a time zone, a currency rate or a translation.

Each row is its own example, so the rows on this page do not all come from one moment.

| Helper | What it gives | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `{lookup.currency}` | An amount in another currency, written `{lookup.currency:amount\|from\|to}`. Example: 10 EUR = 11.62 USD | `{lookup.currency:10\|EUR\|USD}` gives `10 EUR = 11.62 USD` |
| `{lookup.currency.date}` | The day of the latest exchange rates, with an optional from\|to pair (EUR\|USD by default): 2026-10-26 | `2026-10-26` |
| `{lookup.currency.rate}` | The exchange rate between two currencies, written `{lookup.currency.rate:from\|to}`. Example: 1.162 | `{lookup.currency.rate:EUR\|USD}` gives `1.162` |
| `{lookup.game}` | The box art link of a Twitch category, written `{lookup.game:name.boxart}`. Example: https://static-cdn.jtvnw.net/ttv-boxart/27471-285x380.jpg | `https://static-cdn.jtvnw.net/ttv-boxart/27471-285x380.jpg` |
| `{lookup.game.top}` | The most watched Twitch category right now: Just Chatting | `Just Chatting` |
| `{lookup.time}` | The local time in a city, written `{lookup.time:city}` with the optional fields date and offset. Off when the bot runs in saas mode. Example: 23:30 | `{lookup.time:Tokyo}` gives `23:30` |
| `{lookup.translate}` | A translation, written `{lookup.translate:language\|text}` (from English) or `{lookup.translate:from\|to\|text}`. Example: goedemorgen | `{lookup.translate:nl\|good morning}` gives `goedemorgen` |
| `{lookup.translate.chat}` | The text of the command (or the chat message) translated into a language, written `{lookup.translate.chat:language}`. The text is taken to be in the channel language. Example: good morning | `good morning` |
| `{lookup.twitch}` | A fact about any Twitch user, written `{lookup.twitch:login.field}`. Fields: game, title, isLive, viewers, display, avatar, createdAt, uptime, lastGame, url. The fields followers and followage:channel work for your own channel only. Example: Minecraft | `{lookup.twitch:viewertwo.game}` gives `Minecraft` |
| `{lookup.weather}` | The weather in a city, written `{lookup.weather:city.field}`. Fields: temp, temp.f, feels, condition, emoji, wind, humidity, tomorrow. Without a field it prints the city, the temperature and the condition. Off when the bot runs in saas mode. Example: Amsterdam: 12 °C, light rain | `{lookup.weather:Amsterdam}` gives `Amsterdam: 12 °C, light rain` |
