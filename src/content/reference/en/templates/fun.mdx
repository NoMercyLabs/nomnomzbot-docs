Helpers on this page: `{fun.*}`.

You type these helpers into a reply the same way as any other helper.
Nearly all of them work in commands, event responses, timers, pipeline steps, Discord notifications and webhook bodies, and in a viewer's own text when the [Viewer Templates setting and permissions](/reference/settings/features) allow it.
The **Use in** column of the table at the end lists the fields for each helper.

Most fun helpers draw a new result every time the reply runs, so every example on this page shows one possible result.
The ratings are the exception: they give the same answer all day.
An input the helper cannot use, such as a word where it wants a number, leaves the helper in the reply exactly as you wrote it, braces and all.

## Roll dice

`{fun.dice}` rolls one six-sided die and gives the number.
Put a number after a colon for a die with that many sides, so `{fun.dice:20}` gives a number from 1 to 20.
Write the count, a `d` and the sides for more than one die: `{fun.dice:2d6}` rolls two six-sided dice and gives the total, such as `7`.

A die has 2 to 1000 sides, and one roll has 1 to 100 dice.
In a viewer's own text, a roll of more than 20 dice rolls 20.

`{fun.dice.detail:2d6}` shows each roll and then the total, such as `2 + 1 = 3`.
With one die it shows just the number.

`{fun.dice.emoji}` shows a die face such as `⚄`, and `{fun.dice.emoji:3}` shows three faces in a row, up to 20.

## Flip, draw and spin

`{fun.coin}` gives `heads` or `tails`, and `{fun.coin.emoji}` puts a coin in front, as in `🪙 heads`.
`{fun.card}` draws one card from a deck of 52, such as `Q♥` or `10♠`.
`{fun.roulette}` gives `click`, or `bang` one time in six.

`{fun.rps}` picks a hand, such as `🪨 rock`.
`{fun.rps.vs:rock}` plays against the hand you give it, which is `rock`, `paper` or `scissors`.
It gives the bot's hand and the result for your side: `paper — you lose`, `scissors — you win` or `rock — a draw`.

`{fun.slots}` spins three reels from 🍒, 🍋, 🔔, 🍇, ⭐ and 7️⃣, such as `🍒 🍋 🍒`.
`{fun.slots.win}` then gives `true` when all three reels of that spin match, and `false` when they do not.
Put it after the spin in the same reply.
With no spin before it, it gives `false`.
Combined with the if helper, one reply can spin and answer:

```
{fun.slots} {if:{fun.slots.win}|JACKPOT!|Better luck next time}
```

## Ratings that stay the same all day

These helpers give a score that does not change when someone asks again the same day.
A new day starts at midnight in your channel's time zone, and then each score changes.
Capitals do not matter, so `Pizza` and `pizza` get the same score.

`{fun.rate:pizza}` rates the word from 1 to 10, as in `8/10`.
Every viewer gets the same rating for the same word.
To rate what the viewer types after the command, put that text in the helper, as in `{fun.rate:{args}}`.

`{fun.percent:luck}` gives a score from 0 to 100, as in `8%`.
It also depends on the viewer, so two viewers asking about `luck` get their own scores.
It needs a trigger that brings a viewer, so it does not work in timers or Discord notifications.

`{fun.love:{user.display}|{target.displayname}}` gives a score from 0 to 100 for two names with a pipe between them, as in `22%`.
The order of the names does not change the score.

`{fun.ship:Alexander|Bo}` blends two names into one: the first half of the first name and the last third of the second.
It gives `Alexander + Bo = Alexo`.
The ship name is not random, so the same two names always give the same blend.

## Answer with the 8-ball

`{fun.8ball}` needs a pick list named `8ball` in your channel.
Make it on the [Pick lists](/reference/commands/pick-lists) page and add your answers to it.
The helper then gives one answer from that list each time, such as `It is certain`.
With no list of that name, or an empty list, the helper stays in the reply as you wrote it.

The ready-made `!8ball` command keeps its answers in the command itself and does not make this list.
`{fun.8ball}` reads your channel's lists, so it does not work in a viewer's own text.

## Countdowns, bars and text art

`{fun.countdown:3}` counts down to 1 as `3… 2… 1…`, from any number up to 20.
`{fun.meter:60}` draws a bar of 10 blocks with the percent, as in `██████░░░░ 60%`, the same as `{meter}`.

`{fun.flip:table}` flips a table and turns your text upside down: `(╯°□°)╯︵ ǝlqɐʇ`.
`{fun.unflip}`, `{fun.shrug}` and `{fun.lenny}` always give the same face.
`{fun.random.emoji}`, `{fun.random.animal}`, `{fun.random.food}` and `{fun.random.color.name}` each pick from a small fixed set of 12.
