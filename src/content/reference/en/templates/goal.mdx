Helpers on this page: `{goal.*}`.

These helpers read the creator goals that are active on your Twitch channel.
Every goal helper in one reply reads the same list of goals, so the numbers in that reply always agree.
They work in commands, event responses, timers, pipeline steps, Discord notifications and webhook bodies.
None of them take arguments.

## Before you use them

A goal has to be running on Twitch, or there is nothing to read.
When the channel has no active goal of the kind a helper asks for, the reply shows that helper exactly as you wrote it, braces and all.
The same happens when the bot cannot read the goals from Twitch.

So put goal helpers in a reply you use while a goal is running, such as a `!goal` command, rather than in a timer that posts all the time.

## Pick the goal

The middle part of the name picks which kind of goal the helper reads:

- `followers` reads a follower goal.
- `subs` reads a subscription goal. That covers both kinds of subscription goal Twitch offers.
- `newSubs` reads a new-subscription goal. It has only `current` and `percent`.

When more than one active goal is of the same kind, the helper reads the first one Twitch lists.

## What each part gives

- `current` and `target` are the goal's numbers as Twitch has them, such as `1,234` and `1,500`.
- `remaining` is what is still needed, and `0` once the goal is reached, never a negative number.
- `percent` is the progress rounded to a whole number, such as `82%`. It keeps going past `100%` when the goal is beaten.
- `bar` draws 10 marks and adds the percent: `████████░░ 82%`. The bar stops at full, and the percent after it keeps counting.
- `description` is the text the streamer gave the follower goal on Twitch.

Numbers use the thousands separator of the channel's **Language**, and English when it is not set.

A goal with a target of `0` has nothing to measure against. Its `percent` and `bar` show as written, and `{goal.list}` leaves that goal out. Its `current`, `target` and `remaining` still show.
A follower goal without a description shows `{goal.followers.description}` as written.

## Show progress in a reply

A follower goal of 1,500, with 1,234 followers so far:

`Follower goal: {goal.followers.current} of {goal.followers.target}, {goal.followers.remaining} to go! {goal.followers.bar}`

gives `Follower goal: 1,234 of 1,500, 266 to go! ████████░░ 82%`.

To show every goal at once, use `{goal.list}`.
It names each active goal as `followers`, `subs` or `new subs` with its percent, in the order Twitch lists them, such as `followers 82%, subs 80%`.
When no goal can be shown, it prints as written.

## All goal helpers

The examples use a follower goal at 1,234 of 1,500 described as "Road to 1.5k", a subscription goal at 80 of 100, and a new-subscription goal at 6 of 20.

| Helper | What it gives | Example | Use in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `{goal.followers.bar}` | A bar of 10 marks for the follower goal, with its percent | `████████░░ 82%` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{goal.followers.current}` | The follower count of the follower goal | `1,234` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{goal.followers.description}` | The text the streamer gave the follower goal | `Road to 1.5k` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{goal.followers.percent}` | How far the follower goal is, as a whole percent | `82%` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{goal.followers.remaining}` | The followers still needed, never below 0 | `266` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{goal.followers.target}` | The target of the follower goal | `1,500` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{goal.list}` | Every active goal with its percent | `followers 82%, subs 80%, new subs 30%` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{goal.newSubs.current}` | The new subscriptions counted by the new-subscription goal | `6` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{goal.newSubs.percent}` | How far the new-subscription goal is, as a whole percent | `30%` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{goal.subs.bar}` | A bar of 10 marks for the subscription goal, with its percent | `████████░░ 80%` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{goal.subs.current}` | The current amount of the subscription goal | `80` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{goal.subs.percent}` | How far the subscription goal is, as a whole percent | `80%` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{goal.subs.remaining}` | What the subscription goal still needs, never below 0 | `20` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{goal.subs.target}` | The target of the subscription goal | `100` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
