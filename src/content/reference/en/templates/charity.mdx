Helpers on this page: `{charity.*}`.

These helpers read a charity campaign: its name, target and the amount raised.
Use them to show progress in chat, on an overlay or read aloud.

Each row is its own example, so the rows on this page do not all come from one moment.

| Helper | What it gives | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `{charity.bar}` | A bar of how far the campaign is: ████░░░░░░ 42% | `████░░░░░░ 42%` |
| `{charity.donations.count}` | How many donations the campaign has: 17 | `17` |
| `{charity.donations.latest}` | The newest donation, with the donor and the amount: ViewerOne (€10.00) | `ViewerOne (€10.00)` |
| `{charity.link}` | The website of the charity: https://www.samplecharity.org | `https://www.samplecharity.org` |
| `{charity.name}` | The name of the charity of the running campaign: Sample Charity | `Sample Charity` |
| `{charity.percent}` | How far the campaign is towards its target: 42% | `42%` |
| `{charity.raised}` | The money raised so far, with its currency: €420.00 | `€420.00` |
| `{charity.target}` | The money target of the campaign, with its currency: €1,000.00 | `€1,000.00` |
