Helpers on this page: `{date}`, `{date*}`, `{time}`, `{time.*}`.

These helpers work in commands, event responses, timers, pipeline steps, Discord notifications and webhook bodies.
They read the clock in your channel's time zone, so set that first.
In **Settings**, open **Bot basics** and choose a **Timezone**.
Until you do, every time here is UTC.
Month and day names follow **Language** on the same page, and English when it is not set.

## Write a date expression

`{date:...}` takes a source, then any number of steps, each after a pipe.
The steps run from left to right, and the output step, if you use one, comes last.
Without an output step you get the date and time as `yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm`, such as `2026-10-26 15:30`.

`{date:now|+1d|format:dddd}` starts at the channel's current time, moves one day ahead, and prints the weekday name.
An output step is one of `format`, `relative`, `ordinal`, `until`, `since` or `diff`.

## Pick the source

The source is the first part, before any pipe.

- `now` is the current time in your channel's time zone, and `utc` is the same moment in UTC.
- A date such as `2026-12-25`, or a date with a time such as `2026-12-25T20:00` or `2026-12-25 20:00`, is read as a time in your channel's time zone. A date alone is midnight.
- A timestamp that ends in `Z` or an offset such as `+02:00` is a fixed moment, and the bot shows it in your channel's time zone.
- A helper that holds a timestamp works too. `{date:{stream.startedAt}|format:HH:mm}` shows when the stream went live, in your time. While you are offline `{stream.startedAt}` is empty and there is no time to show, so use it in replies that run while you are live.

## Show another time zone

Use `tz` with a time zone name such as `America/New_York` or `Asia/Tokyo`.
It shows the same moment in that zone and leaves your channel's setting alone.
`{date:now|tz:America/New_York|format:HH:mm}` gives the time in New York.

Steps after `tz` work in the new zone, so the place you put it changes the answer.
`{date:now|next:friday|start:day|tz:America/New_York|format:ddd HH:mm}` takes midnight on Friday in your zone and shows it as New York time: `Thu 19:00` for a channel in Amsterdam.
With `tz` first, the same steps find midnight on Friday in New York instead.

## Move the date

Add or subtract time with a sign, a number and a unit: `y` years, `mo` months, `w` weeks, `d` days, `h` hours, `m` minutes, `s` seconds.
You can chain parts in one step, such as `+1y-2mo`, or write each in its own step.

Years, months, weeks and days move the calendar and keep the time of day.
Hours, minutes and seconds count real time.
The two only differ on the night the clocks change: in Amsterdam, `{date:2026-03-28T12:00|+1d}` lands on 12:00 the next day, and `+24h` lands on 13:00.
A month from 31 January is the last day of February.

`start` and `end` jump to the start or end of the `day`, `week`, `month` or `year`.
A week starts on Monday, and an end is 23:59:59.
`next` and `prev` jump to the next or previous weekday and keep the time of day.
Write the weekday in English, in full or as its first three letters or more, such as `next:fri`.

## Count down or count up

`until` counts from now to the date, `since` counts from the date to now, and `diff:<date>` counts between two dates.
Add a unit after a colon to choose the shape of the answer:

| Unit | What you get | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| None, or `human` | The two largest parts | `{date:now\|+2h+15m\|until}` gives `2 hours, 15 minutes` |
| `breakdown` | Every part, from years to seconds | `{date:2026-12-25\|until:breakdown}` gives `1 month, 28 days, 8 hours, 30 minutes` |
| `years`, `months`, `weeks`, `days` | Whole calendar units, rounded down | `{date:2025-09-10\|diff:2026-10-26:days}` gives `411 days` |
| `hours`, `minutes`, `seconds` | Whole units of real time, rounded down | `{date:now\|-90m\|since:minutes}` gives `90 minutes` |

The singular works too, such as `day`.
A count has no sign: `until` on a date that has passed gives the time since it.
To show which side of now a date is on, use `relative`, which gives text such as `3 days ago` or `2 hours from now`.

Hour counts follow real time, so the night the clocks go forward is `23 hours` and the night they go back is `25 hours`.
Count in `days` when you mean calendar days.

## Format the result

`format` takes date letters: `yyyy` the year, `MM` the month number, `MMMM` the month name, `dd` the day, `dddd` the weekday name, `HH` the hour from 00 to 23, `mm` the minutes and `ss` the seconds.
Put words in single quotes to print them as they are, and type `[o]`, with the brackets, for the day as an ordinal.
`{date:now|format:dddd 'the' [o] 'of' MMMM}` gives `Monday the 26th of October`.

A single letter picks a ready-made layout instead: `d`, `D`, `t`, `T`, `f`, `F`, `g` or `G`, laid out the way the channel language writes dates, or `s` for `2026-10-26T15:30:00`.
`lang` uses another language for one expression, such as `{date:now|lang:en|format:MMMM}` for `October` on a channel set to Dutch.

## When an expression is wrong

The reply shows the expression exactly as you wrote it, braces and all.
That happens when a step or unit is unknown (`+1x`), the source is not a date, the time zone or language is unknown, the weekday does not match one, or a step comes after the output step.

## All date and time helpers

The examples use a channel in Amsterdam on Monday 26 October 2026 at 15:30 local time.

| Helper | What it gives | Example | Use in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `{date}` | Today's date in your channel's time zone | `{date}` gives `2026-10-26` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>}` | The date and time of the source: `now`, `utc`, a date, a date and time, a timestamp, or a helper that holds one | `{date:now}` gives `2026-10-26 15:30` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>\|<+/-amount><unit>}` | Moves the date by years, months, weeks, days, hours, minutes or seconds | `{date:now\|+1d}` gives `2026-10-27 15:30` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>\|diff:<date>[:<unit>]}` | The time between the two dates | `{date:2025-09-10\|diff:2026-10-26:breakdown}` gives `1 year, 1 month, 16 days` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>\|end:<unit>}` | The end of the day, week, month or year, at 23:59:59 | `{date:now\|end:day\|format:yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm:ss}` gives `2026-10-26 23:59:59` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>\|format:<pattern>}` | The date in the layout you write with date letters | `{date:now\|format:dd/MM/yyyy}` gives `26/10/2026` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>\|lang:<language>}` | Month and day names in another language | `{date:now\|lang:en\|format:MMMM}` gives `October` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>\|next:<weekday>}` | The next such weekday, at the same time of day | `{date:now\|next:friday}` gives `2026-10-30 15:30` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>\|ordinal}` | The day of the month as an ordinal | `{date:now\|ordinal}` gives `26th` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>\|prev:<weekday>}` | The previous such weekday, at the same time of day | `{date:now\|prev:monday\|format:yyyy-MM-dd}` gives `2026-10-19` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>\|relative}` | How far the date is from now, in words | `{date:now\|-3d\|relative}` gives `3 days ago` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>\|since[:<unit>]}` | The time from the date until now | `{date:now\|-90m\|since:minutes}` gives `90 minutes` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>\|start:<unit>}` | The start of the day, week, month or year, at 00:00 | `{date:now\|start:month\|format:yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm}` gives `2026-10-01 00:00` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>\|tz:<zone>}` | The same moment in another time zone | `{date:now\|tz:America/New_York\|format:HH:mm}` gives `10:30` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{date:<source>\|until[:<unit>]}` | The time from now until the date | `{date:2026-12-25\|until:days}` gives `59 days` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{time}` | The current time in your channel's time zone | `{time}` gives `15:30:00` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
| `{time.utc}` | The current time in UTC | `{time.utc}` gives `14:30:00 UTC` | Commands, event responses, timers, pipelines, Discord, webhooks |
