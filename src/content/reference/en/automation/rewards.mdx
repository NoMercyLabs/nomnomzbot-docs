title: Channel-point rewards
description: Edit, copy, switch off and delete channel-point rewards, choose what the bot does when a viewer buys one, and work the queue of waiting requests.
---

You manage the channel's channel-point rewards on the **Channel points** page, in the **Loyalty** group of the sidebar.
A channel-point reward is something a Twitch viewer buys with the points they earn by watching.
The page has two panels: **Pending rewards** and **Rewards**.

## What the bot needs from Twitch

Channel-point rewards exist only on Twitch.
The channel must be connected to Twitch, as [Connect platforms](/reference/getting-started/connect-platforms) explains.

Twitch gives channel points only to an Affiliate or Partner channel.
For any other channel, the page shows "Channel points need Twitch Affiliate or Partner. These rewards are saved here and go live on Twitch when the channel is affiliate."
Then only the **Rewards** panel shows, and the on and off switches in it are off.

## Who can use this page

A **Moderator**, **Lead moderator**, **Editor** or **Broadcaster** may open the page and read it.
The **Broadcaster** may also let a **VIP** open it, on the **Permissions** settings page.

An **Editor** or the **Broadcaster** may change rewards and complete or reject requests.
Anyone else sees the lists only, with no switches, no **Edit** button and no queue buttons.
The roles are explained on [Permissions](/reference/settings/permissions).

## Read the reward list

The **Rewards** panel lists every reward of the channel, with the count at the top, for example "4 rewards".
When there is none, it shows "This channel has no rewards yet."

Each row shows the reward's name, its cost in points, an on and off switch and an **Edit** button.
The line under the name shows the reward's pipeline or its reply.
A viewer buying a reward is called a redeem on the page.

| Line under the name | What the bot does on a redeem |
| --- | --- |
| A pipeline name | Runs that [pipeline](/reference/commands/pipelines). |
| A line of text | Sends that chat reply. |
| **Only lands in the queue** | See the next table. |

**Only lands in the queue** shows for every reward that has no saved pipeline and no reply text.
A command or an action picked under **Pipeline** is not a saved pipeline, so such a reward shows this line too.
What the bot then does depends on how the reward is set.

| How the reward is set | What the bot does on a redeem |
| --- | --- |
| **Pipeline**, with a command or an action picked | Runs that command or action. |
| Never saved with a reply or a pipeline | Sends the channel's redemption line from [Event responses](/reference/automation/event-responses). |
| **Simple reply**, saved with an empty **Chat reply** | Sends nothing. |

A row can also carry a badge.

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not on Twitch** | The reward is saved in the bot, but it does not exist on Twitch. Viewers cannot buy it. |
| **Paused** | The reward shows on Twitch, but viewers cannot buy it now. |

## Edit a reward

1. Choose **Edit** on the reward's row.
   The **Edit** dialog opens with the reward's name in its title: "Changes go to Twitch when you save."
2. Change the fields you want (see the table below).
3. Choose **Save**.
   The dialog closes and the row shows the new values.

If the name is empty, the dialog shows "Give the reward a name." and saves nothing.
If Twitch or the bot refuses the change, the dialog stays open with the reason.
The change goes to Twitch first, so the list never shows a value that Twitch did not take.

| Field | What it sets |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | "The name viewers see in the channel points list." Up to 45 characters. |
| **Cost in points** | "How many channel points one redeem costs." |
| **Description** | "Viewers see this under the reward on Twitch." Up to 200 characters. |
| **Color** | The reward's color on Twitch, as a hex code such as `#9146FF`. An empty box keeps the color it has. |
| **Viewer must type a message** | "The viewer types a message with each redeem." |
| **Enabled** | At the top of the dialog. "Viewers can redeem it only while it is on." |
| **Paused** | At the top of the dialog. "A paused reward stays visible but viewers cannot redeem it." |

### Choose what the reward does

The **Reaction** group in the dialog has two choices.

| Choice | What you fill in |
| --- | --- |
| **Simple reply** | **Chat reply**: "What the bot says in chat when someone redeems it." Up to 500 characters. **Send the reply to** chooses **Twitch chat**, **Chat overlay**, **Both** or **Nowhere**. |
| **Pipeline** | **Pipeline**: the thing to run, which is a saved pipeline, a command or an action. Until you pick one, the dialog shows "This answers nothing until you pick a pipeline. The pipeline has its own reply step." |

A reward does one of the two, never both.
When you save, the choice you did not pick is cleared.

The reply is a line in the channel's one bot voice.
It can use helpers in braces, listed on [Variables and templates](/reference/commands/variables-and-templates).

**Send the reply to** works as it does for a command, on [Create a command](/reference/commands/create-a-command).

### Set limits

The **Limits** group is folded shut each time the dialog opens.
Its header shows the limits that are set, and **Paused** when the reward is paused, for example "5 per stream · 30s cooldown".
With none of these, it shows "No limits".

| Field | What it sets |
| --- | --- |
| **Limit per stream** | "Most redeems of this reward in one stream." |
| **Limit per viewer per stream** | "Most redeems per viewer in one stream." |
| **Cooldown in seconds** | "Seconds to wait between any two redeems." |
| **Timer in seconds** | A countdown that starts when a viewer buys the reward. 0 clears it. |

An empty box keeps the limit the reward has now.

<details>
<summary>A reward another app made</summary>

Twitch lets only the app that made a reward change it.
For such a reward, the dialog shows "Another app made this reward, so Twitch only lets that app change it."
Its fields and its switch in the list are off.

</details>

## Switch a reward on or off

Each row in the list has a switch.
It sets the same thing as **Enabled** in the dialog, and it saves at once.
For a reward that is on Twitch, the switch changes it on Twitch too; a **Not on Twitch** reward changes only in the bot.

**Paused** is a different setting: the reward stays on, and Twitch shows it but lets no viewer buy it.

A reward that is off cannot be bought.
If a viewer still buys it, the bot runs none of its reply, pipeline or timer.

## Copy a reward

1. Choose **Edit** on the reward's row.
2. Choose **Duplicate** at the bottom of the dialog.
   The dialog closes, and a copy named after the reward with " copy" at the end joins the list.

The copy has the saved settings of the reward.
It starts switched off, so viewers cannot buy it until you switch it on.

Twitch does not allow two rewards with the same name.
If a reward with the copy's name exists, the dialog stays open with the reason.

## Delete a reward

1. Choose **Edit** on the reward's row.
2. Choose **Delete** at the bottom of the dialog.
   The dialog asks "Delete" and the reward's name: "The reward is removed here and on Twitch. This cannot be undone."
   Under it, a line names what the reward takes with it, or says "No viewer has redeemed this reward."
3. Choose **Delete reward**.
   The dialog closes and the reward leaves the list.

## Work the queue

The **Pending rewards** panel lists the requests that wait for you, with the count at the top, for example "3 waiting".
On the left, **All requests** and each reward show their own count.
Choose one to see only its requests; the page remembers your choice.

Each request shows the reward, the time since the viewer bought it, the viewer, the cost and the viewer's message.
When nothing waits, the panel shows "No requests are waiting."

| Button | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Complete** (check mark on the row) | The request is marked done and leaves the list. |
| **Reject** (cross on the row) | A dialog asks you to confirm: "Twitch cancels each request and gives the points back." Its **Reject 1** button confirms it, and the request leaves the list. |

To act on many requests at once, tick their boxes, or tick **Select all requests**.
The buttons at the bottom, such as **Complete 3** and **Reject 3**, act on the ticked requests.
With nothing ticked, they act on every request in the open list.

## Troubleshooting

::Troubleshooting

**A viewer buys the reward and the bot does nothing.**
Check these, in order:

1. The switch on the reward's row. A reward that is off runs nothing.
2. The **Reaction** in the **Edit** dialog. A **Pipeline** with nothing picked answers nothing.
3. **Send the reply to**. **Chat overlay** and **Nowhere** send nothing to Twitch chat.

::
